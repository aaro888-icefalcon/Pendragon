# The Lands Around Hindon — a Gazetteer (summer 482)

Sir Abisec's own survey of his march and its neighbours, set down on the road west with Mabli's notes and Senara's briefing. **★ = established in play; ☆ = placed/known by repute, to flesh as visited.** Standing is *to Abisec*.

## The lie of the land
Hindon sits in **southwest Wiltshire**, between the **Wylye** (north) and **Nadder** (south) valleys, where the chalk downs give way west to the wooded **Selwood** border country. It is the **gate between Salisbury and Summerland** — whoever holds the Hindon valley holds the western road. **Mere** is the other gatepost. East lies Salisbury (Roderick, the Crown's order); west, across Selwood, lies **Summerland** (King Cadwy — silver, lead, and the fresh 482 accord). The **syndicate** hunts the soft southwest march; the **coalition** Abisec wants lives among the houses below.

## The strategic picture
- **Abisec's position:** strong centre (Hindon, fortifying; Stapleford; Roderick behind him E), weak/contested **W flank** (Selwood march), a friendly **SE** (Tisbury), a politic **N** (Tilshead).
- **Gwythyr (Mere)** boxes him W; **Summerland** looms beyond; the **syndicate** is loose among the small border houses between.
- **The coalition play:** Tisbury (Nest) + Tilshead (Caradoc) as the solid near-houses, **Meurig** as the march spine, the at-risk Selwood manors as the body — Abisec as protector "for the King's peace." Win the march before the purse buys it.
