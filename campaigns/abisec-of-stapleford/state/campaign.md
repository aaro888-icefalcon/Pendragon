## Situation

Uther reigns, flush from conquering Bedegraine (481). He is suspicious, proud, lustful, dangerously generous; Merlin is present at court. Saxons (beaten at Salisbury 480, not destroyed) and the Irish remain threats on the horizon. The King is quietly testing doubted lords — and one of them, **Baron Idnerth of Hindon ("the Boar")**, has been caught. The central plot, the **Boar Conspiracy**, is now the King's matter, building toward a reckoning at a great feast near **Twelfth Night** (~8 days off). Hired swords are hunting Abisec at court; the lead is **Sir Pedr** (the coin-taker) and the buyer behind him.

**Immediate live beats:** royal audience coming (Uther's clock); Pedr thread to pull; Wed AM hawking with Lady Nest; Christmas tournament in ~2 days (joust + mêlée, Sir Brastias to beat); the Boar reckoning toward Twelfth Night.

**Roderick's standing orders:** Stay close. Be no man's but mine. Build standing. When Idnerth tests you, do nothing — let him move first in the King's own house. Don't ride or drink alone; keep your own at your back to Twelfth Night.
