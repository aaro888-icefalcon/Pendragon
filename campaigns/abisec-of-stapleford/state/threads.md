## The Boar Conspiracy — dossier (central plot, thread #3/#7)

**Origin (480):** A royal courier on Salisbury Plain was murdered and a sealed dispatch stolen — by trained, British-tongued men. Abisec ran them down, recovered the dispatch, took prisoners. The dispatch proved a **secret royal muster-order *and* a snare**: Uther binding trusted lords while secretly **testing two doubted lords**, one bearing a **Boar** badge. An informant inside Roderick's house had warned the Boar-lord early.

**The chain, now known:**
1. **Father Maucan** — Vagon's chaplain, the informant; paid in boar-stamped coin; serviced a dead-drop at the old mill. **Captured alive (481), broken, will talk.**
2. **Sir Cadeyrn** — the gently-born handler/grey-rider (fine dappled grey, silvered harness), Idnerth's hand and knife. **Captured alive (481), defiant.**
3. **Baron Idnerth of Hindon — "the Boar"** — Logres baron, Roderick's western neighbour; **one of the two lords Uther secretly doubts.** Warned early via the Vagon leak; has had time to prepare and cover.

**Evidence in hand (witnessed by crown officer Brother Syrion, Honest 18):** the boar-coin; a boar-sealed letter (Idnerth running spies into Sarum/Vagon); a paymaster's ledger of bought men across multiple places; Maucan's testimony; Abisec's flat account. **Verdict:** enough to *expose* Idnerth — not enough to hang a great lord outright. **It is now the King's matter.**

**State of play (Christmas Court 481):** Idnerth came to brazen it out — feasting at Uther's board, gift-giving (matched Spanish coursers), gathering lords, building a wall of the King's favor — but it's a **soft** bloc. Uther is letting him dangle; the blow likely falls at a great feast toward **Twelfth Night**. Idnerth is **hiring swords** to silence the case / kill Abisec; a hunt probe was foiled (3 badgeless men, faces known).

**Open leads:** **Sir Pedr** (landless Silchester knight, likely took the assassins' coin — trace to who paid him); **Bledri** the wool-factor (letter-conduit; Roderick to pull him quietly); the wider spy-network named in the ledger.

**Roderick's counter-plan:** a hard rider to Sarum to double the watch on the prisoners; his quiet men tracing the buying here; Abisec to guard himself and pull the Pedr thread carefully — "bring me a name with a boar behind it."
