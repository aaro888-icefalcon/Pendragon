**Tenure:** Crown grant, spring 482 — Knight Banneret holding the caput *for the King*; his till the line fails, forfeit **twice as fast** if the vow is broken. The **outliers (~£95, 8 parcels) are withheld** — escheat, the Crown's to keep/break/grant.

| **Total CR** | **~55** |

**Expenses** (proportional, per the rules):

| Category | % | £ |
|---|---|---|

| Court (admin & hospitality) | 25 | ~14 |

| Discretionary | 10 | ~5.5 |

**Military obligation (servitium debitum):** ~5–6 knights + ~16 foot (per £10 CR = 1 knight + 2 field + 1 garrison foot). **The King may reduce by grace** given the sworn vow. Practical garrison (the "bolt"): ~6 knights + a dozen spears.

**Fortification:** shell-keep castle, **DV 9 / 13 / 2** (keep / bailey-wall / gate). The wool market-town (~10 acres, ~3,000 ft, wants ~30 to hold) currently **OPEN/unwalled**.

**Furnishings:** a **Magnificent hall** — a one-time display value (~£160), **NOT income**; the treason-silver's visible legacy. It sets a hospitality standard the £55 income strains to keep — **margins run lean** (discretionary ~£5.5/yr).

**The "bolt" — UNDERWAY (commissioned spring 482; master mason of the wool-town guild; £37 spent):**
An integrated scheme — keep and town one body, joined by a drawbridge, each able to seal the other out; the **mill-stream dammed** to fill the ditches.

| Work | £ | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Town enclosure (stone wall round the wool-town) | 14 | town DV 5 |
| Complex gateworks (fortified gatehouse) | 4 | +2 gate DV |
| Town ditch & rampart (~3,000 ft) | 9 | DV 3 |
| Town wet moat (dammed mill-stream) | 2 | +4 ditch |

**Timeline:** defensive core (moats + gatehouse + cistern) by raiding season; full wall + drawbridge by autumn 482. When complete: town **DV 5+** behind wall & moat, gate +2, keep moated atop **9/13/2**.
**Tower cadence:** wooden towers (£15 each) to be added **one per season** — no overreach, no debt.
Stout vs. raiders or a rebel; useless vs. a king's host — **loyal initiative, not a power-base** (the pitch that won the King's floor).

**Household (Abisec's own people, not strangers):** steward **Mabli** · seneschal **Bledig** (retained) · household leech **Sioned** (the hall's infirmary — Hindon's emerging *healing* character) · household knights **Alun**, cousin **Madog**, **Elad** (+ **Gerin & Bann** returning from the London escort) · squire **Achil** · sergeants/garrison. Constabulary herd scaled to the caput (oxen, dairy, the wool-flocks, swine, poultry).

**Annual land Glory:** ~£55/yr (= CR; cap 100) while held.

**Mabli's findings (482):** the **wool-staple is the engine**; keep Bledig (clean hands, knows every parcel and penny). Margins are thin behind a too-rich hall. The old rolls still show the severed **outliers' ~£95** — a map of what reuniting Hindon would mean, and what even a single parcel handed to **Gwythyr** would bleed from the gate.
