## Rulings (precedent — keep consistent forever)
1. Campaign opens 480 (not chargen's 485); knighting on-screen at Easter 480.
2. Pagan starting traits used as 13-value guides; final spread set by character translation.
3. Manor d20 → 12 = Stapleford.
4. A squire's "one other skill" may be non-knightly (Achil: Chirurgery 5).
5. Starting Glory 6d6+150 (=177) as family/squirehood glory; +1,000 knighthood Glory at the ceremony.
6. Pre-battle speech = Orate check (+1 / 0 / −1 to the commander's first Battle roll).
7. Knockdown triggers on damage rolled ≥ SIZ (before armor).
8. Knockdown-check fumble: fall + lose grip on weapon.
9. Squire Recovery: Love (family) may substitute for Loyalty (Squire) when the squire is kin.
10. Scripted battle outcomes (year briefs) govern victory type; the battle system governs the player's unit experience within them.
11. Skirmishes run per the combat rules (commander's Battle → first-round tactics modifier; Followers' Fate decides unit losses).
12. An immediate re-charge negates the prior round's failed-tactics penalty if the new Battle roll succeeds.
13. Killing a fleeing/disarmed enemy is a trait moment (Merciful vs. the relevant passion).
14. NPC subordinate fates in a failed charge are rolled individually.
15. Interrogation = Intrigue for the lean + a disposition/reliability roll per prisoner.
16. A dying NPC may give true info on a failed Intrigue if his disposition rolls to unburden — fragments, not a clean confession.
17. Recovered sealed correspondence: an Honest/Just PC need not open it; contents revealed via his lord.
18. Deferring a "dangerous secret" choice to the lord's judgment is auto-success for a Loyal/Just/Prudent PC.
19. Estate income floor: a knight's minimum manor is modeled at £10 gross.
20. Equivocation of literally true words triggers no Honest check; whether an evasion satisfies a hostile listener is an opposed social roll (evader's Courtesy/Deceitful vs. listener's Intrigue).
21. A minimum manor holds the advowson of its own small chapel.
22. Damage uses KAP 5e round-to-nearest (0.5 up), not "round down" (so SIZ+STR 21 = 4d6). Other derived stats round down.
23. Skill personalization uses the canonical KAP 5e process (replacing the file's simplified version). Traits remain free-assigned per Ruling 2.
