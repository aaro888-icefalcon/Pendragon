# Setting Canon — Arthurian Britain / Logres

> Ground truth the `mythic-gm` engine consults before inventing. Lean by design; depth lives in this skill's `campaign/`. Canon overrides recollection. When canon is silent on a world fact, decide it with a Fate Question and record the answer back here.

## Frame
- **Era:** sub-Roman Britain, 480 AD onward — the Great Pendragon Campaign, through 566. Default start: **Salisbury, 480, under King Uther Pendragon**.
- **The player is a knight** of Salisbury (vassal of **Count Roderick**), and later his dynasty. Chivalric tragedy: glory, loyalty, love, and the long slide toward the fall.

## Places
- **Salisbury** — the home county; seat at **Sarum** (the Castle of the Rock). Stapleford and other manors lie on the Plain and its rivers.
- **Logres** — Uther's (later Arthur's) realm of southern Britain; **Londinium** holds the royal court (the White Tower).
- **Borders & enemies' lands:** Saxon shores to the east (Anglia, Kent under Aesc/Octa); Cambria/Wales and Cornwall to the west; Pictland and the Irish beyond. The **Perilous Forest** and faerie places thread the map. Camelot and the Round Table come with Arthur.

## Factions
- **The Cymric Britons** under the High King (Uther, then the interregnum, then Arthur) — feudal, Christian-and-pagan both.
- **The Saxons** — encroaching settlers and war-bands; the long external threat.
- **The Church vs. the old faith** — a Christianizing court over a still-pagan countryside; a live social fault line.
- **Picts and Irish** — raiders from north and west. **Faerie / the Otherworld** — a real, dangerous power, not metaphor.

## Timeline anchors (canonical; fire on schedule — `campaign/`)
- **480–495 Uther** rules; wars with Saxons, Picts, Irish (see `campaign/uther/`).
- **~495:** the **Infamous Feast** / Uther's death; the realm leaderless.
- **496–509:** the **Anarchy** — no high king; lords war; the sword in the stone waits.
- **510:** **Arthur** draws the sword and is crowned; the wars of consolidation follow.
- **519–530 Conquest; 531–539 Romance; 540–552 Tournament; 554–557 Grail Quest; 558–566 Twilight** and the fall (Camlann). Period briefs in `campaign/gpc/`.

## Named NPCs (canonical fates hold unless the player plausibly alters them)

Full stats: `campaign/uther/03-npcs-royal.md`, `campaign/uther/04-npcs-other.md`, `campaign/gpc/09-gpc-appendices.md`.

## Tone & hard lines
- **Tone:** honest danger and chivalric tragedy. Knights die; the saga continues through the dynasty. Glory matters because death is real (`gm/LETHALITY.md`). Prose is spare and concrete (`gm/STYLE.md`).
- **Player-established lore is fixed** (a named lord, a described manor, a sworn feud is canon). Oracles fill gaps; they never overwrite canon.
- **Hard content lines:** keep on-camera cruelty period-appropriate and non-gratuitous; the table sets any further limits at session start.
