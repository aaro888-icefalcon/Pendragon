# Pacing: Forward Motion

AI GMs stall — endless atmospheric scenes, NPC small talk, nothing resolving. Pendragon has a built-in cure: the year structure. Use it ruthlessly.

## 1. The year is the metronome

- Default target: **complete at least one full game year per session**, including Winter Phase. A major adventure or battle may legitimately span longer — a third shopping scene may not.
- The campaign timeline (`campaign/`) fires on schedule. Canonical events happen whether or not the player engages — every year brief has an "If the player does nothing" line. Use it. The world is not waiting for the knight.
- Time skips are a tool: if a year holds nothing for this knight, run court + Winter Phase in a few exchanges and move on. Uneventful years make eventful ones land.

## 2. Scene economy

- **Open late, leave early.** Start the scene at the interesting moment; end it when the outcome is known. No arrival narration, no goodbyes.
- **Every scene must change at least one of:** information, position, resources, relationships, or the clock. If a proposed scene can't, replace it with one summary sentence.
- **Cut connective tissue.** Travel, provisioning, routine feasts: one sentence each unless an event (rolled or scheduled) interrupts.
- **Hard-frame on clear intent.** "I ride to Sarum and petition the Earl" → next beat is the Earl's hall, not the road.

## 3. The stall-breaker

If two consecutive exchanges produce no forward motion (player circling, conversation looping, no decision emerging), act in this order:

1. **Fire the nearest thread** from `state/threads.md` — the rival arrives, the summons lands, the debt is called.
2. **Fire the year's next scheduled event** from the year brief, early.
3. **Roll an oracle**: `generators/07-adventure-seeds.md` or `generators/08-complications-twists.md`, honestly, and bring the result on stage now.

Never break a stall with atmosphere. Break it with an event that demands a response.

## 4. Threads

`state/threads.md` is the campaign's pressure system. Discipline:

- Any unresolved hook, promise, enemy, or debt becomes a thread when its scene ends.
- Each thread has a **pressure line**: what happens next if the player keeps ignoring it. At every Winter Phase, advance every ignored thread one step — rivals gain Glory too; grudges fester; opportunities expire and are taken by NPC knights instead.
- Threads resolve, escalate, or expire. They never sit static for more than two game years.

## 5. Endings

- End every session at a Winter Phase or a genuine cliffhanger — never mid-haggle.
- Last act of any session: update `state/` files (the engine's bookkeeping — see `/CLAUDE.md`), so the next session boots clean.
