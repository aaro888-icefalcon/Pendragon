# Religions and Cults

**Scope:** The British Church, pagan survivals, mystery cults, faerie-touched faiths, Roman religious survivals, and dark sinister cults in Arthurian Britain.

**Use when:** Generating an unfamiliar shrine, a new cult the players have stumbled upon, a heretical sect within the Church, or a pagan tradition that still holds power in the countryside.

**Procedure:** (1) Determine scope (local/regional/kingdom-wide). (2) Roll or choose the god's origin and social function. (3) Roll on the Portfolio table to get a Human Concern and Natural Principle. (4) Roll for What the Faith Wants and its Strictures. (5) Use the One-Roll Temple table for quick improvisation. For sinister cults, use the Malevolent Cult generator.

*Source: WWN Religion Construction chapter, reskinned for Arthurian Britain. Specific Latter-Earth references replaced with Arthurian equivalents.*

## Scope of the Faith

Decide where the faith is worshipped:

| Scope | Description | Arthurian examples |
|-------|------------|-------------------|
| **Local** | Known only in a village, valley, or estate | A holy well saint; a barrow-god; a household spirit venerated by one family |
| **Regional** | Significant in a province or kingdom | A pagan tradition among the hill-folk; a monastic house with regional renown |
| **Kingdom-wide** | The state faith or a major competing religion | The British Church; a Saxon pagan pantheon; a widespread heresy |
| **Dead** | No longer worshipped; relevant only as a ruin's history | A Roman mystery cult; an extinct druidic tradition |
