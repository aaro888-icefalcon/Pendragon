# NPCs

**Scope:** One-roll NPC generation for knights, lords, priests, criminals, merchants, villagers, and warriors. Includes characterization tables for appearances, mannerisms, ambitions, tragedies, friendships, and romances.

**Use when:** You need an NPC the players have just encountered who was not pre-planned; when you need to deepen an existing NPC quickly; when generating the supporting cast of a court or community.

**Procedure:** (1) Choose the social class table appropriate to where the NPC is encountered. (2) Roll d100 for NPC type. (3) Roll d12 for a Characteristic Twist. (4) If deeper characterization is needed, roll on the Appearances, Ambitions, or Tragedy tables.

*Source: WWN Random NPC Types and One-Roll Characterization Tables chapters, reskinned for Arthurian Britain.*

### Characteristic Twists (d12)

Roll once to give any NPC an unexpected dimension.

| d12 | Twist |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | Abnormally rich or poor for their apparent station |
| 2 | Extremely averse to using violence under any circumstances |
| 3 | Tends to break their agreements, wisely or not |
| 4 | Has a reason to distrust or dislike people of the players' class or background |
| 5 | Has a useful but unlikely friend — a hermit, a faerie, a famous knight |
| 6 | A player character or their lord owes this NPC a debt |
| 7 | A personal disaster is looming rapidly for them |
| 8 | Has a reason to like or trust people of the players' class or background |
| 9 | Has few reliable social ties in this place |
| 10 | Conceals a secret that would be socially ruinous if revealed |
| 11 | A former outlaw, slave, or fallen person now trying to live honestly |
| 12 | Has a second identity they maintain carefully |
