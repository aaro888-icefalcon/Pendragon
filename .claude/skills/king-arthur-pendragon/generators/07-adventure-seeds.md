# Adventure Seeds

**Scope:** One hundred fractal adventure premises for a Pendragon campaign, organized by the kind of element they pivot on — Enemy, Friend, Complication, Thing, or Place.

**Use when:** You need a quick adventure premise, or when you want to complicate an existing adventure by adding a new wrinkle around one of its elements.

**Procedure:** (1) Generate a handful of Enemies, Friends, Complications, Things, and Places from any other oracle file (tags, courts, ruins, etc.) or from the session's existing situation. (2) Roll d100 for an initial seed. (3) Plug the seed's slots with your generated elements. (4) Optionally, pick one element from that seed and roll d20 on the matching sub-table to fractal it further — plug the result into the first seed. Repeat as deeply as you wish.

*Source: WWN Fractal Adventure Seeds chapter, lines 20780–21000. The seeds are genre-neutral and need minimal reskinning. "Working" → Roman engineering or faerie relic. "Deep" → mine-complex or barrow. "Blighted" → cursed folk or beast-kin.*

---

## How to Use Seeds

The abbreviations in each seed are:

- **E** = Enemy (antagonist or troublemaker)
- **F** = Friend (sympathetic NPC or patron)
- **C** = Complication (the messy twist or obstacle)
- **T** = Thing (the macguffin, prize, or key object)
- **P** = Place (the notable location)

Each seed is a sentence or two that tells you the shape of the situation. Plug in your actual entities and you have an adventure premise.

## Enemy Seeds (d20, or 1–20 on d100)

The Enemy is usually the main villain or antagonist of the situation. Further Enemies may be henchmen or a second antagonistic group.

| # | Seed |
|---|------|
| 1 | An **E** has suffered a dire reverse, and seeks a **T** in order to rescue their situation. |
| 2 | An **E** is behind a recent flare-up of a **C** that is causing problems for the players. |
| 3 | An **E** keeps a **T** at a well-guarded **P**, and a **F** is willing to pass the players information on it. |
| 4 | An **E** is being targeted by the negative effects of a **C** but is trying to pin it all on a **F**. |
| 5 | An **E** is trying to take control of a **P**, but is being hindered by the activities of a **F**. |
| 6 | An **E** is trying to use a **C** to pry a valuable **T** out of the hands of a **F**. |
| 7 | An **E** is using a **T** to bait a **F** into getting involved in a **C** that will probably destroy them. |
| 8 | An **E** controls a **P** where a **T** is located, but a **C** is currently keeping them from being able to extract it. |
| 9 | An **E** has recently become active near a **P**, and their actions have stirred up a **C** that a local **F** does not know how to handle. |
| 10 | An **E** is struggling over a **T** with another **E**, and the fallout from their fight is making problems for the players or a **F**. |
| 11 | An **E** has a grudge against a **F** and is trying to frame them for some crime related to a **T**. |
| 12 | An **E** has just destroyed the last obstacle that prevented them from full exertion of their malice and is using a **T** they claimed to prosecute it. |
| 13 | An **E** lost a **T** at the worst possible time and is suffering from a **C**. They are trying to get it back before they are ruined. |
| 14 | An **E** is currently at a **P** and is exceptionally vulnerable for some reason. A **F** wants to take advantage of that. |
| 15 | An **E** has promised an ally a **T** and is becoming increasingly desperate to obtain it, as the consequences for failure are dire. |
| 16 | An **E** has imprisoned a **F** for some reason, and if they are not rescued soon a grim fate awaits them. |
| 17 | An **E** has allied with another **E** in order to obtain a **T** from a **P**, yet both are preparing to betray each other and the **T** could be stolen in the chaos. |
| 18 | An **E** is working to weaponise a **C** against a **F**, but their attempt has somehow left open the possibility of stealing or finding a **T**. |
| 19 | An **E** has forced a **F** into service, but in the process the **F** has learned how the players can obtain a **T**. |
| 20 | An **E** has just had some personal triumph that is catalysing a flare-up of a **C** that is harming the players or a **F**. |

## Friend Seeds (d20, or 21–40 on d100)

Some Friends generated by tags are relatively weak NPCs meant to pull sympathetic heartstrings. A Friend seed depicting them as significant rivals to the Enemy can be read as the Friend's community or social group acting in aggregate.

| # | Seed |
|---|------|
| 21 | A **F** stole a **T** from an **E**. The **E** does not know who did it, but their flailing retaliation threatens something precious to the **F** as they search for the culprit and the **T**. |
| 22 | A **F** carelessly fell into a peril related to a **C**, leaving them vulnerable to a threat from an **E**. |
| 23 | A **F** needs to be rescued from the hostile attentions of an **E**, and they are currently hiding out at a **P** dangerously close to enemy forces. |
| 24 | A **F** has acquired a **T**, but it is also being sought by an **E** and they need help to get it safely away and out of their foe's reach. |
| 25 | A **F** got lost or snared in a **P**, and a rising **C** threatens to kill or ruin them if they are not rescued in time. |
| 26 | A **F** needs help to get a **T** from an abandoned **P**, but an **E** is ahead of them and prepared to deal harshly with competitors. |
| 27 | A **F** is not really a Friend, but an enemy agent, and is trying to lure the players into helping them acquire a **T** that will actually be turned over to their master. |
| 28 | A **F** is caught up in a **C** and cannot get to a **P** where they know a **T** is being kept. The players need to act as their agent or get them free of the **C**. |
| 29 | A **F** has been charged with guarding or maintaining a **T**, but an **E** has set in motion a **C** that threatens both of them. |
| 30 | A **F** is being compelled by an **E**'s threats to sacrifice something important to them or endure some grave loss. |
| 31 | A **F** desperately needs a **T** for their own purposes, but an **E** stands between them and it — though the **E** does not realise what is there. |
| 32 | A **F** has obtained ownership or responsibility for a **P**, but an **E** has set up a trap or disaster there meant to ruin or disgrace them. |
| 33 | A **F** has been gravely harmed by an **E** before and fears to confront them, but a **C** looks like it is going to force them into conflict once more. |
| 34 | A **F** has acquired a **T** and is making use of it to further their desires, but an **E** means to spoil their efforts and rob them of the **T**. |
| 35 | A **F** has a sure lead to a **T** at a **P**, but in actuality the **T** is not there at all — only an **E** or a **C**-related peril. |
| 36 | A **F** has been snatched away by an **E**, who is convinced they are the key to acquiring a **T**. They may or may not be right about that. |
| 37 | A **F** has suffered a grave personal loss due to a **C**, but an **E** offers to make good their suffering if they can bring them a **T** they are supposed to know how to get. |
| 38 | A **F** is sick or dying, and desperately needs a **T** if they are to live. Getting it would somehow directly put them in conflict with an **E**, however. |
| 39 | A **F** had a **T** but they hid it at a **P**. Unfortunately, that **P** is now under the control of an **E** who does not know what is hidden there. |
| 40 | A **F** is trying to protect someone from the consequences of a **C**, but their efforts are being thwarted by an **E** who profits by the current situation. |

## Complication Seeds (d20, or 41–60 on d100)

Complications in these seeds tend to be active troublesome forces — things that cause problems for people and make situations dangerous. If the Complication you are working with is passive, use it as the explanation for some more active conflict.

| # | Seed |
|---|------|
| 41 | A **C** threatens a **F**, who offers a way to get at a **T** to the players if they can help the **F** escape trouble. |
| 42 | A **C**'s flare-up is the fault of an **E**, who stirred it up in the process of seeking a **T**. |
| 43 | A **C** is showing a double edge, harming both an **E** and the players in some way and making the former vulnerable. |
| 44 | A **C** threatens to ruin a **F** unless a particular **T** can be found and used to save them. |
| 45 | A **C**'s activity opens the path to a lost **T** at some dangerous **P**. |
| 46 | A **C** is only getting worse until an **E** is defeated and forced to stop their provocations. |
| 47 | A **C** is causing problems for the players, but a **F** can help resolve things if they can be found at a dangerous **P**. |
| 48 | A **C** has rendered an **E** vulnerable to the players if they can get their hands on a **T**. |
| 49 | A **C** is centred at a **P** that a **F** needs to get to for some reason. |
| 50 | A **C** is causing problems for a **F** that an **E** is taking advantage of. |
| 51 | A **C** elsewhere is having knock-on effects that are reaching the players, but a **F** can help resolve it if they can hold back an **E** that is threatening them. |
| 52 | A **C** is being provoked by a **F** who does not want to do it, but is forced to do so by circumstances until an **E**'s related machinations can be stopped. |
| 53 | A **C** just calmed down, with the lull exposing a **T** and making it accessible at an otherwise-dangerous **P**. At some random near future time, however, the **C** will pick back up with a vengeance. |
| 54 | A **C** is empowering an **E**'s plans, allowing them far more influence than they would normally have. A **T** or **F** can stop this, if the players can lay hands on it or find the NPC. |
| 55 | A **C** struck amid a struggle between an **E** and a **F**, just before the **F**'s loss. If the **E** is not defeated before the **C** dies down again, they will win for certain. |
| 56 | A **C** is forcing a **F** and an **E** to work together, but it is inevitable that there will be conflict the moment it ends, and the **F** is seeking help. |
| 57 | A **C** drove a **F** out of a **P** before they could recover a **T** from there. Even though the **C** is still blocking the site, they need the **T** as quickly as possible. |
| 58 | A **C** has robbed an **E** of the current use of their best tool or most effective underling, and a **F** has a plan to make that loss permanent. |
| 59 | A **C** has damaged or involved a **T**, **P**, or institution that no one ever thought would become embroiled. A **F** associated with that target is in need of aid. |
| 60 | A **C** is delicately poised between an **E** and a **F**, targeting neither right now, but both are working to manipulate it to hit the other or escape its eventual explosion. |

## Thing Seeds (d20, or 61–80 on d100)

Things are macguffins that should hold the interest of the players and NPCs alike. Some Things are valuable in themselves; others are valuable only to specific NPCs. When using these seeds, be ready to tweak Things to fit the kind of need or use the seed implies.

| # | Seed |
|---|------|
| 61 | A **T** has been lost in a dangerous **P** by a **F**, and now an **E** hastens to retrieve it. A **C** is making it hard for them to get it, but they will win through soon. |
| 62 | A **T** has turned out to be very dangerous to possess, and an **E** is trying to bait the players or a **F** into taking or stealing it. |
| 63 | A **T** is the crux of a savage conflict between two or more Enemies, and their fighting is stirring up a **C**. |
| 64 | A **T** must be found if some calamity is to be avoided by the players or a **F**, but an **E** stands to profit if it is never obtained. |
| 65 | A **T** is located in a dangerous **P**, and a **F** knows how to get it. |
| 66 | A **T** is supposed to be located in a **P**, but those who go there merely get caught in a **C**, though they do find out the real **P** the **T** has been moved to. |
| 67 | A **T** seems to be useful for a particular function or value, but in actuality it has a hidden value known to a **F** who is currently hiding from an **E**. The players know there is more to it, but only the **F** can tell the full truth. |
| 68 | A **T** has been lost by a **F** and they are desperate to get it back before it is found by an **E**. |
| 69 | A **T** is hidden in a **P** controlled by an **E**, but the **E** is oblivious to it. |
| 70 | A **T** has been stolen from an **E** by a **F** and hidden in a **P**, but the **E** is searching around there and it is too dangerous for the **F** to try to retrieve it. |
| 71 | A **T** is instrumental to an **E**'s plan, but the **F** who knows where to get it is in hiding. |
| 72 | A **T** has been demanded of the **E** by someone they fear, and their plans for getting it involve steps that will bring grave harm to a **F**. |
| 73 | A **T** holds the key to solving or being protected from a **C**, but the **P** it is at is currently very dangerous due to that **C**. |
| 74 | A **T** is responsible for a flare-up of a **C** in a **P**, and the players are somehow affected by it. |
| 75 | A **T** that a **F** is responsible for obtaining or guarding will be lost to an **E** as soon as a **C** stops hindering them, unless the players can spirit it away from its current dangerous **P**. |
| 76 | A **T** was being kept at a **P** by an **E**, but a **F** has figured out a way to get past their defences. |
| 77 | A **T** was lost in a dangerous **P** long ago, but an **E** unwittingly holds the key to finding or unlocking it. |
| 78 | A **T** hidden in a **P** is needed by two different Friends for different important reasons, but if an **E** is overcome, one or both of those reasons will cease to trouble them. |
| 79 | A **T** has just been obtained by a **F**, but an **E** now pursues them for it, and the players are asked to help drive off the **E** or get the **F** someplace safe with the **T**. |
| 80 | A **T** was the lawful inheritance of a **F**, but an **E** has seized it and a **C** prevents the **F** from obtaining it through conventional means. |

## Place Seeds (d20, or 81–100 on d100)

Places often need the most tweaking, since the exploration or experience of them will be a significant part of the evening's fun. A safe-sounding Place can be made dangerous by translating its metaphorical meaning into something physical — a tag's "grimy tavern" becomes a secret meeting-lair beneath the city sewers.

| # | Seed |
|---|------|
| 81 | A **P** has been overwhelmed by some danger related to a **C**, and a **F** is now trapped there. |
| 82 | A **P** has changed, becoming much more dangerous due to the influence of an **E**. This is causing problems for a **F**. |
| 83 | A **P** has been overrun by an **E** or their agents in an attempt to find a **T** that is supposedly located there. |
| 84 | A **P** is currently the focal point of a **C** that is threatening a **F**. |
| 85 | A **P** is being reclaimed by a **F** from its current difficulties, but an **E** has reasons to oppose that change and is using a **C** to interfere. |
| 86 | A **P** is the destination to which a **F** must take a **T**. An **E** means to waylay them, however. |
| 87 | A **P** is the source of an **E**'s power, and they guard it jealously. Despite this, a **F** knows of a **T** located there. |
| 88 | A **P** offers refuge from a **C** that is currently making trouble in the area, but an **E** is preventing others from making use of it unless bribed otherwise. |
| 89 | A **P** is falling apart, but the chaos has exposed a chance to get a **T** that is being pursued by an **E** and others. |
| 90 | A **P** is under siege by an **E**, who is determined to take it before a rival can claim it. A **F** would face disaster if either side claimed it. |
| 91 | A **P** is currently entrapping an **E**, who is powerless to escape. They have taken a **T** from a **F**, however, and will release it only when the **F** releases them. |
| 92 | A **P** is the inherited property or responsibility of a **F**, but they lack a **T** which is critical to their successful obtaining or maintenance of the place. |
| 93 | A **P** conceals its true nature as a very different **P**, and a **F** has been misfortunately taken in by it. |
| 94 | A **P** has a **T** as an integral part of it, and when an **E** took it, a resultant **C** started that will not end until it is returned. |
| 95 | A **P** is used by an **E** to create a **T**, the processes and events going on there utilised to produce the desired result. |
| 96 | A **P** is distant and the journey there is dangerous, but a **F** offers a **T** if the players can get them there safely. |
| 97 | A **P** is the seat of an **E**'s power, where they are both most fearsome and least wary. A **F** has a plan to take advantage of that. |
| 98 | A **P** is the target of a **C**, and the consequences might ruin it. A **T** is located there, and if it is not grabbed first the **C** will probably wreck or take it. |
| 99 | A **P** has been newly-established and is still fragile. A **C** threatens to destroy it. |
| 100 | A **P** is a refuge for a **F**, but an **E** means to bait them out of it or force them to leave with a **C**. |

## Worked Examples for Arthurian Britain

### Example 1: Enemy Seed 11 + Court Tags (Scheming Court)
Elements: **E** = Bishop's chaplain who is secretly a Saxon agent; **F** = Young knight who discovered the truth; **T** = The chaplain's cipher-letters to his Saxon handlers; **C** = The letters are in the chaplain's locked scriptorium inside a cathedral; **P** = The cathedral treasury.

**Premise:** The Bishop's chaplain (**E**) has a grudge against Sir Aldred (**F**) and is trying to frame him for the theft of the cathedral plate, using the distraction to destroy the cipher-letters (**T**) before anyone finds them.

### Example 2: Complication Seed 53 + Ruin Tags (Dire Tombs / Lingering Curse)
Elements: **E** = A Saxon warlord who knows about the barrow-hoard; **F** = The local cunning woman who acts as guardian of the barrow; **T** = The iron war-crown of a dead British king; **C** = The barrow is haunted by its dead king's revenant, who has been quiet for a generation — but the quiet is ending; **P** = Barrow of the Seven Kings.

**Premise:** The haunting (**C**) just calmed down after a generation of quiet, and the lull has exposed a path into the inner chamber where the king's war-crown (**T**) is kept. The Saxon warlord (**E**) has heard the old stories and is moving to claim it. The cunning woman (**F**) knows the haunting will return within three days.

### Example 3: Place Seed 91 + Faction Turn (Church vs. Pagan Cult)
Elements: **E** = A river-goddess cult that captured the village reeve; **F** = The village; **T** = The church's holy well (which is also the cult's ancestral shrine); **C** = The cult cannot leave the river valley without losing their goddess's protection; **P** = The hidden cave-shrine beneath the ford.

**Premise:** The cave-shrine (**P**) is currently entrapping the cult (**E**), who cannot survive if they leave the valley. But the cult has seized the village reeve (**T**) from the villagers (**F**) and will release him only when the Church agrees to share the holy well site.
