# Factions

**Scope:** Saxon kingdoms, British noble houses, the Church of Britain, pagan cults, mercenary companies, merchant guilds, bandit confederacies, faerie courts, and any other off-screen organization that exerts political force on the campaign.

**Use when:** You want to track a group's off-screen activities between sessions, determine what a faction does in response to player actions, or build a new political actor from scratch.

**Procedure:** (1) Determine faction size (Small/Medium/Large). (2) Assign Force/Wealth/Cunning attributes. (3) Assign a Magic rating. (4) Choose or roll a Faction Tag. (5) Assign a starting Goal. (6) Run a Faction Turn after every session or as needed.

*Source: WWN Factions and Major Projects chapter, adapted for Arthurian Britain. Asset names reskinned where genre-blocking.*

---

## Faction Attributes

Every faction has four attributes:

- **Force (1–8):** Military prowess, martial power, the ability to use violence effectively.
- **Wealth (1–8):** Material resources, income, ability to use money as a tool.
- **Cunning (1–8):** Guile, subterfuge, skill at intrigue and manipulation.
- **Magic (None/Low/Medium/High):** Access to enchantment, holy power, faerie aid, or learned sorcery.

### Arthurian Faction Examples by Size

| Size | Force / Wealth / Cunning | Arthurian Examples |
|------|-------------------------|--------------------|
| Small | 3–4 / 2–3 / 1–2 | A single knight's retinue; a village bandit band; a hermit brotherhood; a hedge-witch network |
| Medium | 5–6 / 4–5 / 2–3 | A baron's household; a cathedral chapter; a regional merchant guild; a Saxon war-leader |
| Large | 7–8 / 6–7 / 3–4 | A kingdom; a great abbey; a major Saxon confederation; King Arthur's court itself |

## Faction Hit Points

Maximum faction hit points = sum of all attribute hit point values from the table below.

| Attribute Rating | HP Value |
|-----------------|----------|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 9 |
| 6 | 12 |
| 7 | 16 |
| 8 | 20 |

A faction reduced to 0 HP collapses. Its members and sub-groups may survive but the faction ceases to function as a coherent whole.

## Faction Tags

Each faction may have one or more tags reflecting its special nature.

| Tag | Effect |
|-----|--------|
| **Concealed** | All Assets the faction purchases begin with the Stealth quality |
| **Imperialist** | Once per turn, Expand Influence is a free action rather than a full action |
| **Innovative** | May purchase Assets as if attributes were 2 points higher; max 2 such Assets at once |
| **Machiavellian** | Rolls an extra die for all Cunning checks; Cunning must always be its highest attribute |
| **Martial** | Rolls an extra die for all Force checks; Force must always be its highest attribute |
| **Massive** | Automatically wins attribute checks if its attribute is more than twice the opponent's (unless opponent is also Massive) |
| **Mobile** | Faction movement range is twice normal |
| **Populist** | Assets costing 5 Treasure or less cost 1 point less, minimum 1 |
| **Rich** | Rolls an extra die for all Wealth checks; Wealth must always be its highest attribute |
| **Rooted** | Extra die for checks in headquarters location; rivals roll checks there twice and take the worst |
| **Tenacious** | When a Base of Influence is reduced to 0 HP, it survives with 1 HP instead (once per base per incident) |
| **Zealot** | Once per turn, when an Asset fails an Attack check, it may reroll — but automatically takes counterattack damage |
| **Holy** | (Arthurian addition) When defending against occult Cunning attacks, rolls an extra die |
| **Pagan** | (Arthurian addition) All Assets in wilderness or liminal locations gain +1 HP |

## The Faction Turn

Run a Faction Turn after each session, or monthly in campaign time.

**Step 1: Initiative.** Each faction rolls 1d8; highest goes first.

**Step 2: Earn Treasure.** Each faction earns Treasure = half their Wealth + one quarter of combined Force and Cunning, rounded up.

**Step 3: Pay Upkeep.** Pay any Asset upkeep costs. If a faction cannot pay, Assets may be lost or have negative consequences.

**Step 4: Trigger Special Abilities.** Resolve any Asset abilities that trigger automatically.

**Step 5: Take One Faction Action.** Every Asset owned by the faction may participate in the chosen action.

**Step 6: Check Goals.** Has the faction accomplished its current goal? If so, award XP and pick a new goal.

---

## Faction Actions

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Attack** | Nominate Assets to attack enemy Assets in their location. Attacker and defender each roll 1d10 + relevant attribute. Attacker wins on a higher result; ties go to the defender. Success deals the attacking Asset's damage; failure deals the defending Asset's counterattack damage. |
| **Move Asset** | Move Assets up to one turn's movement (approximately 100 miles for a monthly turn). |
| **Repair Asset** | Spend 1 Treasure per Asset to restore half the faction's relevant attribute in lost HP, rounded up. Each additional repair on the same Asset in the same turn costs 1 more Treasure. |
| **Expand Influence** | Establish a new Base of Influence. Faction must already have an Asset there; spend 1 Treasure per HP the new Base is to have. Then make a Cunning vs. Cunning check against each rival faction with Assets there — a winning rival may immediately Attack the new Base. |
| **Create Asset** | Buy one Asset at a location where the faction has a Base of Influence. Requires the minimum attribute and Magic rating and costs the listed Treasure. One Asset per turn. |
| **Hide Asset** | Requires Cunning 3+. Pay 2 Treasure per Asset to grant it the Stealth quality. Cannot be done in a location with a rival's Base of Influence. |
| **Sell Asset** | Decommission an Asset voluntarily; gain half its purchase cost in Treasure, rounded down. No Treasure if the Asset is damaged. |

## Faction Goals

Each faction has an active goal. Completing it earns XP to improve attributes (cost in XP = next level's HP value; must buy levels in order).

| Goal | Difficulty (XP) | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Blood the Enemy | 2 | Inflict HP damage to enemy Assets/Bases equal to total Force + Cunning + Wealth |
| Destroy the Foe | 2 + avg attribute | Destroy a rival faction entirely |
| Eliminate Target | 1 | Destroy one specific undamaged rival Asset within 3 turns |
| Expand Influence | 1 (+1 if contested) | Plant a new Base of Influence in a new location |
| Inside Enemy Territory | 2 | Have Cunning-score number of Stealthed Assets in rival Base locations |
| Invincible Valour | 2 | Destroy a Force Asset with higher minimum purchase rating than the faction's Force |
| Peaceable Kingdom | 1 | Take no Attack actions for 4 turns |
| Root Out the Enemy | varies | Destroy a rival Base of Influence; difficulty = half average of that faction's attributes, rounded up |
| Sphere Dominance | 1 per 2 destroyed | Destroy a number of rival Assets of one type equal to your score in that attribute |
| Wealth of Kingdoms | 2 | Spend Treasure equal to 4× Wealth rating on bribes and influence |

## Sample Arthurian Factions

### The Saxon Shore Confederation
A loose alliance of Saxon sea-lords raiding the eastern coast.
- **Force 6, Wealth 3, Cunning 2, Magic: None**
- **Tag:** Martial
- **HP:** 12+4+2 = 18
- **Starting Assets:** Infantry (2), Cavalry (1), Scouts (1)
- **Goal:** Blood the Enemy (Difficulty 2)

### The Bishop of Londinium's See
The most powerful churchman in southern Britain, with vast estates and political influence.
- **Force 2, Wealth 6, Cunning 5, Magic: Low**
- **Tag:** Rooted
- **HP:** 2+12+9 = 23
- **Starting Assets:** Court Patronage, Pleaders, Tenant Farmers, Monastery Infirmary
- **Goal:** Expand Influence (Difficulty 1)

### The Faerie Court of the Forest Perilous
An Otherworld power that exerts influence over the deep forests of Britain.
- **Force 4, Wealth 3, Cunning 7, Magic: High**
- **Tag:** Concealed, Holy
- **HP:** 6+4+16 = 26
- **Starting Assets:** Bewitching Charmer, Faerie Shape-Changers, Bound Faerie Killer
- **Goal:** Inside Enemy Territory (Difficulty 2)

### The Brotherhood of the Pendragon Road
A secret society of knights loyal to the old Pendragon dynasty, operating in the shadows of Arthur's court.
- **Force 3, Wealth 3, Cunning 5, Magic: Low**
- **Tag:** Concealed, Rooted
- **HP:** 4+4+9 = 17
- **Starting Assets:** Paid Friends at Court, Watchful Retainers, Parish Gossip Network
- **Goal:** Root Out the Enemy (Difficulty varies)
