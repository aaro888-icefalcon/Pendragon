# Estate People: Household Organization and Staff

All household roles, their functions, costs, and the three-tier personnel model for £10, £50, and £120 estates.

**Use when:** Staffing an estate, determining who performs which function, resolving officer skill rolls, or adjudicating who is present and able to act.

---

## Household Organization Overview

A knight's household divides into three functional groups:

| Group | Who | When Present |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| **Mesnie** (military) | Knights, cavalry, soldiers | Campaign and defense |
| **Famuli** (manor staff) | Officers, craftsmen, servants | Resident at manor |
| **Entourage** | Personal attendants, professionals | Travel with the lord |

See `02-entourage-members.md` for full entourage personnel. This file covers the estate-based staff.

---

## The Army (Mesnie)

**Servitium Debitum Formula:** 1 knight + 3 foot soldiers per £10 of annual income.

The knight himself counts as 1 of the knights owed. He must maintain and pay the rest.

| Estate Income | Knights Needed | Foot Soldiers Needed | Annual Army Cost (55% of income) |
|--------------|---------------|---------------------|----------------------------------|
| £10 | 1 (self) | 3 | £5.5 |
| £20 | 2 | 6 | £11 |
| £50 | 5 | 15 | £27.5 |
| £120 | 12 | 36 | £66 |

## Household Officers

The core administrative and domestic staff of any estate above £10.

### Steward

**Function:** Overall estate management; income collection; accounts; supervision of all domestic staff.

**Key Skill:** Stewardship

**Rolls Made:** Stewardship for annual income recovery; Stewardship Rebuild for permanent damage repair; financial calculations each Winter Phase.

**Upkeep:** £2–£4/year (varies by estate size and seniority).

**Availability:** Required for any estate above £10. At £10, the knight's wife often fulfills this role.

---

### Constable

**Function:** Military household management; weapons and armor maintenance; oversight of the constabulary herd (working animals).

**Key Skill:** Battle, Horsemanship

**Constabulary Herd Responsibility:** All working animals (oxen, cart horses, cattle, livestock) are under the Constable's care.

**Upkeep:** £2/year.

---

### Marshal

**Function:** Horses specifically — the warhorse, palfreys, and riding animals of the lord and family.

**Key Skill:** Horsemanship, Animal Handling

**Distinction from Constable:** The Constable handles working animals; the Marshal handles noble riding animals.

**Upkeep:** £1–£2/year.

---

### Chaplain

**Function:** Religious services; reading and writing; advising on ecclesiastical matters; sometimes serving as secretary.

**Key Skill:** Religion (Christian), Read/Write Latin, Orate

**Additional:** May make Chirurgery rolls if no physician is present; handles official correspondence.

**Upkeep:** £1–£2/year plus board. May also hold a benefice (church living) that partially offsets cost.

---

### Cook / Chef

**Function:** Kitchen management; feeding the household.

**Key Skill:** Industry (cooking specialty)

**Staff:** At larger estates, the Cook commands a kitchen staff of assistants.

**Upkeep:** Cook: £1/year. Kitchen staff: £0.5/year each.

---

### Other Officers (at larger estates)

| Officer | Function | Key Skill | Upkeep |

## Household Bonus Pay Table

Officers may receive annual bonuses above their stated upkeep to reward loyalty and performance.

| Officer Level | Bonus Pay (per year) |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Senior officer (Steward, Constable) | +£1 to +£3 |
| Mid-level officer (Marshal, Chaplain) | +£0.5 to +£1 |
| Skilled servant | +£0.25 to +£0.5 |

Bonus pay does not affect skills but improves the officer's Loyalty passion (GM discretion: typically +1 to passion for meaningful bonus).

## Manorial Staff vs. Household Staff

Two categories within the Famuli:

| Category | Who | Paid From |
|----------|-----|----------|
| Household Staff | Officers and personal servants inside the hall | 50% of Assized Rent |
| Manorial Staff | Agricultural supervisors, skilled tradesmen, estate craftsmen | 25% of Assized Rent |
| Raw Materials | Purchased inputs for production | 25% of Assized Rent |

This breakdown is for accounting purposes; all three come out of the Court expense category.

## Departure and Replacement

Any officer or staff member may depart during the year due to:
- Death (disease, accident, old age)
- Better offer elsewhere
- Dismissal by the knight

**Replacement time:** 1 season to find a replacement of comparable skill (for major officers). Interim period: no rolls for that officer's function, or a lesser roll at -5 if someone else covers.

**Dismissal without cause:** The knight loses 1 Honor if dismissing a loyal officer who has served more than 3 years. Exception: documented misconduct.

## Forestry and Hunting Staff

For estates with significant woodland:

| Role | Function | Upkeep |
|------|---------|--------|
| Forester | Manages woodland; enforces forest law | £1/year |
| Woodward | Oversees sustainable cutting | £0.5/year |
| Verderer | Courts officer for forest pleas | Honorary; no upkeep |
| Huntsman | Coordinates hunt; knows the ground | £1/year |

These roles are only needed if the estate has a Chace, Deer Park, or substantial woods.
