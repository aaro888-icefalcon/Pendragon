# Estate Investments and Improvements

Complete taxonomy of all buildable structures, fortifications, enhancements, and income-generating investments, with costs, income, maintenance, Defense Values, and Glory awards.

**Use when:** A knight wants to build, buy, or upgrade anything on his estate; calculating construction costs; adjudicating what a structure provides.

## Fortifications

### Defense Value (DV) System

DV represents the defensive strength of a structure or complex. Higher DV = more difficult to take by storm, escalade, or treachery.

Attackers must overcome the DV to breach the defense. Add all applicable DV elements to get total complex DV.

## Simple Siege Resolution

### Procedure

1. Attacker states method: assault, escalade, mining, or starvation.
2. Compare attacker's force to DV and garrison.
3. Apply modifiers.
4. Roll and consult casualty table.

### Siege Modifiers

| Condition | Modifier |
|-----------|---------|
| Attackers have siege engines | +2 to attack roll |
| Defenders have adequate supplies (1+ year) | -3 to attacker's chance |
| Gate is the point of attack | Use gate DV (DV -1 or -2) |
| Wet moat present | -2 to all assault attempts |
| Attacker outnumbers defenders 5:1+ | +2 |
| Treachery (inside help) | Automatic success at the gate |

### Siege Casualty Table

| Result | Attacker Casualties | Defender Casualties |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Assault repelled | 20% of attackers | 5% of defenders |
| Assault succeeds | 10% of attackers | 30% of defenders |
| Prolonged siege (starvation) | 5% of attackers/month | 10% of defenders/month |

### Orchard Income Progression

| Years After Planting | Annual Income |
|---------------------|--------------|
| 1–4 | -£1 (maintenance cost) |
| 5–8 | £0 (break even) |
| 9–12 | +£1 |
| 13–16 | +£2 |
| 17+ | +£3 |

Setup cost: included in the negative income of years 1–4.

### Vineyard Special Rules

- Setup cost: £12.
- First 3 years: -£1/year (establishment cost).
- Year 4 onward: +£3/year.
- Each year: roll 1d20. On a **20**, the vineyard fails (disease, frost, neglect) and is destroyed. Must pay £12 to re-establish.

### Horse Herd

Income varies by breed and market. Consult GM for specific values. Requires stabling and pasture infrastructure.

### Woods / Sustainable Forestry

- Income: **10% of the woods' assessed value per year** (sustainable cutting).
- If over-cut (more than 10% taken): cumulative risk of woodland collapse. GM rolls each year of over-exploitation; on a failure, the woodland is depleted and income drops to £0 for 1d6 years.

### Toll

| Local road | £1 |
| Main road | £2–£3 |
| River crossing (small) | £1 |
