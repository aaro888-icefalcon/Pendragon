## NPC Baron Stat Blocks

### Sir Staterius, Baron of Thornbush

- **County:** Silchester (Farnham, Surrey)
- **Customary Revenue (CR):** £245.9
- **Army:** 24 household knights + 52 Foot Soldiers
- **Castle:** Thornbush — Motte-and-bailey with shell keep; tower goes deep into the motte — DV 5/13
- **Anarchy events:** Sir Nastor (vassal of Duke Ulfius) slays Staterius's son and becomes an ally of Ulfius; shell keep added around motte during Anarchy
- **Notes:** A minor baron closely tied to Duke Ulfius. After Sir Nastor's action, Thornbush falls into Ulfius's sphere of influence.

### Count Roderick of Salisbury

- **County:** Salisbury (Wiltshire/Hampshire)
- **Customary Revenue (CR):** £1,574
- **Army:** 15 eschilles (~150 knights + ~300 Foot Soldiers)
- **Castle (caput major):** Sarum Rock — Hilltop fortified settlement; DV 13/3
- **Uther Period role:** Count of Salisbury; loyal to Uther Pendragon; commands the defense of Salisbury
- **Anarchy events:** Dies in the battle (or from wounds; exact timing variable); Countess Ellen rules in his stead, relying on Player-knights
- **Wife:** Countess Ellen (rules Salisbury during Anarchy)
- **Heir:** Sir Robert, Count of Salisbury (Boy King Period)
- **Honour notes:** £1,574 CR makes Roderick one of the wealthiest barons in Logres; 15 eschilles is an enormous army

---

### Duke Ulfius, Duke of the Vale

- **County:** Silchester (becomes center of power); also holds lands in Berroc, Rydychan
- **Customary Revenue (CR):** £1,312.9
- **Army:** 124 knights + proportional Foot Soldiers (estimated 248 field Foot + 124 garrison Foot)
- **Castle:** Silchester Castle — Motte-and-bailey in SW corner of old Roman fort, with ancient walls and ditch on two sides and a stone great tower — DV 16/6/10 (Uther); strengthened in Anarchy
- **Uther Period role:** Duke of the Vale; administrator and general under Uther; described as "the most powerful man in Logres" during the Anarchy
- **Anarchy role:** With no king, Ulfius becomes the de facto power in central Logres, extending control over Silchester, Rydychan, parts of Berroc and Hantonne
- **Boy King Period:** Submits to Arthur; retires; the title of Duke disappears after him (then resurfaces later)
- **Other castles held (Anarchy):**
  - Astolat (Silchester): Takes control; rebuilds to stone in Boy King Period (DV 8/16)
  - Stoneskeep (Berroc): Retakes from East Saxons
  - High Ground (Rydychan): Local commoners surrender to him
  - Buck, Castle of the (Caerwent): Repaired in Boy King Period

## Economics Model: Complete Reference (Appendix D)

### Table D.2: Summary of Demesne Income and Expenses

| Income Item | Equals |
|-------------|--------|
| Assized Rents | Income from the land of the honour |
| Other Income | Income from court profits, money fiefs, and liberties |
| Customary Revenue | Assized Rents + Other Income |
| Free Income | Fee Farm profits + Improvements income + fee simple/allodial income − obligations − maintenance |
| Annual Income | Customary Revenue + Free Income |

| Total Income | Annual Income + Production |
| Pay Bonus | Additional bonus paid by liege to improve officer's Standard of Living |

|-------------|--------|

| Army | 55% of Customary Revenue |
| Court | 25% of Customary Revenue |

| Manorial Staff | 25% of Assized Rents |
| Raw Materials | 25% of Assized Rents |
| Total Expense | Customary Revenue + Free Income + Production |
| Standard of Living | Family + Pay Bonus + £5 |

### £10 Demesne Manor (New Model) — Full Breakdown

**Total Annual Income:** £10 Assized Rent + £10 Production = £20

**From Customary Revenue (£10):**
- Family (£1): Children £1
- Army (£5.5): Lord Knight (with horses) £3, Squire (with horse) £1, three Foot Soldiers £1.5
- Court (£2.5): Lady Wife £1, Lady's Maid £0.5, Chaplain £1
- Discretionary Fund: £1

**From Production (£10):**
- Manorial Staff (£2.5): Bailiff £0.75, Clothier £0.5, Stabler £0.75, Domestic £0.25, Laborer £0.25
- Raw Materials: £2.5
- Household Staff (£5): Cook £0.75, Scullion £0.25, Clothier £0.5, Dog Boy + hounds £1, Two Grooms £0.5, Lackey £0.25, Two Domestics £0.5, Cart and Team (two carters, two oxen) £1, Extra sumpter £0.25

**Standard of Living** (Lord Knight, Squire, horses, Lady Wife, Children): £4 + £1 + £1 = **£6**

## Glossary of Key Economic Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Allodial holding | Held free of any lord; only kings in Britain |
| Annual Glory | 1 point per £ Customary Revenue + Improvements; max 100/year |
| Annual Income | Customary Revenue + Free Income |
| Assized Rent | Income of land, paid as Render |
| Caput major | Chief manor of a holding; where the lord lives |
| Conroi | Full army of a baron (all eschilles + Foot) |
| Customary Revenue | Assized Rents + Other Income; the value of the holding |
| Discretionary Fund | Demesne Free Income + 10% Demesne Customary Revenue |
| Escheat | Reversion of land to the king upon attainder or lack of heir |
| Eschille | Squadron of 5–15 mounted armored warriors; typical = 10 knights |
| Free Income | Income on which no servitium debitum is owed |
| Geld | Land tax at 17% of Customary Revenue (Uther Period) |
| Homage | Permanent loyalty oath from knight to lord |
| Hundred Court | Three percent of holding's Customary Revenue if held |
| Lot | 1/10 of an estate's total income; used in shortage tracking |
| Primer Seisin | King collects 1 year's profits (or 0.5) at succession |
| Production | Income = Assized Rents; from conversion of raw materials |
| Relief | Inheritance tax = approx. 1 year's income of the holding |
| Render | Payment of Assized Rent in goods (usually food) |
| Servitium debitum | 1 knight + 2 Foot + 1 garrison Foot per £10 Honour CR |
| Wardship | King's custody of a minor heir and the holding during minority |

## NPC Warlord Generation (Steps Summary)

For a Gamemaster-created baron NPC, roll or choose:

1. **Rank/Type:** d6 — 1–2 Minor Baron, 3–4 Baron, 5 Rich Baron, 6 Great Baron
2. **County:** d20 on county table
3. **Honour Size:** CR by rank (see Step 2, `02-baronies-and-honors.md`)
4. **Caput Major:** Location, stronghold type, Assized Rent
5. **Outliers:** Number and type
6. **Vassals:** Number of enfeoffed knights
7. **Personality (Steps 1–7 of random generator):**
   - Step 1: Background (1d6 — Roman, Pagan, British Church, Roman Church, Military, Commercial)
   - Step 2: Primary trait (roll on trait table)
   - Step 3: Secondary traits (2d6 additional directed traits)
   - Step 4: Hair (1d20 on table)
   - Step 5: Expression (1d20 on table)
   - Step 6: Face (1d20 on table)
   - Step 7: Voice/mannerism (1d6 each column on 6-column table)
8. **Kit:** 1d6 → Very Expensive / Spectacular / Regal (see `06-warlord-warfare.md`)
9. **Coat of Arms:** Use random heraldry system (Appendix C)
10. **Glory and army:** Calculate from CR

## New Skills (Appendix K)

### Clerk
- **Starting Skill (Knights):** 0
- Write, read, do numbers, file records, fill out forms, know proper forms of correspondence, courtly and diplomatic procedures, judicial forms, accounting. Basically: office work.

### Diplomacy
- **Starting Skill:** 2 (not a knight skill; held by educated administrators and clerics)
- Formal discourse between lords, kings, and their representatives.
- Requires: knowledge of customs; understanding of personalities; knowledge of language (subtle and blunt); knowing what gift to give and when.
- **Use cases:** Settling disputes between barons; dealing with king's officers; dealing with cities, bishops, abbots, and tribes; setting up formal battles; bringing peace; setting terms of mutual raiding; negotiating marriages; competing to rent Fee Farms.
- **Substitute:** Anyone with Courtesy may attempt Diplomacy at ¼ Courtesy skill (modifiers may apply).
