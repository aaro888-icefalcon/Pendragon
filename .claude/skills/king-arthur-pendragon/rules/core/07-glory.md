# Glory

All Glory awards by category, Glory quality tiers, bonus point system, and Glory's effect on skills.

**Use when:** awarding Glory after any event, determining a character's social tier, applying bonus points, or adjudicating Glory-modified skill rolls.

---

## Glory Quality Tiers

| Total Glory | Status |
|---|---|
| 1–999 | Non-knight / beginning knight |
| 1,000–1,999 | Average knight |
| 2,000–3,999 | Notable knight |
| 4,000–7,999 | Famous knight |
| 8,000+ | Extraordinary knight |

---

## Base Glory Scale (Table 5-1)

| Glory Amount | Significance |
|---|---|
| 0 | Mundane (routine, uneventful) |
| 1 | Minimal (minor accomplishment) |
| 10 | Ordinary (noteworthy for a knight) |
| 100 | Heroic (exceptional deed) |
| 1,000 | Extraordinary (legendary act) |

---

## Combat Glory

### Standard Combat

Glory from combat = total damage inflicted on opponent (or points-based, per GM).

- Non-combat Glory = die roll result (e.g., Awareness roll success = the number rolled as Glory)
- Critical success in non-combat = 20 Glory points
- Significant non-combat achievement = ×2 to ×5 multiplier at GM's discretion

### Bonus Glory Awards

| Circumstance | Bonus Glory |
|---|---|
| Bad odds (enemy outnumbers by 3:1 or worse) | +100 per knight involved |
| Witnessed by a greater noble | +25 Glory |
| Witnessed by a king or Round Table knight | +50 Glory |
| Witnessed by own lord | +100 Glory |
| Witnessed by the High King | +150 Glory |
| Helped a famous hero significantly | +100 Glory |
| Performed a flourish (stylish move) | +10 Glory |
| GM laughs aloud at deed | +10 Glory |

### Ranged Combat Glory

Glory from ranged kills = 1/10 normal combat Glory.

## Battle Glory

Battle Glory is calculated per round:

### Basic Glory Per Round by Battle Size

| Battle Scale | Basic Glory Per Round |
|---|---|
| Small (200–2,000 participants) | 15 |
| Medium (2,001–5,000) | 30 |
| Large (5,001–10,000) | 45 |
| Huge (10,001+) | 60 |

*Note: rounds spent at the rear of battle (no risk) do not count.*

### Combat Action Multiplier (Per Round)

| Action Result | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Critical success | ×2 |
| Success or partial success | ×1 |
| Failure or fumble | ×0.5 |
| Disengaged (not fighting) | ×0.1 |

### Victory Multiplier

| Battle Outcome | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Decisive victory | ×2 |
| Indecisive | ×1 |
| Decisive defeat | ×0.5 |

### Special Multipliers

| Situation | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Unit outnumbered more than 2:1 | ×1.5 |
| Unit outnumbered 5:1 or more | ×2 |
| Unit outnumbered enemy more than 2:1 | ×0.75 |
| Unit outnumbered enemy 5:1 or more | ×0.5 |

*Apply only one special multiplier — the one that applied for the longest duration.*

### Commander Glory

- **Army commander:** basic battle Glory × total battle rounds × victory multiplier
- **Battalion commander:** 1/3 of army commander's commander Glory
- **Unit commanders:** no extra commander Glory
- All commanders also gain individual round-by-round Glory normally

## Tournament Glory

- All tournament participants: 1/10 normal Glory per combat round
- Joust winner (elimination champion): additional bonus per tournament size
- Grand Melee champion: named Tournament Champion; prize + additional bonus

### Tournament Size and Glory

| Tournament Size | Participants | Melee/Joust Champion Glory | Sponsor Glory | Participant (base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neighborhood | ~100 knights | 50 | 50 | 10 |
| Local | 500–1,000 | 100 | 100 | 10–20 |
| Regional | ~2,000 | 200 | 200 | 20–30 |
| Regal | 3,000+ | 300 | 300 | 30 |

## Annual Glory Awards

### Landholding
- £6 income from land = 6 Glory/year (1 Glory per £1 of income)
- Castle holdings = additional annual Glory (amount per GM)

### Trait and Passion Awards
- Any trait or passion ≥ 16 = annual Glory equal to that value (e.g., Valorous 17 = 17 Glory/year)

### Rank Awards (On Receiving Title)
- Vassal knight (first time): +50 Glory
- Knight banneret (first year): +100 Glory; thereafter = total estate income in Glory/year (at least 30)
- Baron: yearly Glory = landholding value up to 100 Glory/year
- Earl: "third penny" from county courts plus landholding Glory
- Pennath: up to 100 Glory/year based on holding size

### Religious/Chivalric Awards
- Any qualifying Religious knight: +100 Glory/year
- Chivalrous knight (Arthur's era): +100 Glory/year

### Marriage Glory
- Each spouse gains Glory equal to the other spouse's current Glory (up to 1,000 max from marriage)

### Knighthood
- +1,000 Glory upon being knighted (one-time)

### Spending Awards
- Extravagant maintenance (Rich or Superlative): gain Glory equal to annual libra spent
- Knighting a new knight: father and new knight both gain 1 Glory per librum spent (sums under £100)

---

## Glory Bonus Points

**Rate:** 1 Bonus Point per 1,000 Glory (cumulative)

**Uses:** apply 1 point to increase any stat, trait, passion, or skill by +1.

**Rules:**
- Can exceed cultural maximum (e.g., push SIZ above 18)
- Can increase attributes after age 35 (otherwise age prevents stat increases)
- Applied during Winter Phase Step 9

---

## Glory Effects on Skills

Non-combat courtly skills receive a bonus based on total Glory:
- +1 per 1,000 Glory to rolls for non-combat courtly skills (Courtesy, Romance, Orate, etc.)
- Maximum at GM's discretion

---

## Special Glory Notes

### Ransoms and Prisoners
- Ransoming and capturing nobles in battle: standard combat Glory + additional per their rank
- Blood Price received/paid: no Glory awarded (only affects Honor/passion)

### Recorded vs. Applied Glory
- "Glory This Game" (session total) is transferred to main Glory total during **Winter Phase Step 8**
- Bonus points calculated on new total during **Winter Phase Step 9**
