# Character Creation

Complete step-by-step character generation for a starting Cymric vassal knight, 485 AD, Salisbury campaign.

**Use when:** creating a new player knight or replacing a dead one.

---

## 6-Step Creation Overview

1. Personal Data
2. Traits and Passions
3. Attributes
4. Skills
5. Previous Experience
6. Other Data

---

## Step 1: Personal Data

**Default setting:** Cymric knight in Salisbury, 485 AD, serving Earl Roderick under King Uther Pendragon.

**Starting age:** 21 (squire from age 15; knighted age 21).

**Name, homeland, lord, family:** record on sheet.

---

## Step 2: Traits and Passions

## Step 3: Attributes

**Point pool:** 60 points to distribute among the five primary attributes.

### Cymric Attribute Ranges (Table 2-1)

| Attribute | Minimum | Maximum | Cultural Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIZ | 8 | 18 | — |
| DEX | 5 | 18 | — |
| STR | 5 | 18 | — |
| CON | 5 | 21 | +3 (included in max) |
| APP | 5 | 18 | — |

The +3 CON cultural bonus is **already included** in the point pool allocation.

### Derived Statistics

| Derived Stat | Formula |
|---|---|
| Damage | (SIZ + STR) / 6 d6 (round down; minimum 1d6) |
| Healing Rate | (CON + STR) / 10 (round down) |
| Move | (STR + DEX) / 10 (round down) |
| Total Hit Points | CON + SIZ |
| Unconscious threshold | Total HP / 4 (round down) |
| Major Wound threshold | = CON score |
| Knockdown threshold | = SIZ score |

## Step 5: Skill Personalization (5 Steps)

Each step: choose one option from the list below. Apply before caps.

**Maximum cap from personalization:** 15 (no skill may exceed 15 from personalization alone).

**Restrictions:** may not take Non-Knightly skills during personalization.

**Personalization options (choose one per step):**
- +3 to one combat skill
- +3 to one knightly non-combat skill
- +1 to one combat skill AND +1 to one other skill
- Increase one skill that is already at 10+ by +3

### Previous Experience (Optional)

Up to 5 additional years of experience before play. Each year grants 2 choices from:
- +3 to any one skill (max 15 cap still applies during this phase)
- +1 to two different skills
- +1d3 to Loyalty (lord) or another passion (max 19)

## Step 6: Other Data

### Starting Glory

- Base: 6d6 + 150
- Alternative: 1/10 of father's Glory (from family history rolls)
- Knighthood bonus: +1,000 Glory (already included for starting characters)

### Starting Equipment

**Ordinary Knight's Starting Gear:**
- Chainmail and shield
- 2 spears, sword, dagger
- Fine clothing (worth £1)
- Personal gear, travel gear, war gear
- 4 horses: charger, 2 rouncys, sumpter

**Poor Knight's Starting Gear:**
- Chainmail and shield
- Spear, sword, dagger
- Threadbare clothing (worth 10s.)
- Personal gear
- 2 rouncys

**Rich Knight's Starting Gear:**
- Chainmail and 2 shields (one painted)
- 2 spears, sword, dagger, 8 jousting lances, any one other weapon
- Rich clothing (worth £2)
- Personal gear, travel gear, war gear
- 6 horses: charger, courser, 3 rouncys, sumpter

### Squire

Starting squire statistics:
- Age: 15
- First Aid: 6
- Battle: 1
- Horsemanship: 6
- One other skill: 5

## Distinctive Features

Number of distinctive features = APP modifier:
- APP 1–5: 3 features
- APP 6–10: 2 features
- APP 11–15: 1 feature
- APP 16+: 0 features (notably handsome/beautiful; no quirks needed)

Roll d6 per feature:

| d6 | Feature Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Scar (combat or accident) |
| 2 | Unusual hair (color or style) |
| 3 | Distinctive eye color |
| 4 | Build (very tall, stocky, thin) |
| 5 | Mannerism or habit |
| 6 | Birthmark or skin feature |

## Knighthood Ceremony

The knighting ceremony includes:
1. Vigil (night of prayer before knighting)
2. Bath and dressing in white (purity)
3. Oath sworn to lord
4. Accolade (dubbing with sword — the "coulee")
5. Investiture of sword, spurs, shield

**Leap for +10 Glory:** knight may leap onto his horse unassisted for +10 Glory (requires Horsemanship roll or DEX roll; failure means no bonus and mild embarrassment).

**Glory awarded:** +1,000 Glory upon receiving knighthood.
