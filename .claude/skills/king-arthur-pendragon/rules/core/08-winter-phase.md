# Winter Phase

Complete 9-step annual Winter Phase procedure: experience checks, aging, economic circumstances, stable rolls, family events, training, and Glory calculation.

**Use when:** running end-of-year resolution between scenarios.

---

## The 9 Steps of Winter Phase

---

### Step 1: Solo Scenario (Optional)

Players may run individual solo scenarios for their knights. These are GM-designed personal adventures.

Results from solos take effect before other Winter Phase calculations.

---

### Step 2: Experience Rolls

For each **checked** skill on the character sheet:

1. Roll d20
2. If the d20 result **exceeds** the current skill value → skill increases by +1
3. If the d20 result ≤ current value → no change (the check is erased regardless)
4. Erase all experience checks after rolling (whether they increased or not)

**Skills over 20:** add the bonus (skill − 20) to the d20 roll. If the modified total exceeds the current value → +1.

**This step is mandatory** — all checks must be rolled.

---

### Step 3: Aging

**All characters:** increase age by 1 year.

**Characters age 35 or older:** roll on aging tables.

#### Table 5-2: Number of Stats Lost (Roll 2d6)

| 2d6 | Stats Lost |
|---|---|
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 |
| 6–8 | 0 |
| 9 | 1 |
| 10 | 2 |
| 11 | 3 |
| 12 | 4 |

#### Table 5-3: Which Stat Is Lost (Roll 1d6 per stat lost)

| 1d6 | Stat Reduced by 1 |
|---|---|
| 1 | SIZ |
| 2 | DEX |
| 3 | STR |
| 4 | CON |
| 5 | APP |
| 6 | No loss this roll |

**Consequences of stat loss:**
- Stat reduced to 3 or below: character is **bedridden** (cannot adventure)
- Stat reduced to 0: character **dies**
- Recalculate all derived statistics after aging (HP, Damage, Move, Healing Rate, etc.)

---

### Step 4: Economic Circumstances

Determine the knight's economic grade for the year, then apply modifiers to Steps 5 and 6.

#### Economic Grades and Annual Income

| Grade | Annual Income | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| Impoverished | Less than £3/year | No squire, often no horse; counts as sergeant in battle |
| Poor | £3–£5/year | No squire; mangy horse; ragged |
| Ordinary | £6–£8/year | One squire; standard maintenance |
| Rich | £9–£12/year | Two squires; fine clothing; extra glory |
| Superlative | £13+/year | Three squires; magnificent appearance; glory bonuses |

#### Modifiers Applied by Grade

**Impoverished:**
- −15 to Steps 5 and 6 dice rolls
- Armor loses 1 point of protection per year (rust/disrepair)
- CON roll each year — failure = −1 CON permanent
- No Winter Phase Glory from landholding or rank

**Poor:**
- −3 to Steps 5 and 6 dice rolls
- Cumulative additional −3 per consecutive year of poverty

**Ordinary:**
- No modifiers

**Rich:**
- +1 to child survival rolls (Step 6)
- +3 to childbirth rolls (Step 6)

**Superlative:**
- +2 to horse survival rolls (Step 5)
- +3 to childbirth rolls (Step 6)
- +3 to child survival rolls (Step 6)

---

### Step 5: Stable Rolls

For each horse owned, roll d20:

| d20 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Horse dies |
| 3–20 | Horse survives |

**Modifier:** −1 to the d20 roll for each year the horse is over 7 years old.

Apply economic modifiers from Step 4 to all horse survival rolls.

---

### Step 6: Family Rolls

Apply economic modifiers from Step 4 to all rolls in this step.

#### Marriage (if knight is unmarried)

Roll d20 on Random Marriage Table (5-4):

| d20 | Result | Wife's Holding | Wife's Annual Income | Glory Gained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | No holding | — | £1d3+6 | 10 |
| 5–7 | 1 manor | £1d6 | 25 |
| 8–17 | 1 manor | £1d6+3 | 50 |
| 18–19 | 2 manors | £1d6+6 | 100 |
| 20+ | 2 manors | £2d6+6 | 250 |

**Marriage Glory:** each spouse gains Glory equal to the other spouse's current Glory (maximum 1,000 from this source).

#### Childbirth (Table 5-5)

Roll d20 for each married couple:

| d20 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | No birth this year |
| 11 | Both mother and child die |
| 12 | Child born; mother dies |
| 13 | Twins born (both mother and children survive) |
| 14+ | Child born; mother survives |

**Determining sex:** odd result = female child; even result = male child.

Apply Rich (+3) or Superlative (+3) modifiers to childbirth roll.

#### Child Survival (Table 5-6)

Roll d20 for each child under age 15:

| d20 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Child dies |
| 3–10 | Child falls ill but survives (no permanent effect) |
| 11+ | Child survives normally |

Apply Rich (+1) or Superlative (+3) modifiers to child survival rolls.

#### Family Events (Table 5-7)

Roll d20 for each significant NPC family member:

| d20 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Death |
| 3–7 | Marriage |
| 8–12 | Birth |
| 13–15 | Missing (fate unknown) |
| 16–18 | No event |
| 19–20 | Scandal |

#### Scandalous Rumors (Table 5-8)

Roll when scandal result is obtained; GM determines the nature of the rumor (affects family honor, passions, or social standing).

---

### Step 7: Training

Choose **one** of the following options for the year:

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| Intensive training (one skill) | Roll 1d6+1; that many skill points added; total cannot exceed 15 |
| Dedicated study | +1 to any one skill; maximum 20 from this method |
| Personal development | +1 or −1 to any one attribute, trait, or passion |

**Note:** Training cannot push a skill above 15 (for the first option) or 20 (for the second). Skills above these thresholds must be increased through experience checks or Bonus Points.

---

### Step 8: Compute Glory

Transfer the "Glory This Game" total (accumulated during all scenarios this year) to the permanent Glory total.

Also add all annual Glory awards:
- Landholding Glory (£ income in Glory)
- Rank Glory (vassal, banneret, baron, etc.)
- Trait/Passion Glory (any trait/passion ≥ 16 = that value in Glory)
- Religious/Chivalric Glory (+100 if qualifying)
- Maintenance Glory (if Rich or Superlative, add libra spent)

---

### Step 9: Glory Bonuses

Calculate new Bonus Points:
- **1 Bonus Point per 1,000 Glory** (cumulative total)
- Subtract previously spent bonus points
- Apply new Bonus Points to any stat, skill, trait, or passion (+1 per point)
- Bonus Points can exceed cultural maximums
- Bonus Points can increase attributes past age 35

---

## Annual Glory Quick Reference

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Land income | £ income in Glory (e.g., £6 manor = 6 Glory) |
| Famous trait or passion (each ≥ 16) | = the trait/passion value in Glory |
| Religious qualification | +100 Glory |
| Chivalric qualification | +100 Glory |
| Rich maintenance | = annual libra spent in Glory |
| Superlative maintenance | = annual libra spent in Glory |
| Vassal title (first year only) | +50 Glory |
| Banneret (first year) | +100 Glory |
| Banneret (ongoing) | = total estate income in libra as Glory |
| Baron | Up to 100 Glory/year |
