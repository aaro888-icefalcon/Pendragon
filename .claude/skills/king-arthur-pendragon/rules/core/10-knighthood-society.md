# Knighthood and Society

Feudal obligations, noble ranks, homage, ransom duties, religion mechanics, tournaments, ladies, squires, and court offices.

**Use when:** adjudicating feudal duties, determining a noble's obligations, running a tournament, or navigating social/religious mechanics.

---

## The Feudal Hierarchy

### Noble Ranks (Ascending)

| Rank | Definition | Minimum Knights | Minimum Manors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vassal Knight | Holds at least one manor from lord | 0 | 1 |
| Knight Banneret | Lowest lord rank; holds several manors | 3+ vassals | Several |
| Baron | Holds land directly from king | 25+ | 30-40 |
| Earl / Count | Rules a county | 75+ | 100+ |
| Duke | Highest rank below royalty | 100-200 | 150+ |
| Pennath | Independent; comparable but lesser than king | Varies | Varies |
| King | Independent; owes allegiance only to Pendragon | 100+ | Kingdom |

---

## Vassal Knight

### Requirements
- Must inherit land, receive it as grant/gift, or conquer it
- Holds at least one manor

### Duties
- Serve and protect lord
- Attend lord's court
- Provide military service (fully armed, 40-day provision minimum)
- Garrison duty and summer active service

### Benefits
- Glory: +50 upon receiving title (one-time)
- Annual Glory: = manor income in Glory (usually 6 Glory for £6 manor)
- Income: enough for Ordinary standard of living
- Safety: higher ransom value than bachelor knights
- Wife: usually assigned by lord if unmarried

---

## Knight Banneret

### Requirements
- Hold several manors; have at least 3 vassal knights holding from them

### Benefits (in addition to vassal knight benefits)
- Glory: +100 in first year's Winter Phase; annual Glory = total estate income in libra (minimum 30)
- Income: Rich standard of living (maintains traveling retinue)
- Army: private force (household knights, sergeants, 3+ vassal knights)

---

## Baron

### Annual Maintenance Required
- Minimum £26/year to maintain proper appearance
- Baron himself must live at Rich level minimum

### Benefits
- Annual Glory: = landholding value up to 100 Glory/year
- Crown: eight large pearls
- Form of address: "Lord" (sons and daughters are "Lord/Lady")
- Army: 15+ knights under command

---

## Earl / Count

### Annual Maintenance Required
- Minimum £84/year; earl himself at Superlative level

### Benefits
- Crown: eight strawberry leaves alternating with eight small pearls on spikes
- Form of address: "Right Honorable," "My Lord" (wife = "Countess," "My Lady")
- Army: 50+ knights; at least one medium castle

### Income
- "Third penny" from county courts (significant revenue source)

---

## Duke

- Minimum maintenance: £164/year; Superlative level
- Form of address: "Your Grace," "Most Noble" (wife = "Duchess")
- Army: 100+ knights; at least one large castle

---

## King

- Must live at Superlative level
- Form of address: "Your Highness," "His Majesty" (wife = "Queen")
- Army: all forces of all vassals
- Holds at least one large castle, 2-5 medium, 3-8 small

## Homage Oath (Lord's Homage)

When receiving land from a lord, the vassal swears:

"I [NAME AND TITLE], make known to all men present and to come that I have agreed and sworn to my liege lord [NAME AND TITLE], to give him aid, openly and in good faith, all the days of my life, against all mortal men; and moreover I will never withhold or withdraw such aid from him as long as he shall be ready to do right to me in his court and to let me be judged by those who ought to be my judges in his court."

## Four Universal Aids (Feudal Taxes)

Each may be imposed only once (with exceptions noted):

### 1. Knighting of the Eldest Son
- Each vassal pays = average yearly income of primary holding
- Minimum cost of knighting: £8 (for Ordinary outfitting)
- Excess money: may be used for feast/tournament, or kept (Selfish check if kept)
- Glory from expenditure: goes to new knight AND father

### 2. Wedding of the Eldest Daughter
- Covers wedding expenses and dowry
- Father receives Glory for money spent on the event

### 3. To Ransom the Lord
- Vassals obligated to pay minimum = their usual year's income
- Lord loyalty roll required to fulfill obligation
- Failure to pay: lord loses 1 Loyalty (vassals) AND 1 Honor per knight abandoned
- If lord fails loyalty roll but still ransoms: captured knight released from vassalage oath

### 4. To Support a Crusade
- Must be genuine crusade (not ordinary war)

---

## Ransom Obligations

### Lord Ransoming Household Knights
- Lord makes Loyalty (vassals) roll
- Success: lord ransoms the captured knight
- Failure: lord may pay grudgingly, captured knight loses favor; or knight is abandoned

### Ransomed Knight's Reward
- Knight receives Loyalty (lord) experience check upon being ransomed

## Court Offices

| Office | Function |
|---|---|
| Castellan | Commands and maintains a castle for the lord |
| Chancellor | Keeper of the Royal Seal; second most powerful in kingdom |
| Butler | Oversees food procurement and storage |
| Seneschal/Steward | Commands feeding of court; oversees kitchen |
| Chamberlain | Keeper of lord's chamber and treasurer |
| Constable | Order and comfort of court; commands watchmen, musicians, etc. |
| Marshal | Commands lord's forces in war; household protection |
| Justiciar | Oversees justice in lord's courts |
| Sheriff | Delivers king's justice through shire courts |

Offices are generally held at the lord's pleasure, not as grant or gift. Holder may be removed without cause.

## Religion Mechanics

### Piety and Attitude (Based on Pious Trait)

| Pious Value | Attitude |
|---|---|
| 16+ | Fanaticism (religion first in all decisions) |
| 11-15 | Interest (attends services; accepts beliefs) |
| 6-10 | Indifference (cultural Christian; rarely thinks about it) |
| 0-5 | Hostility (actively dislikes or attacks the church) |

### Truce of God (Peace of God)

Fighting forbidden on:
- All great feast days
- From dusk Friday to dawn Monday
- All church feast days

Protection extends to: churches, clergy, merchants, peasants, and their property.

Maintaining the truce earns an experience check for the Pious trait.

### Just War Requirements (Church Doctrine)

A war is "just" only if ALL conditions are met:
1. The cause is just (clear injury to be redressed)
2. Every reasonable peaceable means has been tried first
3. Declared by legitimate authority (king, duke, bishop, etc.)
4. Waged by honorable means (Truce of God honored, etc.)
5. Damage from the war is less than the original wrong
6. Success is possible (failure = God has prejudged)

## Tournaments

### Tournament Types by Size

| Type | Participants | Champion Glory | Sponsor Glory | Participant (base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neighborhood | ~100 knights | 50 | 50 | 10 |
| Local | 500-1,000 | 100 | 100 | 10-20 |
| Regional | ~2,000 | 200 | 200 | 20-30 |
| Regal | 3,000+ | 300 | 300 | 30 |

### Tournament Events

**The Joust:**
- Single-elimination bracket; one joust winner
- Resolution: Lance skill (blunted weapons)
- Critical success = horse's Damage (not doubled)
- If both lances break without elimination: up to 3 sets of lances may be used
- After 3 lance sets: sword combat on foot; first knockdown loses
- Glory: 1/10 normal per round; winner gets additional size-based bonus

**The Grand Melee:**
- Mock battle; host team vs. visiting team
- All combat with rebated (blunted) weapons
- Rules: no striking from behind; no striking unhelmed opponents; no striking horses
- Eliminated by marshal's order or voluntary withdrawal
- Champion = most outstanding fighter chosen by judges
- Glory: 1/10 normal per round + participation bonus for winning team

**Challenges:**
- A knight may challenge any qualified individual to specific combat
- Open challenges: any knight may accept
- Named challenges: specific knight must respond or suffer Cowardly check
- Refusing without valid excuse: Cowardly check possible; reputation damage

### Tournament Inspection (Helm Show)

Knights may be refused entry if accused or guilty of:
- Arson, breaking oaths, cowardice on the field, excommunication
- Leading a free company, murder, robbery/piracy
- Slandering or dishonoring women, usury, violating churches

### Ladies' Favors

A lady may give a token (scarf, sleeve, glove, ring) to a knight.
- Token worn on arm or tied to lance tip
- Functions as a directed Amor/Love passion for inspiration during tournament

- Move 4; Armor 2; Unconscious 6; Major Wound 14; Total HP 24; Healing Rate 2; Damage 3d6; Glory 850

### Gentlewoman Bonus

If a gentlewoman character has 6 virtues at a total of 80 or more:
- 100 Glory/year
- Economic boost equivalent to one Manor's income

## Squires

### Standard Squire Statistics

- Age: 15
- First Aid: 6
- Battle: 1
- Horsemanship: 6
- One other skill: 5

### Squire Duties

- Tends knight's horses
- Carries knight's extra weapons and gear
- Administers First Aid in battle
- Leads prisoners off the field
- Can be sent to escort wounded knight to rear

## Courtly Romance (Arthur's Era — Future Content)

Available when Queen Guenever establishes the Court of Romance.

### Romantic Knight Requirements
- One specific lover
- 10+ in five of: Compose, Dance, Falconry, Flirting, Gaming, Intrigue, Orate, Play [harp], Read [Latin], Singing, Tourney
- At least once yearly: fulfill lover's bidding (played out or solo scenario)
- Spend at least £1/year on presents or entertainment for lover

### Romantic Knight Benefits
- 50 Glory first year; increase by 50 each successive year (100 second year, 150 third, etc.)

### Amor Passion
- Starting value: 3d6
- Modifiers: +1d3 if either made successful Flirting roll; +1 per 1,000 Glory for significant Glory; +6 if saved lady from dire fate; +1 per APP point over 15
- Consummated Amor becomes Love (amor) passion at same value
