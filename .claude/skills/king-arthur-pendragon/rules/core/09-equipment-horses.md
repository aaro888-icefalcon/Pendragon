## Standards of Living (Grades of Maintenance)

### Impoverished (under £3/year)
Ragged, lean appearance; pitted armor; no squire; often no horse. Counts as sergeant in battle.
- Annual Penalty: -15 to Steps 5 and 6; armor loses 1 point/year; CON roll or -1 CON permanent
- No Glory from landholding or rank

### Poor (£3-£5/year)
No squire; mangy horse; hungry and ragged.
- Annual Penalty: -3 to Steps 5 and 6; cumulative -3 per consecutive poor year

### Ordinary (£6-£8/year)
One squire; standard food, clothing, equipment maintenance. No modifiers.

### Rich (£9-£12/year)
Two squires; fine fabrics and furs; ornate tack; regular feasts.
- Annual Glory: gain Glory = annual libra spent
- Bonuses: +1 child survival; +3 childbirth rolls

### Superlative (£13+/year)
Three squires; elaborate surcoats with gold thread, imported furs.
- Annual Glory: gain Glory = annual libra spent
- Bonuses: +2 horse survival; +3 childbirth; +3 child survival

## Horse Statistics

### Charger
Large, heavy, battle-trained warhorse. Standard knight's warhorse.
- SIZ 34, DEX 17, STR 30, CON 12
- Move 8; Armor 5; Unconscious 12; Major Wound 12; Knockdown 34; Total HP 46; Healing Rate 4; Damage 6d6

### Courser
Large, fast horse; combat-trained; often used as riding/hunting horse.
- SIZ 30, DEX 25, STR 24, CON 15
- Move 9; Armor 5; Unconscious 11; Major Wound 15; Knockdown 30; Total HP 45; Healing Rate 4; Damage 5d6
- Hunting bonus: +5 to Horsemanship while hunting on a trained hunting courser

### Rouncy
Standard riding horse; slower and smaller; combat-trained.
- SIZ 26, DEX 10, STR 18, CON 14
- Move 6; Armor 4; Unconscious 10; Major Wound 14; Knockdown 26; Total HP 40; Healing Rate 3; Damage 4d6

## Horse Combat Training

Combat-trained horses (charger, courser, rouncy): no Horsemanship roll needed for routine combat.

Untrained horses in combat: rider must attempt Horsemanship roll before any action each round:

| Horsemanship Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical success | No further control rolls needed for rest of fight |
| Success | May fight normally this round |
| Failure | Cannot fight this round; may attempt Evasion |
| Fumble | Horse breaks control and bolts |

---

## Ruining Horses

A horse becomes a nag if it takes a Major Wound or fumbles a CON roll during forced march.

Ruined horse: STR -2, CON -2, Move -1. Cannot serve as warhorse.

## Selling Rules

- Selling at market: receives half the listed price
- Selling to own lord: receives full listed price
- Armor sold to other lords: blacksmith value only (half price)
