# Combat

Full combat sequence, weapon resolution, damage, lance charges, mounted combat, multiple opponents, shields, armor, all situational modifiers, and optional tactics.

**Use when:** running any melee, ranged attack, skirmish, or special combat situation.

---

## Melee Round Sequence (5 Phases)

### Phase 1: Determination
- All participants declare actions and targets
- No actions may change after declaration (GM may enforce)

### Phase 2: Resolution
- All combatants roll their weapon skills simultaneously (opposed resolution)
- Determine win/lose/tie for each pair

### Phase 3: Winner's Phase
- Winner rolls damage
- Critical success = double damage (roll dice twice, add totals)
- Winner may gain an experience check for the skill used

### Phase 4: Loser's Phase
- Check for Knockdown
- Apply shield reduction (if applicable)
- Record wounds
- Check for unconsciousness (current HP ≤ Unconscious threshold)

### Phase 5: Movement Phase
- All combatants may move up to their Movement Rate
- Movement is yard-by-yard
- Standing up from knockdown happens in this phase

---

## Combat Outcomes

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Both fail | Miss — no damage to either |
| One succeeds, one fails | Winner rolls damage; loser takes it |
| Both succeed; one rolls higher | Higher roll wins (full success — rolls damage); lower roll = partial success |
| Both succeed; tied roll | No damage; non-sword weapon breaks if sword is involved |
| Fumble | Weapon dropped (sword) or broken (non-sword); may also suffer damage per GM |

### Partial Success (Shield Use)

A character who succeeded but rolled lower than their opponent (partial success/loser who also succeeded) may apply their shield: **6 points of armor reduction** against the incoming damage.

---

## Damage

**Damage formula:** (SIZ + STR) / 6 = number of d6 rolled (round down; minimum 1d6)

**Critical success in combat:** double damage — roll the full dice pool twice and add both totals together.

**Armor reduces damage** before HP is subtracted (see Armor Table below).

---

## Knockdown

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Damage dealt ≥ defender's SIZ | Knockdown check: DEX roll (foot) or Horsemanship roll (mounted) |
| DEX/Horsemanship succeeds | Remain standing |
| DEX/Horsemanship fails | Fall; take 1d6 damage if dismounted from horse |
| Damage dealt ≥ 2 × defender's SIZ | Automatic knockdown; no roll allowed |

**While knocked down:**
- −5 to own combat rolls
- Opponent gains +5 to attack rolls
- Stand up during Movement Phase (counts as full movement action)

| Armor Type | Protection | Skill Penalty | Knighthood? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clothing | 0 | 0 | No |
| Heavy clothing | 1 | 0 | No |
| Padded armor | 2 | 0 | No |
| Leather armor | 4 | −5 | No |

| Chainmail | 10 | −10 | Yes |
| Reinforced chainmail | 12 | −10 | Yes |

**Shield:** 6 armor reduction — applies only on partial success (loser who also succeeded). Shield does NOT stack with worn armor for normal hits; it only activates on partial successes.

**Horse armor (caparison):** provides protection to horse.

## Lance Charge

**Requirements:**
- Must move at least 6 yards in a straight line before contact
- Rider must be mounted on a combat-trained horse

**Modifier:** +5 to Lance roll, UNLESS opponent is:
- Using a Great Spear (negates the +5), OR
- Charging with their own lance (opposing charge = standard opposed roll)

**Damage source:** use the **horse's Damage** (not the rider's personal Damage):
- Charger: 6d6
- Courser: 5d6
- Rouncy: 4d6

**Lance breaking:** the lance breaks if:
- Total damage rolled is an odd number, OR
- The attacker fumbles

A broken lance may not be used further in combat.

**Height advantage:** mounted vs. foot = +5 modifier (reflexive: −5 to foot). Stacks with lance charge: mounted lance charge vs. foot = +10 total vs. foot target's −10.

## Jousting (Tournament)

Nonlethal lance combat. Uses Lance skill with blunted/hollow weapons (coronal tip).

- Resolution identical to lance charge
- Critical success = horse's Damage (NOT doubled)
- Elimination: knocked from horse = eliminated from joust
- If lances break without eliminating either jouster: new lances provided, up to 3 sets
- After 3 sets of lances: combat continues with swords on foot (first knockdown loses)

---

## Multiple Opponents

Maximum opponents vs. one fighter:
- 3 foot combatants vs. 1 foot combatant
- 2 mounted vs. 1 mounted

**Divide the defending skill:** split the skill value evenly (round down) among all attackers. The defender rolls separately against each attacker using this divided value.

**Damage:** winner deals damage to only one opponent per round (defender's choice which attacker they damage if they win their roll).

---

## Mounted vs. Foot (Reflexive Modifier)

- Mounted combatant: +5 to all combat rolls
- Foot combatant vs. mounted: −5 to all combat rolls

This is a reflexive modifier — applies to both sides simultaneously.

---

## Ranged Combat

- **Resolution:** unopposed (no opposing roll from target; just roll vs. skill)
- **Shield cover:** target with shield = −5 to attacker's roll (shield provides cover)
- **Glory:** 1/10 normal Glory from ranged kills

---

## Optional Tactics

### Berserk

**Declaring berserk:** must declare before rolling.

- Opponent attacks first as an unopposed attack (no defense roll from berserk combatant)
- If berserk combatant survives, their attack roll: +10 modifier
- Berserk ends when all visible enemies are defeated or incapacitated

### Defense

**Declaring defense:** must declare before rolling.

- Gain +10 to combat skill roll
- If winning the exchange: deal NO damage (purely defensive)

### Evasion

- Use DEX roll (foot) or Horsemanship roll (mounted) opposed to opponent's weapon skill
- Reflexive: +5/−5 applies (evader gains +5, attacker suffers −5)
- Failure: evader is knocked down (takes 1d6 damage if mounted)

---

## Special Combat Types

### Dodging

- Roll DEX vs. opponent's weapon skill (opposed)
- Failure: character is knocked down

### Brawling

- No weapons; use STR roll, DEX roll, or Grapple skill (GM determines based on action)
- Damage: normal Damage −2d6 (minimum 1d6)

### Grappling

1. Both combatants must drop their weapons (cannot grapple while armed)
2. Resolution: opposed Grapple skill vs. opponent's weapon skill (or Grapple if both grappling)
3. Winner may: pin, attack (Damage −2d6), or throw opponent (1d6 damage)
4. Pinned opponent may attempt to break free each round (opposed STR roll)

### Skirmish (Small-Scale Battle)

A skirmish involves one unit vs. one unit (max ~200 combatants).

**Step 1:** Primary commander makes Battle roll → Table 6-3: Tactics Modifier

| Commander's Battle Roll | First-Round Modifier |
|---|---|
| Critical success | +5 |
| Success | +0 |
| Failure | −5 |
| Fumble | −10 |

This modifier applies to all combat skills in the first round.

**Step 2:** Individual player knights resolve their personal combats normally.

**Step 3:** After 1–5 rounds, unit leader makes Followers' Fate roll (Battle skill, modified by initial Tactics result).

### Table 6-4: Followers' Fate

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical success | One enemy of appropriate rank captured per 5 subordinates. No losses. |
| Success | 10% losses (2% killed, 8% wounded). Survivors victorious. |
| Failure | 50% losses (10% killed, 25% wounded, 15% captured). Survivors retreat. |
| Fumble | 75% losses (50% killed, 25% captured). Survivors routed. |

## Injury Thresholds (Summary)

| Threshold | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Unconscious | Current HP ≤ HP/4 | Falls unconscious |
| Major Wound | Single hit damage ≥ CON | See injury rules |
| Mortal Wound | Single hit damage ≥ Total HP | Death without First Aid within 1 hour |

Full injury rules in **06-injury-death-healing.md**.

## Special Weapon Rules Summary

| Weapon | Special Rule |
|---|---|
| Sword | Never breaks; dropped on fumble; breaks non-sword on tied result |
| Dagger | Damage −1d6 (minimum 1d6) |
| Axe | +1d6 vs. shield users |
| Great Axe | +1d6 vs. all; +1d6 more vs. shields; two-handed (no horseback) |
| Great Spear | +5 vs. horsemen; negates lance charge bonus; two-handed variant |
| Mace | +1d6 vs. chainmail wearers |
| Bow | 3d6 always; max 150 yards; no shield while shooting |
| Javelin | Damage −2d6 (minimum 1d6); max 30 yards |
| Lance | Charge rules above; breaks on odd total damage or fumble |
