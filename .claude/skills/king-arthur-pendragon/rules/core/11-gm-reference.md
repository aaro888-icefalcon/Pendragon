# GM Reference: NPC and Creature Statistics

Stat blocks for all standard NPCs and creatures from the core book, encounter guidelines, hunting rules, and large-scale battle system summary.

**Use when:** running combat against NPCs or creatures, adjudicating hunts, running large battles, or quickly statting an anonymous opponent.

---

## How to Read Stat Blocks

Format: SIZ, DEX, STR, CON, APP; Move; Armor; Unconscious; Major Wound; Knockdown; Hit Points; Healing Rate; Damage.

- **Unconscious** = HP / 4
- **Major Wound** = CON score
- **Knockdown** = SIZ score
- **Armor** includes shield notation where applicable (e.g., "10 + shield" = chainmail + shield)
- **Damage** = dice rolled on a successful attack

## Creatures and Beasts

### Creature Special Rules

**Avoidance:** some creatures use this skill instead of weapon skill when evading hunters (see Hunting, below).

**Ferocity:** some beasts continue to attack for 1 round after being incapacitated.

**Glory Won:** Glory the knight earns for slaying this creature.

**Valorous Modifier:** modifier to Valorous roll required before attacking magical beasts. Failure = will not attack for 1 round; fumble = flees 1d6 rounds.

**Prudent Modifier:** modifier to Prudent roll to avoid combat with creature (when attacking would be suicidal).

### Giant (Typical)
Giants use clubs or natural weapons (grapple/brawl) only.

**Reach:** giants may ignore the standard +5/-5 reflexive modifier for height difference. Their great size negates height advantage for mounted opponents.

Male giants sometimes abduct noblewomen.

Giant stats vary widely; GM should create individual giants. Minimum suggested: SIZ 40+, STR 40+, CON 30+, thick natural armor (10+), Damage 8d6+.

## Hunting Rules

### Hunting Procedure

**Phase 1 — Tracking:** Hunting roll vs. creature's Avoidance (opposed).

**Phase 2 — Pursuit (closing distance):** 1d6 + Avoidance − Hunting roll = number of yards the hunter starts from the quarry.

**Phase 3 — Surprise Attack:**

| Attack Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical success | Double damage. Quarry attacks nearest hunter next round. |
| Success | Normal damage. Quarry attacks unless it took Major Wound or worse (tries to flee). |
| Partial success | Quarry escapes. Hunting roll at +5 on next pursuit. |
| Failure | Quarry escapes. Hunting roll at -5 on next pursuit. |
| Fumble | Quarry escapes and loses trail entirely. -5 to next Hunting roll, back to Phase 1. |

**Phase 4 — The Rush:** if quarry survives surprise, up to 3 melee combatants may attack simultaneously. Quarry divides Avoidance among all attackers (as per multiple opponents rule).

**Phase 5 — Normal Combat:** if quarry survives and attacks, proceed with standard melee.

## Large-Scale Battle System Summary

### Before Battle

1. Determine enemy composition and commanders
2. Note Battle skills of all three level commanders (army, battalion, unit)
3. Determine battle scale (Small = 200-2,000; Medium = 2,001-5,000; Large = 5,001-10,000; Huge = 10,001+)
4. Each army commander secretly writes duration (0-12 rounds willingness to fight)
5. Army commander makes Battle roll; apply Tactics Modifier to all first-round rolls

**Tactics Modifiers to Army Commander Roll:**

| Situation | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Outnumber foe 2:1 or more | +5 |
| Outnumber foe 5:1 or more | +10 |
| Outnumbered 2:1 or more | -5 |
| Outnumbered 5:1 or more | -10 |
| Fighting in own homeland | +10 |
| Fighting in enemy homeland | -10 |

**Tactics Modifier Table (Table 6-3):**

| Battle Roll | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Critical success | +5 |
| Success | +0 |
| Failure | -5 |
| Fumble | -10 |

### First Charge

All mounted knights with lances: use Lance skill modified by battalion commander's Battle roll.

Infantry with great spears in front ranks negates the mounted +5 modifier.

Missile troops shoot first: result determines if knight or horse is hit (odd = horse; even = knight).

### Melee Rounds

Each round: roll 3d6 Melee Events → apply modifier to unit's Battle and Followers' Fate rolls.

| 3d6 | Modifier | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | -15 | Player battalion routs |
| 4 | -10 | Player unit retreats |
| 5 | -10 | Surge of enemy |
| 6 | -5 | Player battalion retreats (triple 2s) |
| 6-8 | -5 | Battalion outnumbered/surrounded/pushed |
| 9-12 | +0 | Undecided |
| 13-15 | +5 | Enemy confused/outnumbered/pulling away |
| 15 | +5 | Enemy battalion retreats (triple 5s) |
| 16-17 | +10 | Surge of victory/enemy unit retreats |
| 18 | +15 | Enemy battalion routs |

### Battle Glory Calculation

Glory per round = Basic × Combat Action Multiplier × Victory Multiplier × Special Multiplier

| Battle Size | Base Glory/Round |
|---|---|
| Small | 15 |
| Medium | 30 |
| Large | 45 |
| Huge | 60 |

| Combat Action | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Critical success | x2 |
| Success or partial success | x1 |
| Failure or fumble | x0.5 |
| Disengaged | x0.1 |

| Outcome | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Decisive victory | x2 |
| Indecisive | x1 |
| Decisive defeat | x0.5 |
