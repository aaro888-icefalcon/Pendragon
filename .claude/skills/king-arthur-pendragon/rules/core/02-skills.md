# Skills

Complete skill list with descriptions, use rules, and weapon/combat special rules.

**Use when:** determining which skill applies to an action, adjudicating a specific skill's mechanics, or looking up a weapon's special properties.

---

## Skill Categories

- **Knightly skills:** appropriate for knights to practice and improve (combat and courtly)
- **Non-Knightly skills:** not normally trained by knights (Industry, etc.)
- **Knowledge skills:** Read, Religion, Folklore, etc.

Personalization may not add to Non-Knightly skills. Max of 15 from character creation.

---

## Non-Combat Skills

### Awareness
Detecting things by sight, sound, or smell. Also used to spot ambushes and reduce visibility penalties.
- In darkness: roll Awareness to reduce visibility penalty from −10 to −5
- Prerequisite for many reactive checks

### Boating
Handling small boats and watercraft. For larger vessels, Boating is attempted at a penalty.

### Chirurgery
Long-term care of wounded characters. See **06-injury-death-healing.md** for full rules.
- Used only on unhealthy patients
- One roll per patient per week
- Success: prevents deterioration that week
- Critical: prevents deterioration AND patient heals double Healing Rate
- Fumble: patient loses 1d3 HP AND suffers deterioration
- Modifier: −5 in unclean/unhealthy conditions
- Cannot have two chirurgeons treat same patient (both automatically fail)

### Compose
Writing poetry, songs, or other artistic works. Roll to create a piece; quality varies by result.

### Courtesy
Social etiquette, noble behavior, proper conduct at court. Used for first impressions, formal requests, and avoiding social gaffes.

### Dancing
Performing courtly dances. Used at feasts and social occasions.

### Faerie Lore
Knowledge of the Faerie realm, magical creatures, and the Other Side.

### Falconry
Training and flying hunting birds.

**Bird Hierarchy (by rank of owner):**

| Bird | Rank |
|---|---|
| Eagle | Emperors |
| Jerfalcon | Kings and Princes |
| Peregrine | Earls |
| Merlin | Ladies |
| Goshawk | Knights |
| Sparrowhawk | Clergy |

### Fashion
Knowing current clothing trends, proper dress, and style.

### First Aid
Immediate emergency treatment of wounds. See **06-injury-death-healing.md** for full rules.
- Restores 1d3 HP on success; 1d3+3 HP on critical
- Fumble: inflicts 1d3 HP damage
- Only works on wounds less than 1 day old
- One application per wound (multiple wounds may each be treated once)

### Flirting
Attracting romantic attention; initial romantic overtures.

### Folklore
Knowledge of common myths, folk remedies, local legends, and peasant customs.

### Gaming
Skill at games of chance and strategy (dice, chess, etc.).

### Heraldry
Recognizing coats of arms, noble houses, and blazons. Used to identify NPCs in combat by their shields/surcoats.

### Hunting
Tracking, finding, and running down quarry. Required for hunts.

**Terrain Modifiers to Hunting:**

| Terrain | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Barren | −5 |
| Forest | +0 |
| Hills | −3 |
| Marsh | −6 |
| Mountains | −4 |
| Open fields | +4 |

Dogs required for hunting: without hounds, −5 penalty (or greater) to Hunting rolls.

### Industry
Crafting, labor, and trade skills. Non-Knightly. Knights performing Industry suffer −2 Honor.

### Intrigue
Court politics, gathering information, manipulation of social situations.

### Orate
Public speaking, formal speeches, delivering proclamations.

### Play [Instrument]
Musical performance. Most common: harp. Each instrument is a separate skill.

### Read [Language/Symbol]
Literacy in a specific language. Most common: Latin. Default starting value: 0 for most knights.

### Recognize
Remembering faces and identifying people previously encountered.

### Religion [Type]
Knowledge of religious doctrine, ritual, and practice. British Christian, Roman Christian, and Pagan are separate skills.

### Romance
Courtly wooing, maintaining romantic relationships, writing love poetry.

### Singing
Vocal performance.

### Stewardship
Managing an estate, overseeing resources, economic administration.

### Swimming
Staying afloat and moving through water.

### Tourney
Skill at tournament competition (general tournament protocol, not specific weapon skills).

## Combat Skills

### Battle
Knowledge and use of individual tactics in skirmishes and large battles.
- Used by commanders to determine Tactics Modifier for first round
- Used by unit leaders for Followers' Fate rolls
- Used by lone knights in melee (−10 penalty if acting alone)

### Horsemanship
Riding and controlling horses in all situations.
- Required to control untrained horses in combat (roll each round before acting)
- Used for Knockdown check when mounted (instead of DEX)
- Used in Evasion tactic (mounted)
- Combat-trained horses (charger, courser, rouncy): no roll needed for routine combat

### Dagger
Standard grip knife fighting.
- Damage: normal Damage −1d6 (minimum 1d6)

### Lance
Couched lance attacks, primarily from horseback.
- See Lance Charge rules in **05-combat.md**
- Two-handed; cannot use from ground effectively (GM discretion)

### Spear
One-handed spear or two-handed use on foot.

### Sword
The premier knightly weapon.
- **Never breaks** (if fumbled: sword is dropped, not broken)
- On tied opposed roll: non-sword weapon breaks
- Sword breaks non-sword weapon on tie

### Axe
Hand axe or battle axe.
- **+1d6 damage vs. shield users** (the axe hooks over shields)

### Great Axe
Large two-handed axe.
- **+1d6 damage vs. all opponents**
- **+1d6 additional damage vs. shield users** (+2d6 total vs. shields)
- Two-handed: cannot use from horseback

### Great Spear
Long spear, often used two-handed.
- **+5 modifier vs. horsemen**
- Negates the lance charge +5 modifier (mounted lance vs. great spear footman = no bonus)
- Two-handed variant cannot be used from horseback

### Mace
War club or flanged mace.
- **+1d6 damage vs. chainmail wearers**

### Bow
Short or long bow.
- Damage: 3d6 regardless of the archer's Damage statistic
- Maximum range: 150 yards
- Cannot use a shield while shooting a bow

### Javelin
Light throwing spear.
- Damage: normal Damage −2d6 (minimum 1d6)
- Maximum range: 30 yards

### Crossbow

| Type | Damage | Rate of Fire | Max Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 1d6 + 10 | 1 per round | 150 yards |
| Medium | 1d6 + 13 | 1 per 2 rounds | 200 yards |
| Heavy | 1d6 + 16 | 1 per 4 rounds | 250 yards |

### Grapple
Unarmed grappling and wrestling.
- Used in Grappling combat (see **05-combat.md**)

## Glory from Skills

Non-combat courtly skills may receive a modifier based on total Glory:
- +1 per 1,000 Glory to non-combat courtly skill rolls (at GM discretion, max capped by GM)
