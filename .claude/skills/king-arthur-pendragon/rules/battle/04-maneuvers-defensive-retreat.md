# Defensive, Out-of-Combat, and Retreat Maneuvers

Every non-offensive maneuver: standing against attacks, pulling back, withdrawing, running away, rallying, sprint/flee fighting, and out-of-combat repositioning. Full prerequisites, opponent selection, skill modifiers, and result tables.

**Use when:** Unit is forced to react defensively, attempting to disengage, or maneuvering in non-combat zones.

---

## How to Read Maneuver Entries

Same format as `03-maneuvers-offensive.md`. Results tables show Army Intensity change (ΔI) and zone movement for Triumph / Win / Loss / Crush.

---

## DEFENSIVE MANEUVERS

### Stand Fast

**Prerequisite:** Unit is Attacked (enemy chose to Attack the player unit this round; or GM specifies enemies are coming)

**Opponents:** 3 random foes rolled; GM chooses 1 to fight players

**Skill Modifier:** None

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −2 | Disengaged | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Normal; Engaged | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |

**Notes:**
- On Triumph, the enemy is repulsed so decisively the unit becomes Disengaged (can Charge next round)
- On Win, unit holds its position; stays Engaged
- On Loss or Crush, unit is pushed back (Recoil = involuntary movement, starts next round Disordered)
- The safest defensive option; GM chooses opponent, but from only 3 rolled

---

### Stand vs. Charge

**Prerequisite:** Unit is Charged (i.e., Unit Commander fumbled their Battle roll — the enemy is counter-charging)

**Opponents:** 3 random mounted foes rolled; GM chooses 1

**Skill Modifier:** None (but enemy has Charge +5 bonus to their Lance)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −3 | No movement | — |
| **Win** | −1 | Normal | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- Triggered only when UC fumbles their Battle roll (enemy gets to counter-charge)
- Opponents must be mounted (only mounted units can Charge)
- If no mounted foes exist, substitute infantry opponents but without the Charge bonus
- A Crush here means the unit is Broken — all members become Alone
- On a Triumph, the player unit actually defeats the charge and can go on the offensive next round

---

### Stand vs. Two

**Prerequisite:** Unit is Attacked by multiple foes (GM scored a Critical on Unit Intensity roll) — forced onto the player

**Opponents:** 3 random foes rolled; GM chooses 2 to fight players

**Skill Modifier:** None (player knights split their attacks between two opponents using Dividing Attacks rule)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −2 | No movement | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Normal | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |

**Notes:**
- Knight must fight both opponents; split weapon skill (halved for each)
- Both results count toward unit tally
- Also available as a UC choice when facing desperate circumstances (Partial Success Battle result)
- A Win here means half or more of the knights held against two opponents — impressive

---

### Stand with Another

**Prerequisite:** None (player chooses this to support a friendly unit)

**Opponents:** 3 random foes rolled; GM chooses 1; enemy at ×½ strength (split across two units)

**Skill Modifier:** None

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −1 | No movement | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Normal | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |

**Notes:**
- Used when a friendly unit is in trouble; your unit adds its strength to their defense
- Enemy is at ×½ because they must fight two units
- Lower Triumph bonus (−1) since kill is shared
- This is the defensive equivalent of Attack with Another

---

### Dropped Rock (Wall Defense)

**Prerequisite:** Unit is defending a wall or elevated position (castle walls, ramparts, cliff tops)

**Opponents:** Any attacking unit climbing up

**Skill Modifier:** +5 to defender (height advantage); −15 to attacker (climbing with ladders)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −2 | No movement | Attackers repulsed |
| **Win** | 0 | Normal | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Attackers gain a foothold | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Attackers breach the position | — |

**Notes:**
- Specific to siege and prepared defensive scenarios
- Attackers must use ladders to climb walls ≥10 ft. (−15 to their skill)
- High walls also prevent normal melee except from ladders; requires Assault maneuver from attackers
- Full terrain modifiers in `07-battle-misc.md`

## RETREAT MANEUVERS

### Pull Back

**Prerequisite:** Unit must be Engaged (in combat); UC must have at least a Partial Success on Battle roll

**Opponents:** 3 random foes; GM chooses 1

**Skill Modifier:** −5 to all player weapon skills (fighting while retreating)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −2 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- The primary tactical retreat option when Engaged
- On Triumph or Win: unit successfully disengages; starts next round Disengaged (can Charge again)
- On Loss: involuntary Recoil (Disordered next round)
- On Crush: unit Breaks — all members become Alone
- Requires Partial Success minimum — cannot be used on a Failure or Fumble Battle roll

---

### Withdraw

**Prerequisite:** Unit must be Engaged AND UC must win their Battle roll (Success or better)

**Opponents:** 3 random foes; GM chooses 1

**Skill Modifier:** +5 to foe's combat skill (they get a free shot at the withdrawing unit); OR −5 to player skill

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −2 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Recoil 1 Zone | — |

**Notes:**
- More orderly than Pull Back; requires winning the Battle roll (not just a partial)
- Enemy gets +5 because the withdrawing unit must turn partially away
- Better Loss result than Pull Back (Move Back vs. Recoil — Move Back does not cause Disorder)
- Triumph here reduces Intensity even while retreating — the withdrawal is so smooth it rattles the enemy
- Compare to Pull Back: Withdraw requires a better Battle result but has better Loss/Crush outcomes

---

### Run Away

**Prerequisite:** None (always available; the last resort)

**Opponents:** 3 random foes; player chooses 1 to fight while fleeing

**Skill Modifier:** None (but Horsemanship roll may be required)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | 0 | Move Back 1 or 2 Zones; Disengaged | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Loss** | +2 | Broken | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- No Intensity reduction possible — this is purely survival
- On Triumph: player chooses to move back 1 or 2 zones; both result in Disengaged
- On Win: Recoil (Disordered next round), but unit survives as a unit
- On Loss or Crush: unit Breaks — members become Alone
- Available on any Battle roll result (even Failure/Fumble); the fallback option
- An Alone knight should use Run Away to escape the battle entirely

---

### Sprint

**Prerequisite:** Unit is marked with "S" in the enemy table (sprinters); this is for ENEMY units using Sprint — player knights may Sprint only if they have the Sprint ability listed

**Opponents (when enemy Sprints):** Any non-missile opponent that attacks them gets +5

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** (enemy sprinting) | 0 | Move Back 2 Zones; Disengaged | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disordered | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Broken | — |
| **Crush** | 0 | Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- Sprint is primarily an enemy capability; players pursue Sprinting enemies with "Run Down Sprinters"
- Sprinting units fight at ×½ in melee while sprinting
- Sprint DEX value in unit table: the enemy must roll under their listed DEX to successfully sprint that round

---

### Flee Fighting (Shoot and Scoot)

**Prerequisite:** Unit is marked "F" in enemy table (Flee Fighting ability); unit must have missile weapons AND be mounted

**Opponents:** Any non-missile horse unit that attacks them

**Skill Modifier:** +5 to the attacking horse unit (they're chasing)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disengaged | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Move Back 1 Zone; Disordered; Disengaged | — |
| **Crush** | +1 | Move Back 2 Zones; Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- Flee Fighting is primarily an enemy tactic (Faris, Byzantine cavalry, etc.)
- The unit shoots missiles while retreating; they fire at the pursuing unit during the missile phase
- "Flee = XX" in unit tables: the listed DEX/score is their movement rate; must roll under it to successfully flee each round
- Player units do NOT normally have this ability unless specifically noted in their unit composition

---

### Turn Around

**Prerequisite:** Enemy has gotten behind the player unit (Attack Enemy Rear is being used against players, or unit is surrounded)

**Opponents:** The enemy attacking from the rear

**Skill Modifier:** +10 to enemy (attacking rear); −10 to players (turning to face rear threat)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −1 | No movement; threat eliminated | — |
| **Win** | 0 | Normal; threat reduced | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Recoil 1 Zone; Disordered | — |
| **Crush** | +2 | Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- Used to counter an Attack Enemy Rear by an enemy unit
- The initial round of rear attack gives enemy +10; after turning, bonuses normalize
- On Triumph, threat is eliminated and unit repositions to face forward again

## OUT-OF-COMBAT MANEUVERS

These maneuvers apply when a unit is in Non-combat Zones (1–3 for players, 7–9 for enemy) or is Disengaged and choosing not to fight.

### Remain Disengaged

**Prerequisite:** Unit is currently Disengaged

**Opponents:** No foes — no melee

**Result:** No movement; no Intensity change; unit remains Disengaged next round; may perform a Disengaged action (seek a friend, seek a target, prepare for Charge)

**Notes:**
- Allows the unit to rest, reorganize, treat wounds, or wait for a better opportunity
- Disengaged units can seek specific targets (roll Heraldry or Recognize skill to find a named enemy)
- Disengaged units may attempt to Rescue a Friend (see `06-battle-aftermath.md`)

---

### Remove from Battle (Self)

**Prerequisite:** Unit is in a Non-combat Zone (Zone 1–3) OR is Disengaged

**Opponents:** No foes — no melee (unless enemies pursue)

**Result:** Move 1 or 2 Zones toward the rear; Disengaged next round; unit is effectively out of the battle

**Notes:**
- Used to remove seriously wounded knights, escort prisoners, or recover squires
- No Glory is earned in the round of removal
- If enemies are pursuing (Pursue maneuver against retreating army), unit may face opponents even in Non-combat Zones

---

### Seek (Specific Target or Friend)

**Prerequisite:** Unit is Disengaged; UC rolls Heraldry or Recognize (or Awareness in chaotic battles)

**Opponents:** No foes — no melee

**Result (skill roll):**
- **Success:** Find the target (specific enemy commander, lost friend, lost squire)
- **Failure:** Cannot locate this round; may try again next round
- **Critical:** Find the target AND determine their exact zone and condition

**Notes:**
- Used specifically to locate a named target for Fight Enemy Leader, Rescue a Friend, etc.
- Not a maneuver per se — it's a Disengaged action that doesn't require a Battle roll
- Player may combine Seeking with Remain Disengaged in the same round

## Rally the Battalion

**Prerequisite:** Army Intensity is greater than 30 (desperate situation)

**Opponents:** ×3 random foes in melee AND 1 random missile unit simultaneously

**Skill Modifier:** None (but fight four threats)

| Result | Army Intensity | Zone Movement | Other |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **Triumph** | −20 | No movement | — |
| **Win** | −10 | No movement | — |
| **Loss** | 0 | Broken | — |
| **Crush** | 0 | Broken | — |

**Notes:**
- Extremely high stakes; available only when Army Intensity > 30
- A Triumph here can swing the entire battle (−20 is massive)
- Requires a UC Full Success on Battle roll (minimum) to attempt
- If the unit Breaks on a Loss/Crush, this catastrophically fails and the battalion may rout
- Knights must fight ×3 melee opponents AND a missile unit in the same round; use Dividing Attacks rules

## Recoil vs. Move Back vs. Retreat: Key Distinctions

| Term | Cause | Next Round Effect |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Recoil** | Involuntary; result of Loss/Crush | Unit starts next round **Disordered** (+5 UI, −10 melee) |
| **Move Back** | Controlled; result of certain Win outcomes | No penalty next round |
| **Retreat** | Any backward zone movement | Umbrella term; may be voluntary or involuntary |
| **Rout** | Army Intensity ≤ 0 for 2 rounds | Entire army collapses; Chase phase begins |
| **Broken** | Unit result = Crush in a critical situation | All members are now **Alone** in battle |
