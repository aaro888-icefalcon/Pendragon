### Aquitanian Notes

**Caussards (entry 11):** Shepherds from the Causses rocky plateau. When slinging, all of them target the same unit leader. Their Sprint value (DEX = 17) means they are extremely fast on foot.

**Almogavers (entry 13):** Savage mountain fighters with Hate (Horsemen) 20. Their Hate is always active. They attack horse first, not rider, when fighting mounted knights.

**Visigoth Leaders:** Use Visigoth Leaders table (below) before year 500. After 500, use Generic Noble Leaders.

### Byzantine Army Notes

**Klibanarii (entry 3):** Very heavily armored front and sides only. Their rear and top are vulnerable: any Attack Enemy Rear or Ambush against them gives −5 to their armor (effective AP reduced by 5 from behind).

**Vardariots (entry 8):** Turkish cavalry settled in the Balkans, hired by Byzantium. They prefer Flee Fighting but will engage in melee. Their Battle skill is Tactics (22), used by their UC.

**Drum Band (entry 18):** When the band is present and playing, the Byzantine army gets −5 to its Core Army Intensity modifier (a standing army bonus). If the band is attacked and "silenced" (Win against them), the bonus is lost. Full Glory (25) is only awarded if the knight laughs loudly every time he recounts the story.

**Cultural Specialty: Tactics.** Byzantine armies use the Tactics skill in place of Battle for their Unit Commander. Byzantine UC Battle values are often 20+ reflecting their superior military training.
