### Saxon Army Notes

**Berserkers:** Never surrender; cannot be ransomed; fight to the death. Their armor is None — they tear it off in battle frenzy. A berserker who takes a Major Wound does NOT stop fighting until the end of that round.

**Cultural Specialty: 2-H Weapon.** All Saxon two-handed axe and great spear attacks use the 2-H Weapon specialty. These weapons cannot be used with a shield.

**Mounted Thegns:** Post-500 development; some Saxon leaders adopted mounted combat. Before 500, reroll this result.

### Danish Army Notes

**Ulfhednar (Wolfmen):** Shapeshifters who transform into wolf-human hybrids. Treat as berserkers: no armor possible; cannot be captured; fight with savage ferocity. Their Hate (All humans) includes allies.

**Varangians:** These Danes serve in the Byzantine army and bring that army's discipline. Determined (D) — will not flee. If fighting alongside Byzantines, they function as Byzantine units for maneuver purposes.

**Hrafnsmerki:** The royal Danish war-banner unit. Each member carries a portion of the Dannebrog standard. Killing any of them earns +10 Glory.

### Routier Notes

Routiers have no ransom value — they are commoners with weapons. None may be captured for ransom. "Kill them all" is the only viable option; offers of money to buy them off will result in the town being pillaged anyway.

Killing the Routier Leader gives −20 to Army Intensity immediately (reflected in the Glory entry).

## Five Renaissance Troops (Anachronistic Optional)

*Available only if GM permits anachronistic forces. Primarily post-Camlann or exotic scenarios.*

| Note | Opponent | Combat Information | Armor | MW | Glory |

| 1 | Pike & Halberd (Swiss Reisläufer) << Hate (Landsknecht) 20 | Halberd (20): 4d6; Pike (22): 4d6 | Chain (10) | 12 | 25 |
| 2 | Spear & Arbalest D | Arbalest (17): 1d6+10; Spear (12): 5d6 | 5; Quilt (4), Helm (1) | 13 | 25 |
| 3 | Arbalest & Pavise | Arbalest (15): 1d6+10; Pavise: −10 to attacker | 5; Quilt (4), Helm (1) | 13 | 10 |
| 4 | Landsknecht ×2 << Hate (Swiss Reisläufer) 20 | Pike (20): 4d6 | Chain (10) | 12 | 25 |
| 5 | Hand Cannon | Kaboom (7): 6d6; Horsemanship −10 | 5; Quilt (4), Helm (1) | 12 | 15 |

**Renaissance Notes:**

1. **Swiss Reisläufer:** Available c. 548. Pikes (Great Spear skill) in rear, halberdiers in front. Both attack the same target in the same round. Both are two-handed: take 1d6 damage through any result.

2. **Spear & Arbalest:** On attack, roll 1d6 for each arbalestier; 5–6 = not ready (no shot). Otherwise fires normally. Defending infantry cover the shooters.

3. **Arbalest & Pavise:** Same fire rules as above. Pavise grants −10 to attacker.

4. **Landsknecht:** Two-to-one odds represented by ×2. Their long pikes reach over the front ranks.

5. **Hand Cannon:** Scares horses (Horsemanship −10 to enemy); sometimes hurts people. Referenced in late Malory. The gun mentioned in the final chapters of the saga.
