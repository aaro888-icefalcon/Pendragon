# Battle Aftermath

All post-battle procedures: determining the victor, Glory calculation (by battle size, result, and role), loot, ransom tables, squire recovery, follower losses and return, wound treatment, and Extended Melee Phase rules for Rescue, Prisoner, Fight Enemy Leader, and Rally.

**Use when:** The battle has ended (Intensity thresholds met, scripted end, or all 5+ rounds resolved), or during battle for Extended Melee Phase Special Actions.

---

## After the Battle: Sequence

1. Determine the Victor
2. Calculate Total Glory
3. Distribute Loot (Decisive Victory only)
4. Calculate Ransom
5. Find Lost Squires
6. Recover Lost Followers
7. Perform Healing

---

## 1. Determine the Victor

| Outcome | Condition |
|---------|-----------|
| **Decisive Victory** | Enemy army Intensity reached 0 for 2 consecutive rounds; enemy army fled the field entirely |
| **Indecisive Victory** | Enemy retreated (Intensity ≤10 for 2 rounds) but was not destroyed; players held the field |
| **Indecisive Defeat** | Player army retreated from the field under pressure; enemy holds |
| **Decisive Defeat** | Player army Intensity reached 40+; routed from the field |

**Note:** Many scenarios specify the outcome regardless of Intensity (Scripted End); GPC battles often have predetermined results that the players' actions can influence but not fully control.

**Position of Honor:** The unit at the rightmost, frontmost position (Position of Honor) earns +5 to its Unit Intensity (more Glory, higher risk). This is declared during setup.

---

## 2. Calculate Total Glory

### Base Glory Per Round

Each round, a knight earns:
**(Enemy Base Glory) × (Result Multiplier) × (Critical Multiplier)**

| Personal Result | Multiplier |
|----------------|-----------|
| Win | ×1 |
| Tie | ×1 |
| Loss | ×½ |
| Critical personal roll (knight) | ×2 additional |
| Inactive / unable to fight but present | ×½ |

### Battle Size Glory Modifier

Applied once to total round Glory at the end of the battle:

| Battle Size | Knight Count | Glory Modifier |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Clash | 10–50 knights | No modifier |
| Battle | 100–500 knights | +50% to total round Glory |
| Campaign | 1000+ knights | +100% to total round Glory |

### Victory Modifier

Applied to the knight's total Glory for the battle:

| Victory Type | Glory Modifier |
|-------------|----------------|
| Decisive Victory | ×2 |
| Indecisive Victory | ×1 (no change) |
| Indecisive Defeat | ×½ |
| Decisive Defeat | ×0 (no Glory from combat) |

### Unit Commander Bonus Glory

Awarded at the end of the battle for leading a unit:

| Battle Size | UC Glory Bonus |
|-------------|----------------|
| Small (Clash) | +15 |
| Medium (Battle) | +20 |
| Large (Campaign) | +30 |

### Glory for Specific Achievements

| Achievement | Glory |
|-------------|-------|
| Position of Honor (player unit held it) | +5 to total |
| First into enemy camp | +25 |
| Killing a Battalion Commander | +100 (per combatant present in that round) |
| Killing the Army Commander | +150 (per combatant present in that round) |
| Multiple opponents (×2, ×3 enemies) | Full Glory for each opponent defeated |
| Fighting as Reserve (and winning) | ×½ Glory (enemies at ×½ strength) |
| Squire killed an enemy | No additional Glory to the knight |

### Leader's Battle Record Summary

Total Glory per knight = (Sum of per-round Glory) × Battle Size Modifier × Victory Modifier + UC Bonus + Achievement bonuses

## 3. Loot

Loot is available **only on Decisive Victory** (enemy camp overrun).

### First in Camp

- The first player knight to reach the enemy camp (Zone 9) earns +25 Glory
- The knight must make a Remove from Battle maneuver or follow a routed enemy

### Loot Value

| Enemy Army Quality | Loot per Player Knight |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Poor/Tribal | 1d6 × £1 |
| Standard | 2d6 × £1 |
| Rich/Well-equipped | 3d6 × £1 |
| Royal army | 4d6 × £1 |

- Loot is divided equally among all knights who participated in the decisive victory
- Loot from the camp includes equipment, supplies, and minor valuables
- Major treasures (banners, royal regalia) are specified by the scenario

## 4. Ransom

Any enemy unit or leader marked with **£** may be taken prisoner for ransom (if not killed outright).

### Ransom Roll

After the battle, for each captured £ unit member:

Roll 1d20:

| d20 | Ransom Result |
|-----|--------------|
| 01–05 | Prisoner escapes or is killed in the chaos; no ransom |
| 06–10 | Ransom offered: £1d6 × 5 libra |
| 11–15 | Ransom offered: £2d6 × 5 libra |
| 16–19 | Ransom offered: £3d6 × 5 libra |
| 20 | Ransom offered: £4d6 × 5 libra |

**Knight prisoners:** ×2 multiplier to the ransom result
**Leader prisoners (Battalion/Army Commander):** ×5 multiplier; minimum £100 libra

### Non-Knightly Ransoms (Optional)

Common soldiers and non-noble captives generally have no ransom value. Exceptions:
- Rich merchants or burghers caught with the army: roll ransom table ×½
- Clergy or officials: negotiated ransom at GM discretion

### Holding a Prisoner

- A knight with a prisoner is **Encumbered** (−5 Horsemanship, −5 melee)
- Knight may hand prisoner to squire or retinue; squire is then at −5 Squire roll
- Prisoners are held at the knight's manor or castle; may attempt escape (GM discretion, typically 1d6 months)

---

## 5. Find Lost Squires

If a squire was lost during battle (Squire roll failed), the knight may search for them after the battle.

**Squire Recovery Roll** (after battle, for each lost squire):

Roll 1d20 vs. the knight's Awareness skill:

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Critical Success | Squire found unharmed and immediately available |
| Success | Squire found; requires 1d6 days to recover from minor wounds |
| Failure | Squire not found; may be dead, captured, or lost |
| Fumble | Squire confirmed dead or permanently lost |

**Alternative:** Knight may roll Loyalty (Squire) if available; same table.

### Squire Retreat Results (During Battle)

When a Squire roll fails during a battle round:

| d20 | Squire Fate |
|-----|------------|
| 01–05 | Squire is killed (horse crushed him, wound was fatal) |
| 06–10 | Squire is badly wounded; cannot return this battle |
| 11–15 | Squire was knocked out; recovers after battle (found on Recovery roll) |
| 16–18 | Squire ran away; returns after battle ashamed |
| 19–20 | Squire made it out; returns next round with replacement horse/lance |

---

## 6. Recover Lost Followers

Followers lost during battle may partially return after the battle.

### Follower Return Table

Roll d20 and add modifiers:

| d20 Roll | Followers Returned |
|----------|--------------------|
| 1–4 | None return |
| 5–8 | ¼ of lost followers return |
| 9–12 | ½ of lost followers return |
| 13–16 | ¾ of lost followers return |
| 17–20 | All lost followers return |

**Modifiers:**
- Bodyguard Bonus was used: −10 to the roll (those followers sacrificed themselves)
- Decisive Victory: +5 to the roll
- Decisive Defeat: −5 to the roll
- Knight treated survivors well (paid wages, arranged first aid): +2

**Followers who do not return:** They are dead, captured, or permanently incapacitated.

**Loyalty Roll:** After losing followers, the Unit Commander rolls Loyalty (Vassals). On failure, the knight suffers Melancholy for 1 week per follower permanently lost.

---

## 7. Healing

- Standard KAP healing rules apply after the battle
- Healers (Chirurgeon or Leechcraft skill) available in camp can treat wounds
- Knights who took Major Wounds during battle must roll Chirurgy to stabilize; failure = death
- Family healers: if knight's family is present, +5 to healing rolls

## Extended Melee Phases (Special Actions)

Extended Melee Phases occur at the end of normal melee (Step 5D), before Army Intensity adjustment. Each is a separate round of combat within the main battle round.

### Prerequisites for Any Special Action

- Unit must have achieved at least a **Win** in the normal melee phase (cannot enter Extended Melee on a Loss or Crush)
- Exception: Rescue a Friend can be attempted on any result if the friend is in immediate danger

---

### Special Action: Rescue a Friend

**Prerequisite:** A player knight or NPC ally is Alone (unhorsed and in danger) in the same or adjacent zone

**Procedure:**
1. Knight attempting rescue must be mounted
2. Rescuer fights the enemies surrounding the downed knight:
   - Roll d20 on enemy table; fight 1d3 opponents
   - Apply same zone modifiers; rescuer is at −5 (Burdened/distracted)
3. Downed knight is **Burdened** on the rescuer's horse: −5 Horsemanship to both
4. Extended Melee resolves separately (Win/Loss/Triumph/Crush)

**Rescue Results:**

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Triumph | Friend rescued; no damage to either knight; move back 1 zone |
| Win | Friend rescued; move back 1 zone; both knights at −5 next round |
| Loss | Failed to rescue; rescuer takes damage; friend remains Alone |
| Crush | Both knights in serious danger; both may be captured or killed |

**Remounting a rescued knight:**
- Rescued knight is placed on rescuer's horse (Encumbered: both at −5 Horsemanship)
- Squire may bring a replacement horse; if squire present and roll succeeds, rescued knight can remount next round

---

### Special Action: Take a Prisoner

**Prerequisite:** Target enemy has £ symbol; player must have Won their melee against that target

**Procedure:**
1. After winning normal melee against a £ unit, player declares intent to capture (not kill)
2. Extended Melee round begins: fight the target's **Bodyguards** (1d3 opponents from Generic Noble Leaders table, Bodyguard entry)
3. Also fight the target themselves (at ×½ since already defeated once)

**Prisoner Results:**

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Triumph | Prisoner captured; no losses |
| Win | Prisoner captured; knight takes minor damage |
| Loss | Target escapes; knight takes damage |
| Crush | Target escapes; knight is at risk of capture themselves |

**Holding:** Knight now has prisoner; see Ransom rules above.

---

### Special Action: Fight an Enemy Leader

**Prerequisite:** Opportunity result 19–20 (from UC Critical Success), OR unit has reached Zone 7+ and GM determines leader is accessible

**Procedure:**
1. Unit must first fight through **Bodyguards:** 1d3+1 Bodyguards from the Generic Noble Leaders table (or specific army's leader table)
2. After Bodyguards, fight the Leader themselves

**Bodyguard Phase:**

| Result vs. Bodyguards | Outcome |
|-----------------------|---------|
| Triumph | All bodyguards defeated; face Leader with +5 bonus |
| Win | Some bodyguards defeated; face Leader normally |
| Loss | Bodyguards hold; cannot reach Leader this round |
| Crush | Driven back; unit takes damage; Disordered |

**Leader Phase (if Bodyguards defeated):**
- Use Leader's stats from the relevant army table (see `05-battle-events-tables.md` Generic Noble Leaders, or armies files)
- Player knights fight Leader individually (no unit resolution — each player chooses a target: leader or bodyguard)
- Killing/capturing the Leader: −10 Army Intensity immediately; bonus Glory (see table above)

---

### Special Action: Killing Commanders

Any commander (Battalion or Army) may be killed or captured:
- **Battalion Commander killed:** −10 to Army Intensity; +100 Glory to all knights in that combat round
- **Army Commander killed:** −15 to Army Intensity (plus automatic "general retreat" check); +150 Glory to all in that combat round
- If captured (£ symbol on leader): apply standard ransom rules

---

### Special Action: Rally the Battalion

**Prerequisite:** Army Intensity > 30 (battle is going badly); Unit Commander must have achieved a Full Success (Critical) on Battle roll

**Procedure:**
1. Unit faces ×3 random foes in melee AND 1 random missile unit simultaneously
2. This is a desperate stand; no bonuses from any source can reduce Intensity below 30 during this round
3. Use Dividing Attacks rules for fighting multiple opponents

**Rally Results:**

| Result | Army Intensity | Other |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Triumph | −20 | Dramatic reversal; narrative impact |
| Win | −10 | Significant improvement |
| Loss | 0; unit Broken | Catastrophic failure |
| Crush | 0; unit Broken | Complete rout |

---

### Extended Melee: General Rules

- Each Extended Melee round costs one round's time within the battle
- All Extended Melee results contribute to the unit's final Unit Results tally for that battle round
- Glory earned in Extended Melee rounds uses the enemy commander/bodyguard Glory values (typically 50 for Bodyguards, 100–150 for Commanders)
- A knight may fight multiple Extended Melee rounds (e.g., fight Bodyguards then Commander) but each round of Extended Melee costs an additional Squire roll

## Captured Horses Table

When a Squire succeeds their roll AND the enemy unit has ♞ (mounted), roll d20:

| d20 | Captured Horse |
|-----|---------------|
| 01–05 | No horse captured (enemy retained theirs) |
| 06–08 | Rouncy (4d6 damage) captured |
| 09–11 | War Pony (5d6) captured |
| 12–14 | Charger (6d6) captured |
| 15–17 | Large Charger (7d6) captured |
| 18–19 | Courser (6d6, fast) captured |
| 20 | Destrier or special horse (per enemy table entry) captured |

**Disposition of captured horse:**
- Knight may keep it (adds to stable)
- Squire may guide it (−5 Squire roll next round)
- Knight may release it (no benefit)
- Squire who captures a horse earns the knight additional glory at GM's discretion

## Retinue Survival Roll

For non-combatant retinue members (personal attendants, servants) who accompanied the army:

After the battle, roll d20 vs. the knight's Loyalty (Household) or a flat roll of 10:
- Success: All retinue members present and accounted for
- Failure: 1d3 retinue members are missing (dead, captured, or fled)
- Decisive Defeat: −5 penalty to this roll (chaos of the rout)

---

## Post-Battle Passions

After a battle, the following Passion checks may be required:

| Situation | Passion Check |
|-----------|--------------|
| Close companion killed in battle | Loyalty (Friend); failure = Melancholy (1d6 weeks) |
| Followers permanently lost | Loyalty (Vassals); failure = Melancholy (1 week per follower) |
| Battle was a Decisive Defeat | Loyalty (Lord) or Loyalty (King); failure = −1 to that Passion |
| Knight personally distinguished himself | Relevant Passion increases by 1 point |
| Knight fled (Run Away result) when unit fought on | Honor check (GM discretion); possible −1 to Honor |

```

├─ 1. Determine Victor (Decisive/Indecisive, Victory/Defeat)
├─ 2. Calculate Total Glory
│   ├─ Sum per-round Glory
│   ├─ Apply Battle Size Modifier
│   ├─ Apply Victory Modifier
│   ├─ Add UC Bonus
│   └─ Add Achievement bonuses
├─ 3. Loot (Decisive Victory only)
├─ 4. Ransom (for each £ prisoner)
├─ 5. Find Lost Squires
│   └─ Roll Awareness for each
├─ 6. Recover Lost Followers
│   ├─ Roll Followers Return Table
│   └─ Loyalty (Vassals) check if deaths occurred

    └─ Standard KAP Chirurgy rules
```
