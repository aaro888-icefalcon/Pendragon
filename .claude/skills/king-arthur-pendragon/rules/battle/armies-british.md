# British and Logres Armies

Complete stat blocks for all British/Logres army tables from the Book of Armies: All-Knight Army, Anarchy Period armies, Boy King armies (Malahaut, Lothian), Conquest Period Logres Army, Romance Period armies, Late Tournament/Camelot armies, and the Battle of Camlann. Includes all period leaders and the Seven Special Units.

**Use when:** Fielding British opponents or allied armies; all GPC periods.
