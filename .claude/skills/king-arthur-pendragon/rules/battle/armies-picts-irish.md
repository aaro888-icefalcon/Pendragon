### Irish Army Notes

**Chariot Warriors (entry 10):** Irish chariot use is archaic but present in early period (pre-500). The chariot acts as a mobile platform; treat as mounted for movement but the warrior fights on foot. After leaping from the chariot, they attack as infantry with Javelin first, then Great Spear.

**Fanatics:** These warriors fight until dead. They are never captured (no £). ×1d3 means roll 1d3 to determine how many attack the player knight simultaneously.

**Kerns MM:** Use missile (javelin) first round, then close for knife combat. Both attacks occur in the same round; player must deal with both.

**Cultural Specialty: Javelin.** Most Irish units carry javelins regardless of their primary weapon listing. Javelins are thrown before closing into melee.

### Pict Army Notes

**Cultural Specialty: Stalk.** Pict units using Stalk reduce the enemy's Awareness roll to detect them. The Awareness penalty is listed in the unit entry (−5, −8). This applies before the battle begins and in ambush scenarios. During normal battle rounds, Stalk provides +5 to Pict melee on the first round of contact only.

**Ambush:** When Picts set an ambush, the ambush modifiers apply AND all Pict units get +5 to melee in the first round. See `07-battle-misc.md` for ambush rules.

**Painted/Naked Warriors:** Their terrifying appearance may trigger a Fear response in the player's horse. GM may call for Horsemanship roll when first engaged.

### Peasant Army Notes

**Cowardly Peasants:** Many entries are S (Sprinters) — they flee rather than fight. Roll 1d6 before melee; on 1–2, they sprint instead of fighting (no opponent; player rolls Run Down Sprinters or stops). No Glory for sprinters who escape.

**Determined Peasants ×2, ×3:** Despite their fear, they are so numerous that they swarm. The ×2 or ×3 opponents rule applies.

**Peasant Leaders:** Peasant armies rarely have trained leaders. Use the "Town Guard Captain" (equivalent to Young Commander from Generic Noble Leaders but at Battle: 10, Combat skills at 12) or a minor noble knight.

### Faerie Army Notes

**Spriggans (entry 03):** These faeries begin small but grow to enormous size as a battle progresses. Start at MW 12; after each round of combat, MW increases by 2 (to 14, 16, etc., maximum 22). Their apparent size increases; their combat skill remains unchanged.

**Undead (entries 11–12):** Undead warriors do not feel pain. They ignore Major Wounds until they reach 0 HP. They are Determined (D) and never retreat. No ransom.

**Faerie Ransom (£):** Faerie ransom is paid in Faerie gifts (magical items, favors, promises) rather than mundane currency. GM determines what a captured Faerie lord or knight offers.

**Faerie Passions:** Many Faerie units have strong Passions not listed (Loyalty to their Lord, Love of their realm). GM may invoke these at any time without rolling.
