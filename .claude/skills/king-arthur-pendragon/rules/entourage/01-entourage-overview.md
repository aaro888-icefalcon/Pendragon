# Entourage Overview

Defines the entourage system, follower categories, upkeep costs, improvement mechanics, and the Glory-from-retinue rule.

**Use when:** A player wants to know what followers are possible, how improvement works, what upkeep costs apply, or how much Glory retinue employment generates.

## The Three Layers of a Knight's Social Sphere

1. **Center:** The knight himself.
2. **Inner ring (Supporters):** Squire, Wife. Closest relationship; most game mechanics.
3. **Retinue:** Famuli (manor-based staff) + Entourage (travel with the lord).

The **entourage** specifically refers to those who travel with the knight. The **famuli** remain at the manor when the lord is away.

## Pay Grade Reference

| Grade | Examples | Annual Upkeep |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| Laborer | Field worker, digger | £0.25 |
| Assistant / Personal servant | Lady's maid, groom | £0.50 |
| Skilled craftsman | Carpenter, smith | £0.75 |
| Professional | Chaplain, physician, herald | £1.00 |
| Senior professional | Physician (licensed), master herald | £2.00–£3.00 |

All upkeep figures are annual. The knight pays this from his discretionary or court budget.

## Glory from Retinue

**Rule:** For every £1 spent on retinue upkeep per year, the knight gains **1 Glory**.

**Maximum:** 100 Glory per year from retinue spending (regardless of how much is spent above £100).

**What counts:** All paid followers — servants, soldiers, professionals. Does not include the knight's own expenses or immediate family's maintenance.

**Example:** A knight pays £12 in retinue upkeep this year. He gains 12 Glory from retinue (capped at 100 if total were over 100).

## Follower Improvement

Followers improve their skills during the Winter Phase according to the following rules:

| Current Skill Value | Improvement Rule | Roll |
|--------------------|-----------------|------|
| 15 or lower | Automatic +1 per year | None required |
| 16–20 | Roll 1d6; improve on a 6 | Roll each year |
| 21 or higher | Roll 1d20; improve on a 20 | Roll each year |

**When to apply:** Step 7 (Training and Practice) of the Winter Phase. Apply to each follower independently.

**Which skills improve:** Only skills the follower has used during the year, or skills the knight has specifically arranged training for. The GM may limit improvement to relevant skills.

## Upkeep Failure

If the knight cannot pay a follower's upkeep:

1. **Servants:** Depart immediately (no loyalty check). No Honor loss unless a long-serving officer.
2. **Supporters (Squire):** Make Loyalty (Lord) check. Success = stays despite hardship. Failure = departs (honor effects: see `02-entourage-members.md`).
3. **Wife:** She cannot simply leave (social reality), but Love (Family) passion may decrease.

## Household vs. Entourage Distinction

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Household** | All persons dependent on the knight, including those at the manor |
| **Famuli** | The manor-based staff; stay home when the lord travels |
| **Entourage** | Those who travel with the lord when he leaves the manor |
| **Mesnie** | The military component; knights, soldiers, cavalry |

A typical knight travelling to court takes: his squire, 1–2 servants, and perhaps his wife. The rest of the household remains at the manor.

---

## Retinue and Standard of Living

The knight's Standard of Living (as defined in the core rules) sets the baseline for household quality. The entourage rules provide specifics on:
- Who must be maintained as Supporters (squire, wife)
- The cost of optional additional personnel
- How additional squires work (not free; £1 each per year)
