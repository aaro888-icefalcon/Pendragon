# Uther Period: 485–495

> **NOTE:** The separate Book of Uther (campaign/uther/) takes precedence for 480–495; this file is the GPC's leaner version — use it for events Book of Uther lacks.

---

## Period Overview

**Situation:** Uther Pendragon rules Logres as High King, but authority is contested. Saxon kingdoms press from the east and north. The northern British kings (Malahaut, Lothian) are reluctant vassals. Merlin operates as kingmaker in the background.

**Themes:** Legitimacy, Saxon wars, treachery at court, Merlin's schemes leading to Arthur's birth.

**Default player knight home:** Salisbury, vassal to Earl Roderick.

**Key NPCs this period:**
- King Uther (Battle 20) — High King of Logres; ill from 492 onward
- Earl Roderick of Salisbury — liege lord of player knights
- Merlin — wizard advisor; operating covertly
- Duke Gorlois of Cornwall — vassal; will die at Terrabil (490)
- Queen Ygraine — Gorlois's wife; Uther's obsession; Arthur's mother
- Prince Madoc — Uther's son; leads naval raids; dies at Terrabil
- Duke Ulfius of Silchester — loyal ally of Uther
- Sir Brastias — marshal; survives the poisoned feast

## 485 — First Year of Service

**Situation:** Player knights begin active service as newly made knights under Earl Roderick. Saxons are raiding the eastern counties. Uther's court is establishing itself.

**Court:** Held at Sarum. Uther visits periodically.

**Events:**
- Player knights assigned to garrison duty and riding patrol around Salisbury county border
- Saxons raiding Caercolun in the east; Duke Lucius leads army against them
- War in southern Caercolun; Duke Lucius ambushed near Ipswich and killed; Saxon advance contained by Brastias

**Adventure: SWORD LAKE**
- Hook: While on patrol, an old man (actually Merlin in disguise) asks player knights to retrieve his wandered goat from a nearby hill
- Scene 1: Knights climb hill; pursue the goat into scattered woodland; a three-eyed giant appears holding the goat
- Scene 2: Fight the giant; Merlin (revealed as the old man) walks to a lakeside barge while knights hold the creature
- Scene 3: A nukalavee (horse-man creature, slimy green) charges from the brush at Merlin; knights must stop it
- **Nukalavee:** SIZ 28, STR 22, DEX 10; cannot be shaken from ride form by damage; leaves water trail; one arm per knight attacking; tries to get past knights to reach Merlin; dies when killed; melts to green slime
- **Three-Eyed Giant:** Glory 100; SIZ 30, DEX 7, STR 24, CON 25; Armor 15; HP 55; Damage 9d6; Small Tree 18, Thrown Boulder 12 (7d6), Grapple 15 (4d6)
- Outcome: Merlin retrieves Excalibur from the Lady of the Lake; players see him take it but not what it is
- **Glory:** 50 for helping Merlin; divide giant-kill Glory among combatants

**Christmas Court:**
- Royal: Uther unhappy; checking weapon production; receiving spies from continent
- Salisbury: Roderick pessimistic about Caercolun losses but realistic
- **Winter Phase Modifier:** Caerwent — land was raided (+1d6)

**If the player does nothing:** Saxons continue raiding Caercolun regardless; Duke Lucius is killed and Brastias contains the advance without Salisbury's involvement. Absent knights gain no Glory and miss Merlin's retrieval of Excalibur from the lake.

## 487 — Uther Visits Salisbury

**Situation:** Uther brings full court to Sarum for Crown Wearing. He has Excalibur and is planning to bring recalcitrant northern lords to heel.

**Court:** Held at Sarum. King Uther, Prince Madoc, Duke Ulfius, Duke Brastias, large noble retinue present.

**Events:**

**Adventure: GREAT SWORD FEAST**
- Setting: Christmas Feast at Sarum Castle; all knights present
- Events: Gift-giving ceremony; Prince Madoc presents war booty (chests of silver, gold, silks, jewels)
- Uther gives player knights £1 each (more if Glory warrants)
- Merlin arrives dramatically; presents Excalibur to Uther publicly — "For the High King" — and names it aloud
- If knights who helped in Sword Lake are present: Merlin tells Roderick "Watch these men well, and give them rein to help Britain"
- Earl asks player knights to publicly recount the Sword Lake adventure; Orate or Singing roll (Compose first for +5/+10 modifier)
- **Glory:** 50 for public presentation to High King

**Player Hook — A Choice:** Uther wants to visit Duke Lindsey at Lincoln to shore up allegiance. Player knights can:
- **Option A:** Accompany Uther to Lincoln (LINDSEY EMBASSY)
- **Option B:** Join Prince Madoc's naval raids (NAVAL RAIDS)

**Adventure: LINDSEY EMBASSY**
- Setting: Lincoln; Duke Lindsey is reluctant vassal, afraid Uther is a tyrant
- Player knights tell the Sword Lake story at feast; Merlin then produces Excalibur and tells its history
- Lindsey's attitude warms; he is impressed despite himself
- Option extension: Uther sends players as messengers to King Heraut of Malahaut (Eburacum)
- **Glory:** 15 for witnessing Lindsey's submission; 10 for delivering king's message to Malahaut

**Adventure: NAVAL RAIDS**
- Setting: Hantonne harbor; Prince Madoc leads fleet of 100 knights
- Mission: Destroy Saxon ships along the coast; protect sailors going ashore to burn vessels
- Raids at Pevensey (10 Glory), Dover (20 Glory), first naval battle vs. East Saxons (30 Glory), Battle of the Wash (30 Glory)
- Note: Prince Madoc commands; Gwenwynwyn son of Naf is admiral

**Christmas Court:**
- Royal: Uther in better mood; acquired Lindsey's promise; probing Cumbrian lords
- **Winter Phase Modifier:** Sussex, Kent, Essex, Rheged, Catterick — raided (+1d6)

**If the player does nothing:** Lindsey submits to Uther regardless and naval raids proceed under Prince Madoc; absent knights gain no Glory from either embassy or sea battles.

## 488 — Winchester Court / Terrabil

**Situation:** Uther's obsession with Duchess Ygraine of Cornwall drives him to war. Merlin makes his bargain.

**Court:** Held at Winchester.

**Special Guests:** Duke Gorlois of Cornwall; Duchess Ygraine; an Irish king; Heroes of Terrabil afterward

**Gossip:**
- "Don't you think it's frightening that she said her husband visited her that night? The night he died? I'll tell you, it's Merlin's doing!"

**Events:**
- Uther demands Gorlois give him Ygraine; Gorlois takes offense and departs without permission (great insult)
- Uther declares war on Cornwall; musters army; marches to besiege Terrabil Castle
- Simultaneously besieges Tintagel on coast (where Ygraine is sheltered)

**Adventure: BATTLE OF TERRABIL**

Uther's army besieges the castle of Terrabil in Cornwall. Duke Gorlois and his men hold.

- **Length of Battle:** 2 rounds + see below
- **Battle Size:** Medium
- **Commanders:** King Uther (Battle 20) vs. Duke Gorlois (Battle 18)
- **Modifiers:** None
- Before Battle: It is night. Player knights in their unit
- Round 1–2: Normal
- Key Event: Gorlois charges out with a sortie; Prince Madoc is killed in the fighting (Gorlois personally cuts him)
- Player knights may attempt to interpose between Gorlois's bodyguards and Prince Madoc
- Killing the Duke: 250 Glory among any who participate
- Leading the assault into the castle after the duke falls: 250 Glory

Merlin uses magic that night at Tintagel: Uther enters disguised as Gorlois; Ygraine conceives Arthur.

- **After the Battle:** Victor — Decisive Victory (Uther). Gorlois killed in the sortie. Uther "learns" Gorlois is dead, drops the disguise, and marries Ygraine
- **Plunder:** £3 in goods
- **Glory:** 30 per round, plus any from personal combat; x2 for Decisive Victory

**Christmas Court:**
- No Royal (Uther consumed by Ygraine); Salisbury court normal
- **Winter Phase Modifier:** None listed

**If the player does nothing:** Terrabil falls, Gorlois is killed, and Uther marries Ygraine regardless. Absent knights gain no Glory and miss the £3 plunder; Prince Madoc dies without their aid and cannot be saved.

## 489 — A Quiet Year

**Situation:** Uther has married Ygraine. Merlin claims the infant Arthur as agreed. Uther continues consolidating power. Saxons temporarily quieter.

**Court:** Held at London.

**Gossip:** "Oh, by the way, where is that Merlin now?" / "Good riddance to that son of a devil, I say."

**Intrigue:** Merlin takes the newborn Arthur to be fostered with Sir Ector in an obscure manor. Ygraine grieves. Uther does not know where the boy is. Rumors that Merlin cursed the king.

**Events:** Skirmishing in Caercolun; no major battles. Earl Roderick's knights continue patrol duty.

**Christmas Court:**
- Roderick discusses politics with his knights
- **Winter Phase Modifier:** None

**If the player does nothing:** Skirmishing in Caercolun continues without Salisbury's involvement; no Glory is at stake this year and no consequences fall on absent knights.

## 490 — Arthur Conceived / Born

*Note: Arthur was actually conceived at Terrabil (488/489); born 490 or shortly after. The GPC is flexible. This year is quiet in the GPC's framework.*

**Situation:** Uther rules firmly but is beginning to show signs of illness. Ygraine is at court. The child Arthur is fostered secretly.

**Events:** Routine service; patrols; local adventures (use Uther Period Adventures section below).

**If the player does nothing:** Uther rules in quiet decline; Arthur is secretly fostered with Ector. No significant events demand a knight's presence this year; absence carries no direct penalty.

## 491–494 — Middle Uther Years

**Situation:** Uther's health declines. Saxons press harder in Caercolun and the north. The king is increasingly bedridden but refuses to name a successor publicly.

**Court varies:** London (491, 493), Winchester (492), Sarum (494).

**Events Pattern:** Each year:
- Gossip about Uther's health and the succession question
- One or two Saxon raiding events in eastern counties
- Local adventure opportunities (use Uther Period Adventures)
- Winter modifiers: Caercolun, Hertford, Huntington frequently raided

**Key Event — Year 492 (approximate):** Uther is carried on a litter to battle. His presence inspires the army even in his weakness.

**Key NPC Changes:** Brastias becomes de facto military commander. Ulfius manages court politics.

**If the player does nothing:** Saxons raid eastern counties each year regardless; Uther is carried to battle on his litter without Salisbury's participation. Absent knights gain no Glory from the campaigns and miss any court-building opportunities during these crucial years.

## 495 — Uther Dies / St. Albans / The Poisoned Feast

**Situation:** Saxons under King Octa have wintered at Eburacum and are marching south. Uther is gravely ill. The army musters at Logres.

**Court:** Held at Sarum. The assembled army of Logres plus many allies.

**Special Guests:** The assembled army of Logres.

**Gossip:**
- "Don't you think we should go away now, before it is too late?"
- "The king is here with all the knights of Logres. We are safe."
- "Do you think we have enough supplies set aside, in case we have to run for it?"

**News:**
- Brastias: "They're just Saxons, men. We have beaten them before; we will beat them again. They aren't even smart enough to ride horses!"
- Ulfius: "The Saxons wintered at Eburacum and have begun moving south. We are going to get them."

**Royal Conversation:** None. The king remains ill.

**Battle of St. Albans**
*Source: Geoffrey of Monmouth*

Uther is carried on a litter to the field at St. Albans to face the Saxon army under King Octa.

- **Length of Battle:** 8 rounds
- **Battle Size:** Large
- **Commanders:** King Uther (Battle 20) vs. King Octa (Battle 19)
- **Modifiers:**
  - British are not confident: –5
  - Uther has superior troops: +5
  - Saxons are all on foot; mounted knights get +5 to Combat skills
- **The Fight:** Normal. At some point Earl Roderick's horse is struck down; his men must rescue him from Saxon warriors (berserkers possible)
- **After the Battle:** Victor — Decisive Victory (Uther). Saxons took heavy losses; survivors fled. Ulfius and Brastias both gravely wounded
- **Plunder:** £10 in goods
- **Glory:** 60 per round, x2 for Decisive Victory

**The Infamous Feast**

The army celebrates at St. Albans castle. A great victory feast is held.

- Temperate/Indulgent rolls: failure = knights drink to excess
- Chaste/Lustful rolls: failure = knights slip away from feast
- Around midnight: everyone who drank anything feels a horrible onset — vomiting blood, dying of magical poison
- **Survival condition:** Only a Temperate critical success (you drank nothing) allows survival in the Great Hall
- Knights in the bailey or elsewhere (drunk and asleep, or off with a woman) survive by accident
- The entire ruling class of Logres is wiped out: Uther, Earl Roderick, nearly all barons
- Surviving player knights witness the carnage; castellan's wife eventually restores order
- **Action required:** GM asks what each player knight does — this moment defines character

**Aftermath:**
- Uther, Earl Roderick, and ~hundreds of nobles dead
- Brastias and Ulfius survived (they were in hospital from battle wounds)
- The countess (Roderick's widow) becomes regent for the 3-year-old heir
- **Glory:** 25 for attending Earl Roderick's funeral; 100 for King Uther's funeral at Stonehenge

**Christmas Court — End of the Uther Period:**
- **Royal Court:** No Royal House; no heir publicly named; Brastias sought by all lords, accepts none
- **Salisbury:** Countess is grieving and terrified; no officers; needs loyal knights
  - Sir Lycus agitates for strong male leadership ("not women"); Sir Leo swears for countess
  - Player knights must decide: swear to Countess Ellen?
  - If swear: check to Loyalty (lord) passion + 1 point Honor; even Lycus eventually swears
- **Winter Phase Modifiers:**
  - All Logres regions: +5 to Starting Bad Weather (king's death)
  - Lincoln, Lonazep: raided (+1d6)
  - Huntington: pillaged (+2d6)
  - Hertford: plundered (+3d6)

**If the player does nothing:** The Battle of St. Albans is won regardless; Uther, Roderick, and the ruling class are still poisoned at the feast. Absent knights survive by accident and still face the choice of swearing to Countess Ellen — but gain no Glory from the battle, funeral, or the defining moment of action in the Great Hall.

### Adventure: BORDER CHALLENGE
**Setting:** Any border of Salisbury or allied lands.
**Hook:** Knights ride patrol; dogs bark, armed men mount up at a border post.
**Resolution options:**
- Fight: Glory for combat; continue into enemy land
- Stand and talk: Routine identification; lord reception varies from Friendly to Hateful (see lord's personality)
- Run: Brief pursuit, then dropped

**Lord reception table:**
- Friendly: Wash up, dinner, night's rest, provisions, escort
- Neutral: Interview, possible advice or escort
- Unfriendly: Interview, then dismissal with escort back
- Hostile: Interview, then capture while guests sleep
- Hateful: Attack guests in his own hall; he loses Honor

**Glory:** None for routine; combat Glory as usual

### Adventure: YOUR SAXON FOE
**Structure:** A recurring NPC relationship arc across multiple years.
**Setup:** Player knight has a specific Saxon enemy (GM creates: name, clan, fighting style).
**Episodes:** Border ambush; prisoner exchange; chance meeting at parley; winter skirmish near the enemy's village; eventual confrontation.
**Purpose:** Personalizes the Saxon wars; develops Hate (Saxons) passion through specific grievance.

### Adventure: PRESUMPTUOUS PRAETOR
**Setting:** A Roman praetor (official from the last Roman outpost) arrives at Salisbury court seeking Logres's help against the Franks.
**Hook:** The praetor Syagrius has been driven out of Soissons (last Roman territory in Gaul, conquered by King Claudas of the Franks). He seeks Uther's army to reclaim it.
**Complication:** The praetor is arrogant, treating British knights as barbarians; his Greek secretary is condescending.
**Intrigue roll:** Success = Syagrius is desperate, not arrogant; Critical = he has already promised half the Frankish treasury to Uther
**Resolution:** Uther will discuss it but probably not act; player knights mediate between Syagrius and Roderick's men

### Adventure: MERLIN'S FAVOR
**Structure:** Merlin appears and asks player knights to perform a specific service without explanation.
**Typical requests:** Escort a covered wagon; fight off attackers at a crossroads; guard a building until dawn; carry a sealed letter.
**Reward:** Merlin's gratitude (he remembers them favorably); may grant a minor magical boon; Glory 25–50

### Recurring: RIDING IN ENEMY LANDS
**Setting:** Any hostile territory entered deliberately or accidentally.
**Approach options (roll 1d6 or choose):**
- 1–2: Cautious (hide, avoid roads): Suspicious roll; failure = spotted anyway
- 3: Circumspect (small roads, normal pace): Awareness roll; failure = locals notice
- 4: Reckless (main roads, full pace): Almost certainly encounter local knights
**Typical encounters:** Border patrol (go to Border Challenge); lone knight challenge; bandits; lost traveler who's actually a spy

## Period Summary: What Changes

- **485:** Knights enter service; Saxons threaten east
- **486–488:** Uther consolidates; northern lords come to heel or resist; Gorlois war ends Uther's domestic peace
- **489–493:** Arthur born and hidden; Uther declining; Saxons press harder
- **494:** Last year of effective Uther rule
- **495:** Battle of St. Albans; Uther and all of Logres's nobility poisoned; Anarchy begins

**Into the Anarchy:** Player knights are survivors of a catastrophe. The structure of Logres is shattered. Their loyalty is now to Countess Ellen of Salisbury and each other. There is no High King. There will not be one for fifteen years.
