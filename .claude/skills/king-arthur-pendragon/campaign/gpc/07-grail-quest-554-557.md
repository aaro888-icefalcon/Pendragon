# GPC Chapter 9: Grail Quest Period (554–557)

**Situation:** The Holy Grail appears at Camelot. The entire Round Table swears to seek it. For four years, most of Britain's best knights are absent. The Wastelands reach their peak. King Mark of Cornwall invades. The quest ends with Galahad achieving the Grail, the land healing (the Germination), and Tristram's murder.

**Period Boundary:** 554–557. The Grail Quest itself takes 3.25 years (Galahad begins and ends it; Bors reports back).

**Key Mechanics This Period:**
- Grail Quest terrain effects: Enchantment, Wasteland, Glorification
- Yellow Pestilence — peaks in 555, fades by 557
- Temptation system (worldly vs. spiritual)
- Road knights and Grail Quest opponents (special stat blocks)
- Quest Returnee rules (most non-Round Table knights home by autumn of first year)
- Cornish Invasion (555) — siege rules

---

## Year 554 — The Grail Appears

**Situation:** The Holy Grail manifests at Camelot. Galahad arrives, takes the Siege Perilous, and the entire Round Table departs on the High Holy Quest.

**Court:** Pentecost at Camelot. The most significant court of the campaign.

**Away From Camelot:** None yet — all are present for the Pentecost feast.

**Events:**

### Event: GRAIL APPEARANCE

**The Pentecost Sequence:**

1. **Lancelot Knights Galahad** — A young man of extraordinary beauty arrives; Lancelot (his father, though Galahad does not know it) knights him. All Awareness rolls notice something uncanny about the youth.

2. **The Siege Perilous** — The empty chair at the Round Table has letters of gold spelling out: "This is the Siege of Galahad." When Galahad sits, Arthur weeps, for the Table is complete — and thus its purpose is done.

3. **The Floating Sword** — A red marble stone floats down the Thames with a sword embedded in it. The inscription: "Never shall man take me hence but only he by whose side I ought to hang, and he shall be the best knight of the world." Every knight fails to draw it. Galahad draws it easily.

4. **The Grail Manifestation** — At the Pentecost feast:
   - Sweet odor fills the hall
   - Sunbeam through the windows, seven times brighter than day
   - The Holy Grail, covered in white samite, floats through the hall
   - Every knight sees the food and drink he most desires
   - The Grail vanishes

5. **Emperor Yesterday's Speech** — A mysterious figure appears and delivers a prophetic speech about the Quest's dangers and necessity.

6. **Gawaine's Oath** — Sir Gawaine swears to seek the Grail for one year. Others follow. Within minutes, all Round Table knights have sworn.

7. **Arthur's Grief** — "Alas! This quest shall never return together... there shall be many a good knight die in this quest."

**Player Hook:** Does your knight swear the oath? Round Table knights MUST. Non-Round Table knights who swear gain the quest as an Amor passion (vs. worldly temptations).

**Glory for Attending:** 100 Glory for witnessing the Grail manifestation.

**The Departure:**
- 150 Round Table knights depart
- Each takes a road alone (quest tradition forbids companionship)
- Guenever weeps "right so they departed and every each of them took the way that him best liked"

**Christmas Court 554:**
- Arthur holds court alone with those who remain
- 15 Round Table chairs already show blank names (knights dead)
- Galeholt hears false rumor that Lancelot is dead; dies of grief; his empire fragments
- Yellow Pestilence begins (roll per holding; see table below)

**Yellow Pestilence 554:**
| 1d6 | Effect | Starting Bad Weather Modifier |
|-----|--------|-------------------------------|
| 1–4 | No plague | +0 |
| 5 | Some sickness | +5 |
| 6 | Pestilence | +10 |

**Winter Phase Modifiers (554):**
- Western Isles, Long Isles, Dal Riada: Land was raided (+1d6)
- Kent, Essex, Wessex, Sussex, Nohaut: Work slowdown (+1d6)
- Anglia, Sorestan: Uprising (+2d6)

**If the player does nothing:** The Grail manifests and all Round Table knights swear the quest regardless; 150 knights depart and Galeholt dies of grief, fragmenting his empire. Absent non-Round-Table knights gain no Glory from the manifestation (100 Glory missed) and are left behind to face whatever comes with a depleted court.

## Year 555 — The Cornish Invasion

**Situation:** With almost all knights away on the Grail Quest, King Mark of Cornwall seizes the opportunity. He assembles a multinational army and invades. He also kidnaps Queen Isolt from Joyous Garde.

**Court:** Camelot. Arthur is effectively beleaguered.

**Away From Camelot:** All Round Table knights on Quest. Early returnees: Orkney clan (Mordred, Agravaine, Gaheris) lounge in Stirling. Tor, Sagramore, Dodinal also returned. Old Knights: Lucan, Bedivere remain at Camelot.

**Gossip:** "And by the worst knights in the world. Woe to us!"

**Events:**

### Event: CORNISH INVASION

**Source:** Prose Tristran, Roman du Graal
**Note:** Not in Malory; included as it provides the key adventure for non-Quest knights.

**The Invasion Sequence:**

**Mark's Army:** Irish kerns, French mercenaries, Welsh warriors, Cornish knights, hired Huns (under Sir Andred), Ethiopian bodyguard Sir Dawit.

**The Infernal Engine:** A primitive cannon — 50-yard iron tube on massive wheeled frame, pulled by 20 oxen. Burns black powder (gunpowder) loaded from marked carts bearing a **red-and-yellow mystic sign** (CRITICAL: this is the same sign from the Stone House Visit in 550–551).

**Key NPC:**

#### Sir Dawit (Ethiopian Mercenary)

```
SIZ 17, DEX 12, STR 18, CON 14, APP 8
Move 3; Armor 16 + shield
Unconscious 8; Major Wound 14; Knockdown 17; Hit Points 31; Healing Rate 3; Damage 6d6
Glory 6,300
Combat Skills: Dagger 9, Lance 28, Spear 19, Sword 23; Battle 16, Horsemanship 24
Skills: Awareness 16, Boating 13, Folklore 12, Heraldry 10, Hunting 15
Traits: Chaste 10, Energetic 18, Forgiving 11, Generous 15, Honest 19, Just 10, Merciful 5, Valorous 18
Passions: Loyalty (lord) 12, Love (family) 16, Hospitality 16, Honor 13, Hate (Breton knights) 14
Horse: Destrier (8d6)
```

**Kidnapping Raid (simultaneous with invasion):**
- Mark personally leads 30 knights by sea to Joyous Garde
- Captures Queen Isolt (Tristram's love) while the castle guard is reduced
- Sir Dawit kills anyone who resists
- Huns encircle the party on horseback for the return march

**Player Hook:** Do the knights witness the kidnap party on the road? Can they rescue Isolt?

**The Invasion Sequence:**

**Phase 1: Somerset Falls**

- Somerset is occupied

**Skirmishing:** Make a single Battle roll; on success, fight one group from the table. Player knights may fight up to 4 skirmishes before nightfall.

**The Siege of Sarum:**
- 3-option player decision: defend Sarum, breakout to Camelot, seek allies
- Defending the Walls:
  - Enemy archers: Bow skill 6 (effective after modifiers); 1 roll per round per defender
  - Simple bows do 3d6 damage
  - Wall defense bonus: +12 to defender's weapon skill (attackers climbing ladders)
  - Glory: 25 per round of wall defense (×2 for critical results)
- The Infernal Engine continues past Sarum toward Camelot (doesn't stop there)

**The Breakout:**
- Loyalty (King Arthur) vs. Loyalty (Earl Robert) — losers of the opposed roll must stay or lose Honor
- Breakout skirmish: roll 1d20 three times on "Battle Enemy — Footmen" table (Pendragon Appendix 4)
- Once past: long ride to Camelot through enemy-controlled countryside

**Phase 3: The Siege of Camelot**
- Gawaine commands the defense
- Assault probe: 3 rounds; defenders throw it back if full garrison present
- Enemy archers: Bow 18 –12 (battlements) = effective 6; roll per round
- The Infernal Engine approaches (same mechanic as Wells)

### Battle of the Engine

**The Climax:**

**Squire roll:** Success = your squire produces a smoldering fire pot.

**Awareness:**
- Failure = single cart with red-and-yellow sign visible
- Success = multiple carts with the sign
- Critical = people near carts are already running away (they know what will happen)

**Action:** Fire the carts with the red-and-yellow sign (gunpowder). Ride away at full gallop.

**The Explosion:**
- Deafening, blinding blast
- Huge dust cloud
- The Infernal Engine destroyed (barrel bent, frame shattered)
- Entire surrounding area cleared of standing foes
- Cornish army breaks and flees

**Christmas Court 555:**
- "Triumph! We are blessed by God for our victory!"
- King offers Round Table membership to Heroes of Camelot
- Guenever is angry Lancelot did not appear

**Yellow Pestilence 555 (peaks this year):**

| 1–4 | No plague | +0 |

**Winter Phase Modifiers (555):**
- Clarence, Rydychan, Southports, Windsor, London; NW Isles, Long Isles, Dal Riada: raided (+1d6)
- Salisbury, Silbury, Marlboro, Somerset: pillaged (+2d6)
- Hampshire: plundered (+3d6)

**If the player does nothing:** Mark's army overruns Somerset, besieges Sarum, and advances on Camelot regardless; the Infernal Engine is only stopped if someone who learned the red-and-yellow sign (Stone House, 550–551) acts. Absent knights gain no Glory from wall defense or the engine destruction, and if no one fires the powder carts the Infernal Engine may breach Camelot's walls.

## Grail Quest Period Adventures (554–557)

These adventures apply to knights who are ON the Grail Quest. They may be run in any year 554–557.

### Settings

**The Enchantment:** Faerie effects active everywhere; terrain changes without notice; elves desperate to stop or help the Quest depending on faction.

**The Wasteland:** Listeneisse blasted for 40 years; expanding; dead forests, parched streams, gaunt people.

**Glorification:** After Grail appeared, unique wonders appear — white hermits, miraculous fountains, holy sites.

### Temptations

Every questing knight faces worldly temptations (tailored by GM to that knight's character sheet). Each succumbing to temptation earns a Cowardly or vice check and brings a monk/hermit to lecture the knight on spiritual unworthiness. Examples:
- Amor or Love passion triggers: a woman who seems to embody the beloved
- Hospitality triggers: a magnificent feast in an enchanted castle
- Avarice triggers: a hoard of gold in a ruined castle
- Reckless triggers: a challenge from the most fearsome knight ever seen

### Adventure: ON THE ROAD

**Time:** Any
**Setting:** All roads of Britain

Road opponents appear without warning:

#### Black Knight I
- Charges at full gallop; delivers single lance blow; continues past without stopping
- Never knocked off horse; appears unkillable; just thunders past
- Stats: as average knight, but Knockdown 150 (magical), Lance 25, charger (6d6)

#### Black Knight II
- Can be unhorsed; fights on foot; sword says aloud "Fight me"
- If defeated: offers mercy and requests it
- Stats: as old knight, but Lance 25, Sword 18, courser (5d6)

#### Black Knight III
- Asks and gives no quarter; black destrier also fights
- Stats: as notable knight, but Lance 25, Sword 20, Mace 18; battle-destrier: Hoof/Stomp 15 (5d6)

#### Angelic Knight
- One of the heavenly host in knight's form
- Blindingly bright; appears indistinct
- Stats: as small giant, ALL combat skills 39; heavenly charger (10d6)
- **Special:** vs. Christian Religious knight: always loses graciously, no damage
- Glory: 250 even if winner is a Christian Religious knight

#### Fiery Knight
- Entirely on fire or made of fire
- Anyone close enough to fight takes 6 points per round (no armor except Chivalric magic armor)
- Stats: as old knight

#### Devil Knight
- Wicked morning star; plate armor that groans
- Stats: as any quality (GM's choice); often Breathe Fire (@20, 2d6 damage, only magic armor helps)
- Against all devils: Religious knight gains 10-point Spiritual Armor
- Can fade to nothing if confused

#### Faerie Knight
- Chivalrous; uses all human weapons; not shy of iron
- Stats: See Pendragon core Appendix 2

#### Round Table Knight (disguised)
- Stats: famous or extraordinary quality
- May be deeply affected emotionally (roll d20):

| d20 | Attitude | Modifier |
|-----|----------|----------|
| 1 | Despondent | –20 |
| 2–5 | Depressed | –10 |
| 6–10 | Normal | +0 |
| 11–14 | Impassioned | +10 |
| 15–19 | Manic | +20 |
| 20 | Blissed out | Double (critical passion) |

### Adventure: DUALITY TOURNAMENTS

**Time:** Any (especially 555)
**Setting:** Various castles

Tournaments during the Grail Quest are strange affairs with supernatural significance.

**BLACK/WHITE TOURNEY:**
- Both sides wear armor supplied by the sponsor: one side all white, one all black
- Sponsor insists colors mean nothing (they do)
- Are there disguised Round Table questers among the crowd?
- Solutions: Wear the armor; compete; watch for the Great Knights' appearances

**Other Variants:**
- Castle of Swords: Red vs. White (red = bad)
- Castle of Shadows: Red vs. Black (red = good)
- Castle of the Pines: Red vs. Green (red = winter)
- Castle of Bran: Pagan vs. Christian
- Castle of Towers: Humans vs. Elves
- Castle Ben: Humans vs. Undead (led by devil knights)
- Castle of the Proud: Rich knights vs. Poor knights (armor+steed wager)

### Adventure: THE ADVENTUROUS SHIELD

**Time:** 555
**Setting:** Isolated abbey of Brown Monks, Forest of Logres

**Hook:** A white shield with blood-red cross hangs behind the abbey altar. Monks warn: "No man may bear this shield but he be the Worthiest Knight of the World."

**The Test:**
- Take the shield: within 3 days, the Shield Knight appears
- Shield Knight Stats: as small giant, ALL combat skills 39; heavenly charger (10d6)
- **Special:** vs. the knight DESTINED for this shield (Sir Galahad): always loses graciously
- vs. any other: fights until opponent is helpless, removes shield, returns to abbey

**Secret:** Shield belonged to Joseph of Arimathea. Destined for Galahad.

**Glory:**
- Take the shield away: 25 Glory
- Unsuccessful but heroic fight vs. the Shield Knight: up to 100 Glory
- Defeating the Shield Knight: 500 Glory (or 50 if the winner is Galahad)

### Adventure: CASTLE OF MAIDENS

**Source:** Malory XIII, 15–16
**Time:** 555–556
**Setting:** Strong castle by the Severn River

**Hook:** The castle holds an evil custom — all knights and ladies who pass are captured.

**The Seven Deadly Brothers (Villains):**
- Notable knight stats × 7
- Always fight together; no chivalry; no fairness
- Secretly seized the castle 7 years ago; imprison all ladies (many held for years) to prevent word getting out

**Warning Sequence (as knight approaches):**
1. Old man at outer edge warns them off
2. Seven fair maidens at castle perimeter warn them
3. Squire near the gate warns them (also stalling while brothers arm)

**Action:** Only solution is to challenge the Seven Brothers. They cannot be parlayed with.

**Aftermath:** 
- Prisoners freed: large groups of women heading home across Britain (many held 1–7 years)
- These freed women create the "Liberated Maidens" event of 555 (luring some questers home)

**Glory:** 100 for liberating the castle + combat glory for the Brothers.

### Adventure: ADVENTUROUS CASTLE (CASTLE OF ADVENTURE)

**Time:** 555 or later
**Setting:** Turning Castle, Listeneisse

**Hook:** A plain-looking castle with gates closed. Porter admits knights. The lord is King Pellam.

**Scene 1 — Dinner:** King Pellam interviews players; satisfied they are not evil, invites to dinner. Normal meal. If players ask about the castle's name, he begs them to forgo the adventure. "It has killed many good young men."

**Scene 2 — The Trial:** Only one knight can attempt it. He is taken by six weeping maidens to a tower chamber. Elegantly furnished. Huge bed. Crossbows loaded in the walls. Another locked door.

**Awareness, Suspicious, Prudent:** Success = hear a voice: "Ha! Sir Knight, you will die if you lie here unarmed, for this is the Couch Adventurous."

**Pious:** Success = +10 to all subsequent rolls. Critical = double all skills.

**The Adventurous Bed (20 rounds):**
- Bed rolls wildly: STR roll each round (Success = stay on; Failure = fall off, 3d6 damage)
- If off the bed: DEX modified for armor (Failure = 4d6 from bed impact)
- After 20 rounds: bed stops

**Crossbows (10 fire simultaneously):**
- Awareness: Success = use shield for partial cover against 5 bolts
- Each bolt: 1d6+12 damage
- Shield blocks up to 5 bolts if Awareness succeeded

**The Lion (from Pendragon Appendix 2):**
- Door springs open; a fierce lion leaps in; door closes immediately

**The Silent Knight:**
- After 10 minutes' rest: armed knight appears, attacks without speaking
- Famous knight stats with Sword 20
- When at half HP, staggers back; door opens and closes; reemerges whole
- Does this up to 3 times or until killed outright (HP to 0 in single blow)
- Following through the door: DEX modified for armor (Success = enter; in that room, healed of all wounds)
- If the player knight follows through and forces himself back: he is healed; fight continues
- Victory condition: force the Silent Knight back 3 times OR slay him outright

**Success:**
- In the morning, maidens rejoice
- King Pellam comes; leads the knight to the Great Hall
- King Fisherman lies on a bed in rich gown
- Go to THE GRAIL FEAST

**Failure:**
- Knight goes unconscious at some point
- Grail procession enters (see THE GRAIL FEAST) — witnessed but cannot participate
- Knight is seized, put in cart, whipped through town while helpless
- Loses 1 Honor
- Wakes alone on the Wasteland; companions not far away; Penrith Castle visible to north

**Glory:** 100 to attempt the Perilous Bed. 500 to succeed.

### Adventure: THE GRAIL FEAST

**Source:** The end of the Arthurian Quest
**Time:** After succeeding at the Perilous Bed
**Setting:** The Castle of Adventure (Adventurous Castle)

**Hook:** Knight is shown the Great Hall. King Fisherman lies on a bed, wounded.

**Recognize, +5:** Success = This is the King Fisherman.

**The Adventure:** As described for "Castle of Joy" in Chapter 5, but:
- King Fisherman and King Pellam appear to have separated into two distinct people
- The Question must be asked: "Whom does the Grail serve?" (or variant — Gamemaster's choice)
- Asking the right question heals the Fisher King; the land blooms instantly

**Glory:** 1,000 for succeeding.

### Event: RECENT GRAVES AND TOMBS

**Time:** 555–557

While wandering, knights encounter new graves of questers. Shields identify the dead.

**Recent Graves Mechanic:**
- Fresh dirt = recently dead
- Settled dirt = older
- Level = old grave
- Heraldry roll (with modifiers) to identify the arms

**Deaths in Malory:** Bagdemagus, Dinadan, Ywaine the Bastard
**Deaths named elsewhere:** Erec son of Lak, Palomides, Aglovale

**Note:** About half the Round Table dies on the Quest. Witnessing a half-empty Table afterwards emphasizes the cost.

## Year 556 — War in the West / Return of Some Questers

**Situation:** The Grail Quest enters its third year. Over 50 Round Table knights confirmed dead. Brian of the Isles attacks Clarence. Tristram surrenders to Mark.

**Court:** Camelot. Arthur appears to have aged 30 years in 2; hair thin, beard long, needs help walking.

**King Arthur's Stats (556):**
```
SIZ 7, DEX 5, STR 13, CON 8, APP 10
Note: Chronologically 64 years old; appears 90
```

**Away From Camelot:** Lancelot, Gawaine, Gareth, and all Great Knights still on Quest.

**Events:**

**Returnees:** Orkney clan back (Stirling). Constanine, Griflet, Tristram returned.

**Revenge on Cornwall Deflected:** Gawaine leads army to punish Cornwall for 555 invasion; turned back by news of Brian's attack on Clarence.

**The Firebrands:** A small band of knights who took oaths to fight Cornwall this summer refuse to turn back; they ride on alone, calling themselves the Firebrands.

### Battle of Cirencester (War Against Brian)

**Source:** GPC original

**Setup:** Brian of the Isles attacks Clarence with magical assistance; defeats the ducal army at Gloucester through confusion magic. Gawaine arrives to fight.

**Special Rule:** No Honor lost, no Cowardly check for refusing to fight — but volunteers gain a Valorous check.

```
Length of Battle: 2 rounds
Battle Size: Medium
Commanders: Sir Gawaine (Battle 22) vs. King Brian (Battle 20)
Modifiers:
  Brian has magical assistance: –10 (to Gawaine's Battle)
  Gawaine is badly outnumbered: –5
Round 1: First Charge. Normal.
Round 2: Normal.
Victor: Indecisive.
Plunder: None.
Glory: 30 per round.
```

**Aftermath:** Brian withdraws after looting Gloucester. Griflet takes command; Brian finally withdraws entirely.

**Tristram's Surrender:**
- Tristram arrives unarmed and humble at Castle Dore
- Confesses love for Isolt; promises to serve faithfully
- Mark accepts; Sir Andred (who holds Tristram's former lands) is silenced by Tristram's offer to give up all claims
- Tristram moons about in Cornwall, acting champion in public, cuckolding Mark at night

**Yellow Pestilence 556 (fading):**
| 1d6 | Effect | Starting Bad Weather Modifier |
|-----|--------|-------------------------------|
| 1–5 | No plague | +0 |
| 6 | Some sickness | +5 |

**Winter Phase Modifiers (556):**
- Escavalon, Wuerensis, Salisbury: raided (+1d6)
- Clarence: pillaged (+2d6)
- Gloucester: plundered (+3d6)

**If the player does nothing:** Brian loots Gloucester and withdraws regardless; Tristram surrenders to Mark and the affair with Isolt quietly resumes. Absent knights gain no Glory from the Battle of Cirencester and cannot join the Firebrands who rode on alone against Cornwall.

## Year 557 — The Germination / Lancelot Returns / Tristram Murdered

**Situation:** The Grail Quest ends. Galahad achieves the Grail; dies in a state of grace. Percivale heals the Fisher King; the Wasteland lifts. The Germination occurs — all of Britain bursts into sudden miraculous bloom. Lancelot returns. Tristram is murdered by King Mark.

**Court:** Camelot. Suddenly, Arthur looks younger than yesterday — not young, but like his actual 65 years.

**King Arthur's Stats (557):**
```
SIZ 10, DEX 13, STR 17, CON 14, APP 14
Hair: fuller, silver; beard: trimmed
Note: Still somewhat underweight but vital and aware
```

**Away From Camelot:** Bors, Percivale (in Listeneisse to rule as Grail Kings), Galahad (ascended to heaven), Mordred and Orkneys (questing — still north).

**The Germination:**
- Spring arrives abruptly — all springs of Britain released at once
- Trees leaf in visible time; grain thrusts from ground overnight
- Magical forests fade; new growth springs from dead trees
- People gather food in joyous frenzy; some frightened by supernatural speed
- The Wasteland and Enchantment both end simultaneously
- Druids and witches claim credit; Christians give thanks; both are right

**Events:**

**Lancelot Returns:**
- Returns to Camelot after 3.25-year absence
- Arthur declares immediate tournament in his honor (Lancelot judges only)

**Lancelot's Honor Tournament:**
```
Sponsor: King Arthur
Type: Classic style, Local
Teams: Round Table vs. everyone else
Prize: Gamemaster's choice (~£10 value)
Canon: Sir Gawaine declared Champion
Afterward: Lancelot speaks humbly of his quest — he saw the Grail only from afar, redeemed by grace
```

**Pious or Modest roll:** Success = you know he's trying to be sincere but isn't quite.

**Tristram's Murder:**
- Mark finds Tristram in a "foolish, helpless moment" with Isolt
- Mark takes a "trenchant glaive" — thrusts it through Tristram's body, twists it, pulls out organs
- Tristram dies. Isolt, separated from him, fades and dies soon after.
- Note: One version says Tristram's death-seizure crushed Isolt in his arms. Another says she simply pined away.
- Mark buries them in separate cemeteries on opposite sides of a church
- From Tristram's grave grows ivy; from hers a rose bush; they climb the church and meet atop it, entwined

**Lyonesse Sinks:**
- Tristram's realm does not survive him
- Beasts begin lowing in alarm; priests receive visions of destruction
- The earth vibrates; the land sinks into the sea
- Only foundations visible on clear days in the shallows

**Sir Bors Returns:**
- Bors arrives at Camelot (the last Great Knight back)
- Relates: Percivale healed King Fisherman; Galahad and Bors sailed to the Middle East; Galahad died ascending to heaven
- Bors returned home alone

**Brian Raids Cheshire:**
- Brian's fleet pillages the western coast from Cheshire northward
- Arthur's army approaches; Brian withdraws with loot

**Yellow Pestilence 557:** Does not strike this year.

**Special Winter Phase Modifiers (557):**
- Because of the Germination: ALL lands in Britain receive –20 to Starting Bad Weather
- Cheshire, Listeneisse, Cambenet, Gallowey: raided (+1d6) — cancels some of the bonus in those areas

**Tournament Schedule (557):**
- July: Neighborhood (ransom), Local
- August: Neighborhood (armor), Local (ransom)
- September: Neighborhood, Local (ransom)
- October: Neighborhood (armor+horse)
- Pentecost: Prize = five magnificent tapestries from French palaces; Grand Ball one night; Court of Love one afternoon; "Fifty Wines of France" event

**Christmas Court 557:**
- All knights in something of a daze about the Germination
- Lancelot present; glowing with humble grace
- Tristram's death discussed with sorrow
- Mordred's comment: "This Grail Quest was the worst thing to ever happen to us."

**If the player does nothing:** The Germination occurs regardless as Galahad achieves the Grail; Tristram is murdered by Mark and Lyonesse sinks into the sea. Absent knights miss Lancelot's Honor Tournament (25 Glory for witnessing) and cannot carry the Excalibur knowledge forward from Bors's account.
