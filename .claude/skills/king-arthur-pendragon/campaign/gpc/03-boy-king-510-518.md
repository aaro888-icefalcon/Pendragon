# Boy King Period: 510–518
## GPC Chapter 4

> **Period Theme:** Arthur fights to secure his throne and Britain's High Kingship through a series of brutal battles. Chivalry is institutionalized. The Round Table is founded. The Enchantment of Britain begins with the Dolorous Stroke.

---

## Period Overview

**Historical analog:** 12th-century England — Norman dynasty end, coming of Henry II / Angevin dynasty. Guenever = Eleanor of Aquitaine.

**Politics:** Arthur's Logres is internally united but threatened by northern coalition (Lot, Nentres, Uriens, etc.) and Saxon pressure. He follows the route of conquest, not acclamation, to become High King.

**What's New This Period:**
- Armor: 12-point reinforced chainmail + closed helm now common
- Weapons: Jousting lance, morning star, warflail newly available
- Horses: Chargers widely available (sergeants ride them too)
- Heraldry: Personal coats-of-arms become standard; heralds appear
- Chivalry: Instituted Year 511; required for Round Table from Year 514
- Tournaments: First established Year 510 (Frankish style, mocked at first)
- Women gain more social status; Arthur marries for love

## Year 510 — First Tournament; Sword in Stone

**Situation:** No king in Britain. The sword in the stone has stood in London (St. Paul's churchyard) for years. A tournament is called — the first on British soil.

**Court:** London. Lords present: King Alain of Escavalon, King Leodegrance of Cameliard, Centurion King of Malahaut, King Lot of Lothian and Orkney, Duke Escan of Gloucester, Duke Derfel of Lindsey, Earl Sanam of Bedegraine, Earl Robert of Salisbury, Earl Gilbert of Hertford, Baron Macsen of Lonazep.

**Events:**
- Young Earl Robert takes his retinue (including player knights) to the tournament in London
- Tournament is the first Frankish-style event; mocked by many as non-lethal; praised by others for same reason

**Adventure: THE FIRST TOURNAMENT**
- Type: Regional Old-style. Blunt weapons; every lord for himself. Winner = last man on field
- The tournament is never finished — interrupted by crowd shouting "The Sword! The Sword!"
- **Glory:** 25 for fighting in the tournament

**Event: THE SWORD IN THE STONE**
- Setting: St. Paul's Cathedral, London
- Merlin stands nearby (notably distant from Archbishop Dubricus)
- Three men at the stone: Sir Ector (old knight), Sir Kay (young knight), Arthur (squire)
- All noblemen try and fail; crowd gets surly; "Let the boy try!"
- Arthur pulls sword; celestial trumpets, beam of light, dove and eagle spiral upward (Merlin's work)
- Immediate loyalists: King Leodegrance, Sir Ector, Sir Kay, Sir Hervis de Revil, Prince Lanceor of Estregales, Sir Brastias
- King Lot: "Are we to be ruled by a beardless bastard?" — refuses; other great barons follow
- Merlin and Dubricus quiet crowd; schedule repeat trials at Candlemas, Easter, Pentecost

**Subsequent drawings:**
| Date | Drawing | Result |
|---|---|---|
| February 2 (Candlemas) | 2nd drawing | "Eager Vassals" swear — Ulfius, Duke Silchester; Duke Corneus of Lindsey; Bishop Baudwin; Earl of Hertford; Earl of Dorset; Earl Robert of Salisbury |
| March 21 (Easter) | 3rd drawing | "Good Vassals" — Duke of Clarence, Earl of Jagent, Earl of Wuerensis; northern coalition musters against Arthur |
| May 1 (Pentecost) | 4th drawing | Peasant mob chants "Ar-thur!" — last recalcitrant lords concede; coronation |

**Coronation (May 1):**
- Arthur knighted by "the best man present" (Malory leaves unnamed — GM may use player knight, Bishop Baudwin, Duke Corneus, or Cador)
- Crowned King of Logres at St. Paul's; takes arms: gold shield, two green dragons back-to-back
- Arms of High King (later): red shield with seven gold crowns
- **Glory:** 100 for witnessing coronation; 150 for participating

**May 10 — Supreme Collegium at Carlion:**
- Arthur elected Imperator and Caesar of Britannia; Wledig (Chief Warlord) over British tribes
- Six northern lords arrive with armies; Arthur bids welcome — Lot and allies insult messengers

## Year 511 — Round Table Gifted; Rebellion Begins

**Situation:** Arthur's court is established. Chivalry formally instituted. Northern kings bicker but remain hostile. Romans arrive demanding tribute.

**Court:** Carlion. Special Guests: Merlin, Archbishop Dubricus, King Lak of Escavalon, Hervis de Revels, Duke Ulfius, Brastias, Sir Ector, Sir Kay, **Queen Margawse of Lothian** (with sons Gawaine, Agravaine, Gaheris).

**Events:**
- Roman ambassadors (senators, praetors, centurions) arrive demanding tribute; Arthur refuses — "Only tribute Britain sends is sharp swords and spears" — sends them packing
- Chivalry formally introduced; player knights may now strive to become Chivalrous
- Margawse attends court — Lot sends her to spy; she is lusty and observant; Arthur is comfortable with her
- Army raids Saxon lands under Hervis de Revil (player hook: join the raid; note Hervis's Hate (Saxons) causes wholesale slaughter)
- Army repairs St. Albans walls; builds castles throughout Logres
- Late Summer: Margawse departs; she is pregnant (Mordred)

**Christmas Court:**
- Strange knight arrives: Brown Knight of the Wilds (le Chevalier Brun des Terres Sauvages) — old Norman armor all brown (not rust); shield bears oak branch. Arthur invites him to winter. He accepts.
- Royal: "A year without war! Auspicious already."

**Special Winter Phase Modifiers:** None.

**If the player does nothing:** Chivalry is introduced; the north bickers; Hervis raids Saxons. No major battles this year.

## Year 512 — Arthur Acknowledged Son of Uther; War Moves North

**Situation:** Arthur's northern campaign begins. His parentage is publicly revealed by Ygraine. Balin arrives at court causing scandal. Roman tribute refused.

**Court:** Silchester. Special Guests: Sir Lanceor, many lords and knights.

**Gossip/Intrigue:**
- Northern kings spent winter bickering; Lot has new army + King Ryons (son of giant and sorceress)
- Talk of northern babies kidnapped and possibly killed (Merlin took them to sea — all feared dead; Lot is enraged)

**Events:**
- **Late February — Shrine of St. Albans:** Arthur visits; Queen Ygraine (now a nun) brought to dinner
  - Duke Ulfius publicly accuses Ygraine of treason — tizzy ensues
  - Ygraine publicly tells story of stranger with dead husband's semblance; gave child to Merlin
  - Merlin: "My lady, this is your son, and Uther was his father"
  - Arthur confirmed as son of Uther Pendragon — "no more can he be called Merlin's brat"
  - **Glory:** 100 for witnessing Ygraine's revelation

- **Early Spring — The Fountain Knight:**
  - Strange knight at Otmoor fountain kills all challengers; squire arrives with dead master's body
  - Young squire Griflet begs to fight; Arthur (reluctant) knights and sends him — Griflet badly wounded and returned
  - Arthur himself slips away to fight Pellinore; Arthur beaten; Excalibur from stone broken; Merlin saves Arthur with magic
  - Arthur receives Excalibur from Lady of the Lake (hand rises from misty lake)
  - **Glory:** 100 for witnessing Arthur's return with Excalibur

- **Late Spring — Balin and the Sword:**
  - Strange damsel arrives at court wearing sword at waist; claims only the best knight can draw it; all fail
  - A poor knight ("Sir Balin") draws it; prophetess warns "you will slay your best friend with that sword"
  - Balin refuses to return it: "I take the adventure God will ordain"
  - Lady of the Lake arrives; demands Balin's head (or the damsel's) as her boon — Arthur refuses
  - Balin enters, recognizes Lady of the Lake as his mother's killer; beheads her before anyone can stop him
  - Arthur enraged (Hospitality passion inspired); exiles Balin from court forever
  - Sir Lanceor pursues Balin; player hooks: arm and follow? (Energetic roll)
  - Lanceor found dead by roadside, spear through chest; his lady Colombe fell on his sword in grief
  - Sir Mark of Cornwall found eulogizing nearby; erects tomb (marble); Merlin prophecies two greatest knights will fight here
  - Tomb of Lanceor: inscribed "Here shall fight the two best knights in the world" (later: Lancelot and Tristram)

- **Early Summer:** Merlin and Lady Nineve travel north visiting kings with prophecies; take many newborn boys

- **Mid-Summer — Battle of Bassus River (southwest Malahaut):**

## Year 514 — Arthur and Guenever Wed; Round Table Founded

**Situation:** Arthur marries Guenever; receives Round Table from Leodegrance (150 seats, 100 knights). Chivalry required for Round Table membership from this year. Guenever first-meeting mechanic applies.

**Court:** Carlion. Special Guests: King Pellinore; Leodegrance's envoys (arranging marriage of Arthur and Guenever).

**Events:**

**King Arthur's Wedding:**
- Long Mass; wedding vows; gift exchange
- Every knight may ask for one gift (within reason); refusal = lose 1 Honor; asking appropriate gift (≈ annual income) = Just check; too little = Modest check + king gives extra up to that amount
- Old man asks Arthur to knight his son Tor — turns out Tor is Pellinore's son; Gawaine brothers whisper jealously
- **Glory:** 100 for witnessing coronation; 150 for participating

**The Spectacle (Feast):**
- White hart dashes into hall; white hound pursues; sixty black hounds burst through door
- White hound bites hart; man takes white hound and leaves
- Woman on white horse shouts — black knight on black charger grabs her and rides out
- Merlin insists Arthur send knights to resolve these marvels; canonical quests assigned to Gawaine (hart), Tor (brachet/white hound), Pellinore (lady)
- Days of tournament follow; each quest resolved and reported back

**The Guenever First-Meeting Mechanic:**

*Men (Lustful roll +10):*
| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical | Utterly smitten; Amor (Guenever) = 3d6+10 |
| Success | Attracted but knows it's futile; Amor (Guenever) = 3d6 |
| Failure | She's just not your type; no passion |
| Fumble | Something makes you mistrust her; Suspicion (Guenever) directed trait +2d6 |

*Note:* If existing Love/Amor passion > rolled number, no new passion gained (unless player wants). A knight may take Amor (Guenever) openly — seeking to be among her Queen's Knights as sincere admirer, not lover.

*Women (Trusting roll):*
| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical | Would do anything to serve her; Trusting (Guenever) directed trait +2d6 |
| Success | Great admiration; Trusting (Guenever) +1d3+1 |
| Failure | Beautiful but just another woman |
| Fumble | Feel threatened; Suspicion (Guenever) +1d3+1 |

**The Round Table:**
- Leodegrance's wedding gift: Round Table seating 150, plus 100 knights
- Merlin helps Arthur find 28 more; 22 filled by notables (Gawaine, Pellinore, Tor, etc.)
- Names appear in gold on seats magically

**Qualifying for Round Table:**
- Minimum 8,000 Glory
- Must be Chivalrous
- Must have served Arthur (battles, missions, loyalty)
- Arthur may appoint for his own reasons; even former enemies may qualify

**Early Appointees:** Bishop Baudwin of Britain, Sir Ector, Sir Brastias, King Alain of Escavalon (Sir Alain de Carlion), Cador of Cornwall

**Wedding Tournament:**
- Type: Regal Old-style; Logres vs non-Logres; rebated weapons; Love stakes (prove loyalty to Arthur)
- Several days long; notable events: Gawaine kills white hart but accidentally slays a lady who interposes herself
- Gawaine publicly shamed by Guenever; weeps; takes great oath to always help women (never breaks it)
- This inaugurates the new trend of honoring women

**Quelling Rebels:**
- Arthur sends small forces to quell unsurrendered vassals of slain lords
- Player hook: excellent opportunity for Glory and rewards

**Christmas Court:**
- Royal: References women's realm and honor; working with Guenever toward social ideals
- Salisbury: "Did you understand anything that lovesick king was talking about?"

**Special Winter Phase Modifiers:**
- All of Britain: –10 to Starting Bad Weather rolls (blessing of the king's wedding)

**If the player does nothing:** Arthur marries Guenever and the Round Table is founded regardless; the Guenever first-meeting mechanic still applies at next court attendance. Absent knights miss 100–150 Glory for the coronation/participation and cannot qualify for early Round Table appointment this year.

## Year 515 — Dolorous Stroke; Birth of the Wasteland

**Situation:** Arthur goes on 40-day pilgrimage in sackcloth for unstated reasons. Meanwhile, Sir Balin wounds King Pellam with the Grail Spear, creating the Wasteland. Sagremor arrives from Byzantium.

**Court:** Silchester. Special Guests: many lords and ladies; Sagremor, nephew of Byzantine Emperor.

**New Round Table Members:** King Pellinore.

**Intrigue:** King Idres of Cornwall has died; his son Mark is new king (great holdings in both Brittany and Cornwall).

**Events:**
- Arthur appears in sackcloth; goes on pilgrimage to shrine of St. Albans; remains 40 days in prayer
- Court says nothing about the reason (GM implication: guilt over his role in the infant deaths)

**The Dolorous Stroke (Hidden Event — not witnessed by players):**
- Source: Malory II, 12–15
- Sir Balin enters Listeneisse; finds his way to King Pellam's castle
- Invisible Knight strikes Balin; Balin kills the Invisible Knight — who turns out to be King Pellam's brother
- Pellam pursues Balin; Balin grabs a spear off a wall and strikes Pellam
- The spear is the Grail Spear (used to wound Christ); Pellam is wounded with a dolorous blow
- Castle shakes and falls; entire kingdom of Listeneisse is laid waste — becomes the Wasteland
- The Enchantment of Britain begins, radiating slowly across the whole island
- After ~515: Listeneisse/Lake District region is dead, empty, haunted; borders seem limitless inside due to Faerie

**The Death of Balin (Hidden Event):**
- Source: Malory II, 16–19
- Balin wanders from ruins; given new arms; told to fight "the Red Knight" at a ford (local custom)
- Balin fights and kills his own brother Balan — neither recognizes the other
- Both die; Merlin buries them in a great tomb

**GM Note:** Adventures from Chapter 5 (High Adventure in the Wastelands) can be run from this point forward.

**Christmas Court:**
- Royal: "Here we are, two years at peace. I will honor God with new churches."
- Salisbury: "The king has promised money for strengthening Sarum and other castles — need your input."

**Special Winter Phase Modifiers:** None.

**If the player does nothing:** The Dolorous Stroke occurs in secret regardless; the Wasteland begins spreading and the Enchantment of Britain is set in motion without any player involvement. Absent knights gain no Glory and miss the chance to witness Arthur's 40-day pilgrimage or aid in the castle-building programme.

**Court:** Carlion. Special Guests: Assembled lords of Britain. Many new Round Table members announced to fill table before Badon.

**Intrigue:** Messengers from Ganis — Ban and Bors being attacked by King Claudas of France; they ask for Arthur's men in return. Arthur cannot help now.

## Boy King Period Adventures

These adventures are appropriate for Years 510–518.

### Adventure: DOLOROUS GARDE
- **Setting:** Garloth
- **Hook:** Haunted castle of an evil undead enchanter. Hidden treasure. Many knights entered and never returned.
- **Guardians (30 total):** Lions; Celtic ghost knights (average knight stats, no Unconscious/Major Wound); copper giants (see Appendix 1); ghostly magician (GM-designed)
- **Special:** Huge rock in courtyard — STR roll at –25 to lift; only the fated lord (whose name will appear on it) can lift it
- **Secrets:** Canonically, Lancelot achieves this quest and renames it Joyous Garde. Players can contribute to his pre-success, attempt to beat him, or accumulate Glory here
- **Glory:** 100 to claim castle, plus any from guardians defeated

### Adventure: KNIGHT OF THE MOON
- **Setting:** Along the road
- **Characters:** Knight of the Moon (famous knight stats); famous bard standing by to satirize cowards
- **Hook:** Knight bars the road; demands a joust. Terms: loser pays 6 months' knight-service or £1 scutage starting next Pentecost
- **Secrets:** Knight is gathering an army to attack Castle Spidora where his imprisoned ladylove is held
- **Resolution:** Joust. Win = take the money and go. Lose = join army, pay, or renege (–1 Honor)
- **Glory:** 25 if you beat him; GM must design Battle of Spidora if players join him

### Adventure: KNIGHT OF THE WOLF
- **Setting:** Along the road
- **Characters:** Knight of the Wolf (famous knight stats); his Irish wolfhound; a dozen archers
- **Hook:** Terrified squire stumbling down road — his lord was killed by the Knight of the Wolf, daughter taken. Wolf knight uses hidden archers.
- **Secrets:** Knight of the Wolf has simply avenged his father, who was killed by the ambushed lord using hidden crossbowmen. Attack was dishonorable but motivated by Love (family).
- **Resolution:** If players parley before attacking, Knight reveals the truth. Daughter is unharmed; dead lord properly buried. Knight will release daughter if players recover his family heirloom — stolen by the Lord of the Ivy Tower (who must be defeated in single combat)
- **Glory:** 50 for not fighting and recovering the heirloom

### Adventure: CRUEL KNIGHT OF LANDOINE
- **Setting:** Along the road
- **Hook:** Knight carried in wagon; cannot heal his poisoned wounds until his attacker is slain. Merciful roll compels help; first volunteer without rolling gets a Just check.
- **Enemy:** Proud Knight of Landoine (Evil knight of similar Glory/skill to players; invokes Hate (Chivalrous knights) in battle)
- **Secrets:** His magical sword of poisoned steel: any Major Wound dealt by it can never heal while its wielder lives
- **Resolution:** Fight and kill the Proud Knight
- **Glory:** 150 + combat
- **Note:** Sword is a prize, but costs bearer a Cruelty check each use

### Adventure: BOOBRIE
- **Setting:** Lake or marshy area
- **Hook:** A boobrie is troubling the area
- **Resolution:** Must be hurt to half its total Hit Points to drive it off; it will then fly away
- **Glory:** 10 + combat

### Adventure: TOWER OF THE BRASS BELL
- **Setting:** Tower in hills near a chasm; only possible passage across
- **Characters:** A miserly dwarf porter who never leaves his tower
- **Hook:** Bridge costs £1 per traveler, paid via bucket; dwarf fishes for food from river far below
- **Resolution:** Pay to cross or besiege the dwarf
- **Glory:** None unless combat

### Adventure: THE LITTLE HARE
- **Setting:** Anywhere
- **Hook:** Player knight chasing a hare; hare hides under robes of a praying nun; nun asks knight to spare the creature
- **Resolution:** Merciful roll; befriend the nun = she offers abbey hospitality
- **Secrets:** Nun may be a saint-in-training who can miraculously stop hostile action — knight might gain Fear (nuns) 3d6
- **Glory:** 10 for not killing the hare

### Adventure: CASTLE OF THE BEARD
- **Setting:** Castle near a road
- **Hook:** Three knights demand the beard of every passing man; if refused, they attack and cut it off (1d3 damage)
- **Characters:** Lord of Castle of the Beard (notable knight); three knights (average knight stats)
- **Resolution:** Defeat three knights; lord shuts gates if his knights are defeated; must then defeat the lord
- **Glory:** 100 for defeating the lord and stopping the custom forever

### Adventure: GOTHAM
- **Setting:** Gotham (town near Nottingham)
- **Hook:** Players need lodging or supplies
- **Action:** Townspeople refuse cooperation; act completely insane (rake sun/moon reflection from lake; try to drown eels; roll cheeses to market; stand in circle around cuckoo in a bush)
- **Secrets:** Villagers are feigning madness so noblemen won't demand labor from them
- **Resolution:** None — purely an encounter
- **Glory:** 10 for witnessing the "madness"
