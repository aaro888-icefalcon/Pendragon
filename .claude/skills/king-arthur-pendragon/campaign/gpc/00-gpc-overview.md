# Great Pendragon Campaign — GM Overview

> **Scope of this file:** How the GPC is structured, GM advice, system rules expansions, and the full period/year index. Year-by-year entries are in the period files (01–04).

---

## Campaign Structure

The GPC runs **485–566 AD**, divided into eight periods. Each year has a fixed annual framework the GM fills in. A different agent handles 531 onward — this set covers only 485–530.

### The Eight Periods

| # | Period | Years | Theme |
|---|--------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Uther | 485–495 | Legitimacy, Saxon threat, Merlin's schemes |
| 2 | Anarchy | 496–509 | No king; survival, petty wars, roads not safe |
| 3 | Boy King | 510–518 | Arthur proves himself; Round Table founded; Badon |
| 4 | Conquest | 519–530 | Pax Arthur; French/Roman wars; Lancelot arrives |
| 5 | Romance | 531–539 | Courts of Love; Courtly culture peaks |
| 6 | Tournament | 540–553 | TDL system; knights seek glory via jousting |
| 7 | Grail Quest | 554–557 | Galahad; the Grail achieved |
| 8 | Twilight | 558–566 | Civil war; Arthur's fall |

## Annual Entry Format

Every year uses this structure (not all sections appear every year):

- **Court** — where held, who attends
- **Special Guests** — notable visitors
- **New Round Table Members** — named knights added
- **Gossip** — female courtier chatter (social tone; rumor hooks)
- **News** — male courtier reports (Kay, Brastias, Gawaine, etc.)
- **Royal Conversation** — Arthur's public policy announcement
- **Intrigue** — hidden backstory for GM
- **Events** — canonical happenings; includes battles, arrivals, crises
- **Adventures** — specific scenarios with hooks and Glory
- **Christmas Court** — Royal and Salisbury statements
- **Special Winter Phase Modifiers** — land condition dice adjustments

## How to Run the GPC

### Core Principle
The GPC is a **framework**, not a script. The year entries give canonical events; player knights act within them, around them, or against them. The world advances whether or not players engage.

### Using the Annual Framework
1. **Open court:** describe location, guests, atmosphere
2. **Gossip/News:** deliver NPC lines verbatim or paraphrase — they seed hooks
3. **Royal Conversation:** Arthur's speech establishes the year's political theme
4. **Intrigue:** share only what characters could know; rest is GM-only
5. **Events/Adventures:** run in any order that makes dramatic sense
6. **Christmas Court:** wrap up; apply winter phase modifiers

### The "If Players Do Nothing" Default
Every year, canonical events occur regardless of player action. If players ignore a battle, their side still wins (as written). If they skip an adventure, an NPC handles it. The GPC world is not player-dependent — player involvement adds Glory and personal stakes, not plot progression.

### Compressing Time
Not every year needs a full session. Years with no battles and generic adventures can be resolved in 30 minutes: deliver court gossip, ask one or two questions, fast-forward to Christmas. Reserve full sessions for battle years and pivotal events (Badon, Lancelot's arrival, Bran's Head).

### Glory Economy
Glory is the primary character advancement currency. Major sources:
- Battles: per-round Glory × victory multiplier
- Adventure completion: listed flat awards
- Round Table membership: 1,000 Glory (first year), annual fee thereafter
- Decisive Victory multiplier: ×2
- Indecisive: ×1
- Defeat: ×0

### Player Knights and Major NPCs
The GPC assumes player knights are background figures to canonical heroes (Gawaine, Lancelot, Percival). Player knights can:
- Participate in all battles at their unit
- Be involved in any adventure as supporting cast or leads
- Occasionally take a canonical hero's role if Gamemaster chooses
- Never replace canonical story outcomes (Lancelot will achieve Dolorous Garde; Gawaine will behead Gainus; etc.)

## System Rules Expansions (Used Across All Periods)

### Battle System
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Battle Size | Small (<100), Medium (100–499), Large (500–2000), Huge (2000+) |
| Commander Battle skill | Replaces Unit Events Table modifier if commander is in player's unit |
| Glory per round | Listed per battle; multiply by victory type at end |
| Decisive Victory | ×2 Glory multiplier |
| Indecisive Victory | ×1 |
| Plunder | Listed per battle in £ and horses |

**Battle Round Sequence:** Roll on Unit Events Table (modified by Commander skill and situational modifiers) → resolve personal combat vs. random enemy → apply wounds.

### Tournament Difficulty Level (TDL)
Used for solo jousts and melees at tournaments. The GM sets the TDL by event size and prestige. Consult Pendragon rulebook Appendix 4 for full table.

| Leisurely | 20 | 15 | 12 | 8 |
| Normal | 30 | 20 | 15 | 10 |

### Forest Hunting Modifiers
Each named British forest has a modifier to Hunting rolls. Refer to Pendragon core for specific forest table.

### Madness Activity Table (d20)
Roll when a knight goes mad. Results include: wanders roads, attacks companions, strips naked, howls at moon, etc. Full table in Pendragon Appendix.

### Double d20 System
For undefined opposed rolls: both parties roll 1d20, add relevant skill; higher total wins. Used when no specific opposed roll mechanic exists.

### Ties in Combat
If both combatants roll equal results on opposed combat: both take half damage, rounded down. No knockdown check.

### The Grudge Score
Running tally of Saxon grievances against the player knights' region. Increases from raids, treaties broken, insults. High scores mean more severe Saxon attention during Anarchy Period.

### Round Table Qualification
To be eligible: minimum 8,000 Glory + Chivalrous trait dominant + must have served Arthur directly. GM awards seats based on canonical schedule plus extraordinary deeds.

### Guenever First-Meeting Mechanic
On first meeting Queen Guenever, player knights make trait checks:
- **Lustful success:** Instantly smitten; passion Amor (Guenever) formed at 2d6+6
- **Chaste success:** Admire platonically; no passion formed
- **Amor formed:** Fuels Courtly Romance attempts
- **Trusting success:** Trust her judgments readily; useful for queen's missions

### The Favor System
Favors are an immaterial currency of feudal obligation. Granting a favor creates a debt the recipient must repay with equivalent service. Favors accumulate in court. High-ranking favors (from kings) can unlock resources, titles, or missions. Track on character sheet.

### Companions of King Arthur (from 522)
- Voluntary annual oath at Pentecost
- Benefits: 100 Glory on joining; golden crown/red griffin badge; network of contacts across Logres and Cambria
- Obligations: maintain honor, keep word, support Arthur's ideals
- Not a substitute for Round Table — no seat, no formal rank

### Chivalric Siege (from 521)
Arthur decrees: within his realm, sieges must follow these rules:
- May isolate a castle (starvation tactics allowed)
- May assault, but cannot kill non-combatants or destroy income sources
- May offer individual combat to resolve battle
- Besieged surrenders if unrelieved within 90 days
- Surrendered city pays plunder tax = 1/5 total city value

### Changeling Adventures
Faerie encounters involving switched children, glamoured knights, or gentry in disguise. No fixed mechanic — use Faerie Lore and Awareness rolls. Outcomes: rescue, trapping in Faerie, gaining fey allies.
