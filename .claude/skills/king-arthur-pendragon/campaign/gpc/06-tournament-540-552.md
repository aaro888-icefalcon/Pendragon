# GPC Chapter 8: Tournament Period (540–552)

**Situation:** Arthur's reign reaches its full glory. The Round Table is complete, the tournaments are elaborate and regular, and the Enchantment of Britain grows stronger. This is the most prosperous peacetime era of the campaign. However, the Grail begins to impinge on consciousness, the Wastelands spread, and cracks appear in the fabric of Camelot.

**Key Mechanics This Period:**
- Elegant-style tournaments (pageantry, Courts of Love, poetry)
- Blood tournaments (Mordred's innovation in the north)
- Gothic Plate not yet available (comes in Twilight Period)
- Romance difficulty levels increase
- Wasteland tracking becomes important for holdings
- Arthur's physical decline visible through stat blocks

## Year 540 — Grail Vision / Seeking the King

**Situation:** The Holy Grail is seen in a vision at Pentecost court. Many knights go questing in response, but it is premature — the Grail Quest is not yet at hand. The king is troubled and announces a quest to find him champions.

**Court:** Pentecost at Camelot. The vision occurs during the feast.

**Events:**
- During Pentecost feast, the hall fills with sweet odor and golden light; every knight sees what he most desires — the Grail floats above the table for one heartbeat, then vanishes
- Sir Gawaine swears to seek the Grail for one year
- Arthur grieves: "You have bereft me of the fairest fellowship that ever was in Christian realm"
- Dozens of Round Table knights depart on quests
- Castle of Annowre adventure begins

**Adventures:**

### SEEKING THE KING / HYENAS (540–541)

**Source:** GPC original
**Hook:** While the king is absent on a hunt, a rumor arrives that he has not returned to camp. Player knights must find him.

**Scene 1:** King Arthur hunted deep in the forest and did not return — his escort is scattered
**Scene 2:** Tracks lead to a clearing; hyena spoor (foul-smelling waste everywhere)
**Scene 3:** Hyenas are herding the king toward their lair (paralyzing circle tactic)

**Hyena Stats:**
```
Glory Won 75
SIZ 30, DEX 10, STR 15, CON 15
Move 8; Armor 8
Unconscious 12; Major Wound 15; Knockdown 30; Hit Points 45; Healing Rate 3; Damage 5d6
Combat Skills: Bite 18
Paralyzing Circle: Win 2 successive opposed DEX rolls (one per round) to encircle victim; three encirclements = paralysis
Note: Cannot turn around — must reverse entire body
```

**Outcome:** Rescue the king: 50 Glory. Slay each hyena: 75 Glory.

### CASTLE OF ANNOWRE (541)

**Source:** Malory (adapted)
**Time:** 541
**Hook:** King Arthur is lured to the castle of the enchantress Annowre, who desires him. She has captured him by magic. Player knights must rescue him.

**Scenes:**
1. **The Lure** — Arthur follows a white stag into the forest of Listeneisse and disappears
2. **Annowre's Trap** — She has bound him with magic (Enchantment 18 vs. Arthur's Pious 15 — she wins)
3. **Castle Approach** — DV 20 castle; gates open (illusion of welcome)
4. **The Great Hall** — Annowre offers the players hospitality; she is testing their resolve
5. **Breaking the Enchantment** — A holy symbol or Pious 15+ roll breaks the binding; Arthur is freed

**Annowre the Enchantress:**
```
SIZ 10, DEX 15, STR 9, CON 14, APP 21
Move 4; Armor 0 (magical protection counts as 10)
Unconscious –; Major Wound 14; Knockdown 10; Hit Points 24; Healing Rate 3
Magic: Enchantment 18, Glamour 20, Emotion 15
Note: She cannot be fought directly; she must be outmaneuvered or her magic broken first
```

**Aftermath:**
- Annowre destroyed (her magic depends on Arthur remaining captive)
- Glory: 450 for freeing the king + 50 for destroying Annowre's power

**If the player does nothing:** Dozens of Round Table knights depart on premature Grail quests regardless; Arthur remains missing until someone rescues him from Annowre. Absent knights gain no Glory from the Hyena encounter or the Castle of Annowre rescue, and Arthur remains captive longer — weakening the court.

## Year 541 — Tristram Arrives / Tournaments

**Situation:** Sir Tristram of Lyonesse arrives at Camelot and is acclaimed greatest knight after Lancelot. His love for Isolt (King Mark's wife) becomes the most famous scandal of the court.

**Court:** Pentecost at Camelot. Tristram presented; joins Round Table.

**Events:**
- Tristram arrives from Cornwall; Lancelot refuses to joust him (recognizes his equal)
- Tristram is invited to join Round Table; his chair appears without needing to wait for vacancy
- Mark's jealousy noted; Cornish knights begin surveillance of Tristram
- Palomides (Saracen knight) also arrives; his unrequited love for Isolt provides counterpoint

**Tournament Schedule (541):**
- Pentecost: Classic style, Regal; prize = gilded sword
- July: Neighborhood (2), Local (armor)
- August: Regional at York (ransom wager)
- September: Neighborhood, Local (gear+steed)
- October: Neighborhood (love), Local (armor), Regional at Dorchester

**If the player does nothing:** Tristram joins the Round Table and his chair appears without needing a vacancy regardless; Mark's jealousy is noted at court. Absent knights gain no Glory from the tournament circuit and miss the chance to joust against or alongside the newly acclaimed Tristram.

## Year 543–545 — Stable Years / Tourneys / Romances

**Situation:** Three years of relative prosperity and tournament activity. The Wasteland continues expanding in the north; court life is refined and elaborate.

**Events (each year):**
- Full tournament circuit (see schedule format from 541)
- Amorous adventures continue (use Ten Steps to Love system from Ch. 7)
- Mordred's "Blood Tournaments" begin in the north (real weapons, full ransom)

**Blood Tournament Rules:**
- Real weapons (not rebated); full combat rules apply
- Glory is NOT divided by 10 (full combat glory for each round)
- Stakes: full ransom (winner takes 1/4 loser's annual income)
- Risk: Major Wounds are real; death possible

**Adventures:**

### BESSINGER INHERITANCE

**Source:** GPC original
**Hook:** A family dispute over land inheritance has escalated into a legal matter before the king. Sir Bessinger recently died; his widow and his brother both claim the estate.

**Scenes:**
1. **The Legal Hearing** — At court; player knights may serve as witnesses or champions
2. **Church vs. Estate** — The bishop claims the land should revert to the church under a prior grant
3. **Discovery** — The prior grant is forged; who did it and why?
4. **Resolution** — Combat trial (if no legal resolution) or negotiated settlement

**Key Skills:** Intrigue, Law (Pendragon skills), Recognize (to spot the forgery)

**Outcomes:**
- Expose forgery: 50 Glory
- Serve as champion in trial by combat: 50 Glory + combat vs. church champion
- Broker settlement: 25 Glory + relationship with Bessinger family

**If the player does nothing:** The full tournament circuit runs and Mordred's Blood Tournaments begin in the north regardless; the Bessinger inheritance dispute is settled by other champions or drags on. Absent knights gain no tournament Glory for these three years and miss amorous adventure progress from the Ten Steps system.

## Year 546 — The Grail Procession / Morgan's Plots

**Situation:** Several knights witness partial Grail processions — but from a distance; the time is not yet right. Morgan le Fay's plots against Arthur intensify.

**Court:** Pentecost at Camelot. Reports of Grail sightings discussed.

**Events:**
- Three knights separately report seeing "a castle of light" and a procession with bleeding lance
- Morgan sends Arthur a poisoned mantle (Guenever discovers the plot; maiden burned instead)
- Lancelot is captured by Morgan's enchantment; freed after several weeks

**Adventures:** Morgan's mantle plot can be a court intrigue adventure:
- Intrigue vs. 15 to spot the suspiciously perfect gift
- If player knights alert Guenever before Arthur dons it: 50 Glory
- If they do nothing and Guenever catches it: 25 Glory (for witnessing)

**If the player does nothing:** Guenever catches the poisoned mantle plot regardless and the maiden is burned; Lancelot is freed from Morgan's enchantment after several weeks. Absent knights gain only 25 Glory (witnessing) rather than 50, and Lancelot remains captive with no one to investigate on Arthur's behalf.

## Year 547–548 — Troubles in the North

**Situation:** Brian of the Isles raids the northern coast. The Wasteland spreads further. Arthur sends expeditions north.

**Events:**
- Brian's fleet raids Lothian coast
- Saxon unrest in Anglia
- Mordred returns briefly to court; his charm is noted by older knights who knew him as a child

## Year 549 — Tristram's Exile

**Situation:** King Mark finally acts against Tristram. He sends Tristram into exile from Cornwall with a false charge of treason.

**Events:**
- Mark accuses Tristram before his barons; Tristram departs rather than fight his uncle
- Tristram wanders as a knight errant for a year
- Eventually settles in Brittany briefly (meets Isolt of the White Hands, but does not fall in love — his passion for Isolt of Ireland is too strong)
- Player knights may encounter Tristram on the road (famous knight stats, Glory Won 1,000+)

**Adventures:** Road encounters with Tristram. If player knight defeats Tristram in joust: 500 Glory (unlikely — Sword 28, Lance 27 stats).

**If the player does nothing:** Mark exiles Tristram regardless and he wanders as a knight errant; Isolt of the White Hands fails to capture his heart. Absent knights miss all road encounters with the wandering Tristram and the exceptional 500-Glory joust opportunity.

## Year 550–551 — Stone House Visit

**Situation:** A peculiar two-year adventure involving a mysterious stone house on the edge of the Wastelands.

**Court:** Pentecost 550 at Camelot. Pentecost 551 at Camelot.

**Adventures:**

### STONE HOUSE VISIT (550–551)

**Source:** GPC original
**Time:** 550 (visit), 551 (payoff)
**Hook:** Player knights find a stone house on a hilltop near the Wasteland border. The door bears a red-and-yellow mystic sign. No one is home — but supplies are laid out for guests.

**Year 550 — First Visit:**
- Awareness vs. 15: Notice strange items inside (engineering tools, alchemical equipment)
- Heraldry vs. 15: Recognize the mystic sign (unknown; note it)
- Lady Melia: Beautiful, gracious hostess who appears at dusk; leaves at dawn
- She offers food and hospitality; asks news of the outside world

**Lady Melia (NPC):**
- Mysterious woman; actually a magical guardian of the stone house's secrets
- APP 19, Courtesy 20, Intrigue 18
- She knows about the red-and-yellow sign but will not explain unless asked correctly (Honest roll vs. her Deceitful 15)

**Banneret Cesar Stone (NPC):**
```
SIZ 12, DEX 13, STR 13, CON 20, APP 11
Move 3; Armor 14 + shield
Unconscious 8; Major Wound 20; Knockdown 12; Hit Points 32; Healing Rate 5; Damage 4d6
Glory 4,380
Combat Skills: Bow 14, Dagger 10, Lance 17, Spear 15, Sword 18; Battle 12, Horsemanship 17
Note: Cesar Stone is the owner of the stone house; he is away at war but returns in 551
```

**Year 551 — Return Visit / Payoff:**
- If player knights return, Cesar Stone is present
- He explains: the stone house contains a primitive cannon (the "Infernal Engine" technology)
- The red-and-yellow sign marks the gunpowder carts
- **CRITICAL GM NOTE:** This knowledge becomes essential in Year 555 (CORNISH INVASION) — when player knights recognize the sign on King Mark's war machine, they know how to destroy it (fire the powder carts)

**Glory:** 50 for first visit (meeting Lady Melia). 50–150 for second visit depending on how much information they extract.

**If the player does nothing:** The stone house stands on the Wasteland border and Cesar Stone returns in 551 regardless; the red-and-yellow gunpowder sign goes unlearned. Absent knights gain no Glory and, critically, will not recognize the Infernal Engine's powder carts during the Cornish Invasion of 555.

## Year 552 — Loholt's Death / Gilded Box

**Situation:** Arthur's illegitimate son Loholt is murdered by Sir Kay (in a fit of jealous rage). This terrible secret is hidden for a time.

**Court:** Pentecost at Camelot. Arthur's physical decline is obvious — he looks 10 years older than his age.

**Events:**
- Sir Loholt (Arthur's son by Lisanor) was on a quest; he killed a great giant
- Sir Kay found Loholt exhausted after the fight, slew him, and cut off the giant's head to claim the kill
- The head is brought to court; Kay is celebrated
- Loholt's body eventually found; the truth comes out

**Adventures:**

### GILDED BOX (548–552)

**Source:** GPC original
**Time:** Event in 548; revelation in 552
**Hook:** A gilded box arrives at court (delivered by a young woman). It contains Loholt's severed head. The woman is Loholt's companion; she witnessed Kay kill him.

**Scene 1 — The Box:** Awareness roll to notice something wrong with the "gift." Recognize vs. 15 to identify Loholt.

**Scene 2 — Investigation:** The woman tells her story; player knights must verify it against Kay's account.
- Intrigue vs. Kay's Intrigue 18 to find inconsistencies
- Heraldry vs. 15 to match the giant's head with known kills

**Scene 3 — Confrontation:** Kay denies it. How do the player knights handle this?
- Challenge Kay to trial by combat (he refuses — loses Honor)
- Present evidence to Arthur (he is devastated; cannot act against Kay without proof)

**Scene 4 — Revelation:** In 552, a dying villain confesses to helping Kay dispose of the body. Truth comes fully to light.

**Glory:** 50 for discovering the truth in 548–550. 100 for forcing a public reckoning in 551. 150 for bringing it to full resolution in 552.

**If the player does nothing:** Kay's crime is eventually exposed by the dying villain's confession in 552 regardless; Arthur is devastated but cannot act without proof. Absent knights gain no Glory from the investigation and cannot force a public reckoning earlier, leaving Kay celebrated and Loholt unmourned for longer.

## Year 553 — Final Year of Tournament Period / Grail's Shadow

**Situation:** The last full year before the Grail Quest. The Wastelands cover much of Britain. Tournament activity is reduced by the visible desolation in the countryside. Galahad is born this year (raised in secret by King Pellam).

**Court:** Pentecost at Camelot. Unusually subdued; many chairs at the Round Table are empty (knights dead or on quests).

**Events:**
- Wasteland now covers: Listeneisse (entirely), Escavalon (partly), parts of Norgales and Logres
- Galahad born (will appear in 554)
- Merlin's disappearance now confirmed (enchanted by Nimue in the Forest of Brioceliande)
- Nimue of the Lake takes Merlin's role as magical adviser to Arthur

**Adventures:**

### CASTLE OF THE BLACK HERMIT (any 540–553)

**Source:** GPC original
**Hook:** A curse has been placed on a castle; its lord is dying; only a knight of pure heart can break it.

**Pious Mechanics:**
- The Black Hermit's curse can only be broken by a knight with Pious 15+
- Each scene requires a Pious roll; failure means the curse "pushes back" and the knight suffers 1d6 damage
- Critical Pious success on the final confrontation ends the curse permanently

**The Curse Rules:**
| Pious Level | Effect in Castle |
|-------------|-----------------|
| <10 | Cannot enter; repelled at gate |
| 10–14 | Can enter; take 1d6 damage per scene |
| 15–19 | Can enter; damage reduced to 1d3 per scene |
| 20+ | Immune to curse damage; full effectiveness |

**Scenes:**
1. **Approach** — The castle's fields are blighted; serfs are mindless
2. **Entry** — Wraith-knights guard the gate (fight or Pious roll to pass)
3. **The Lord** — Dying in his hall; can tell the story of the curse
4. **The Black Hermit's Lair** — Beneath the castle; he must be confronted and defeated or driven out
5. **Breaking the Curse** — Pious 15+ roll; then destroy the hermit's talisman

**Wraith-Knight (each one):**
```
Glory Won 25
SIZ 14, DEX 12, STR 15, CON — (undead)
Move 3; Armor 12 (ghostly mail)
Unconscious –; Major Wound –; Knockdown 14; Hit Points 27; Healing Rate 0
Damage 5d6
Combat Skills: Sword 15
Note: Only holy weapons or Pious 15+ knight can damage them; mundane weapons pass through
```

**Outcomes:** Break the curse: 200 Glory. Defeat the Black Hermit: 200 Glory. Fail: curse continues; lord dies; holding becomes Wasteland.

**If the player does nothing:** The Wasteland now covers Listeneisse entirely and parts of Escavalon and Norgales regardless; Galahad is born and kept in secret by King Pellam. Absent knights gain no Glory from the Castle of the Black Hermit or Boiling Girl adventures, and the cursed lord's holding tips into Wasteland adding to next year's spread roll.

### BOILING GIRL

**Source:** GPC original
**Hook:** A beautiful woman is imprisoned in a vat of boiling water by an enchantress. Only a knight of exceptional strength can pull her out.

**The Mechanic:**
- STR roll vs. STR 25 to pull her free (the magical current holds her)
- Each failed attempt: 1d6 damage to the pulling knight (scalding water)
- Critical success: frees her in one pull; 175 Glory
- Normal success: frees her after 3 rounds; 100 Glory
- Failure: cannot free her; enchantress must be defeated first (Enchantment 15 duel vs. Pious)

**Aftermath:** The freed woman is a noblewoman who grants the knight a magical boon (choose one):
- A sword that deals +2d6 vs. enchanted beings
- A ring of Healing Rate +2
- Knowledge of one secret of Arthur's court (GM's choice)

## Tournament Period Summary: Glory Benchmarks

By end of 553, a player knight who engaged actively should have accumulated:

| Activity | Cumulative Glory |
|----------|-----------------|
| Tournament wins (average 1/year) | 1,000–2,000 |
| Participation in major adventures | 500–1,500 |
| Holding and manor income (Glory for prosperity) | 200–500 |
| Court attendance and service | 100–300 |
| **Estimated total added this Period** | **1,800–4,300** |

A knight with 5,000+ total Glory is now Famous. A knight with 10,000+ is Extraordinary.
