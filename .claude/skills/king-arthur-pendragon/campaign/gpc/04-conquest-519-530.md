# Conquest Period: 519–530
## GPC Chapter 6

> **Period Theme:** Arthur expands beyond Britain, defeating France and Rome. Lancelot arrives and establishes his supremacy. Camelot is built. The Companions of Arthur system and Chivalric Siege rules are introduced. Courtly romance begins to emerge.

---

## Period Overview

**Historical analog:** Henry V of England / Agincourt era. Arthur = British conqueror who becomes Roman Emperor.

**Politics:** Britain united; Roman Empire demands tribute; Arthur refuses and counterattacks. He ultimately conquers France, marches through Italy, and is anointed Emperor by the Pope in Rome (~Year 527).

**What's New This Period:**
- Armor: Reinforced chainmail (12-point) + closed helm now common on all but poorest knights
- Weapons: Medium crossbows available mid-period (military weapons, not peasant gear)
- Horses: Andalusian chargers available (only wealthy knights / high-favor service to lieges)
- Camelot: Site selected Year 520 (Caer Gwent / Winchester); construction begins; Arthur holds court there from Year 521
- Companions of Arthur: Instituted Year 522; annual swearing at Pentecost; 100 Glory; golden crown badge
- Chivalric Siege rules: Instituted Year 521 after Fort Guinnon sacking
- Round Table: Remains elite; Arthur does not appoint new members while in foreign lands (526–527)

**Andalusian Charger (Conquest Period):**
SIZ 36, DEX 17, STR 32, CON 12; Move 8; Armor 5; Unconscious 12; Major Wound 12; Knockdown 36; HP 48; Healing Rate 4; Damage 7d6. Larger than normal charger; deals more damage on lance charge. Found primarily among notable French knights or those who adventure in France.

## Companions of King Arthur (Year 522+)

**Instituted:** Pentecost, Year 522. Annual ceremony each Pentecost thereafter.

**Oath:** Uphold justice, be chivalrous, obey the king. "Arthur's man" even if serving another liege.

**Membership:** Entirely voluntary. College of examiners eventually created. Generally open to any knight with a general desire to act chivalrously and no reputation for churlishness or dastardly deeds.

**Benefits:**
- 100 Glory for joining (extra 100 if among the first group in Year 522)
- Badge: small gold crown surrounding a small red griffin (not on coat of arms without herald permission)
- Network: Companions may more easily gain assistance and contacts throughout Logres, Cambria, Cumbria; less effective among Saxons; not recognized by Picts

**Caution:** Being a Companion is sufficient cause for many knights to become enemies.

## Chivalric Siege Rules (Year 521+)

Arthur declares these rules apply to all wars between his subjects:

1. A besieging army may isolate a castle by siege, seeking victory through starvation
2. A besieging army may assault with full force, but **may not** kill non-combatants; may not burn buildings, crop fields, orchards, vineyards, or other sources of income
3. Besieger or besieged may offer individual combat to resolve the battle; combatants agree to obey chivalric rules
4. The besieged agrees to surrender if not relieved within **90 days** after siege is fully enforced; encroachment is determined by the besieged
5. A surrendered city pays a "plunder tax" equal to **1/5th** the total value of the city at moment of surrender (royal treasury workers determine value)

## Guenever's Rules of Courtly Romance (Year 529)

*(Issued at Christmas court; apply to Queen's Knights candidates and courtly pursuit)*

1. A lover will avoid greed and will be generous
2. A lover will keep himself chaste for the sake of her whom he loves
3. A lover will not knowingly strive to break up a correct love affair that someone else is engaged in
4. A lover will not chose for his love anyone whom a natural sense of shame forbids him to marry
5. A lover will avoid falsehood
6. A lover will not tell many about his love affair
7. A lover will be completely obedient to his lady
8. A lover will always work in the service of Love
9. A lover will always be modest
10. A lover will speak no evil
11. A lover will not reveal others' love affairs
12. A lover will be polite and courteous at all times
13. A lover will never exceed the desires of his lover

*To pursue a lady: have squire carry message seeking to meet in the Queen's Garden on Venus' Day (Friday). Men must be bathed and groomed.*

## Year 519 — Badon Aftermath; Alclud and Loch Lomond

**Situation:** Badon's conquered territory is divided. Saxons of Anglia rebel immediately. Irish and Picts attack the north, believing Arthur too weak after Badon losses.

**Court:** London. Special Guests: Many de Ganis refugees (King Ban and Bors killed in Ganis by King Claudas of France; family scattered).

**New Round Table Members:** Sagremor le Desirous; Dodinas le Sauvage; the Brown Knight of the Wilds; White Knight offered seat — refuses ("The Holy Grail is my lady") — first ever to refuse.

**Anglia Uprising:** Saxons kill tax collectors; rob merchants; Duke Hervis clamps down viciously.

## Year 521 — Camelot First Court; Morgan Plots Arthur's Death

**Situation:** Arthur holds court at Caer Gwent (future Camelot) for the first time. Morgan le Fay plots to kill Arthur via Sir Accolon, and then via a poisoned cloak. Chivalric Siege instituted.

**Court:** Caer Gwent (future Camelot). Special Guests: King Uriens, Queen Morgan le Fay, their son Sir Yvaine.

**New Round Table Members:** Gaheris de Orkney.

**Royal Conversation — Chivalric Siege announced:**
- Plunder of commoners forbidden; soldiers must be paid wages only
- War between Arthur's own vassals must follow Chivalric Siege rules (see system above)

**Events:**

**The King Is Missing!**
- Arthur goes hunting; fails to return; King Uriens also lost but appears in chamber next morning knowing nothing
- Morgan le Fay responsible; uses magic to confuse everyone in the woods; Arthur spends miserable night in forest
- All knights sent to search

**At the Castle of Sir Damas:**
- Arthur battles Sir Accolon, who is armed with Excalibur (Morgan switched swords)
- Accolon is killed; Arthur badly wounded but victorious; taken to nearby monastery
- Player Hook: Lady of the Lake's role in the rescue can be taken up by player characters

**Pursuit of Morgan:**
- Morgan steals Excalibur's scabbard from monastery while Arthur sleeps; Arthur pursues
- Morgan and entourage disappear into a field of standing stones (she turns them into stones)
- Arthur realizes this is the first prophecy of eagle Eliwlod: "You will almost die, and then find yourself lost among a field of stones"

**The Cloak:**
- Maiden arrives from Morgan bearing beautiful cloak; begs Arthur's forgiveness
- Lady of the Lake advises Arthur to make maiden try it on first; maiden immediately bursts into flame and dies
- Arthur repeats banishment of Morgan to eternal exile; all her lands declared forfeit

**Aftermath:**
- King Uriens swears he knew nothing; Arthur restates trust; but banishes Yvaine for being Morgan's son (Arbitrary check)
- Gawaine protests injustice; departs with Yvaine during "unfair exile"
- Lady of the Lake later explains Morgan's entire scheme; reveals Merlin is not dead but sleeping at enchanted pool

**Autumn: The Triple Questers**
- Sir Gawaine, Sir Yvaine, and Sir Marhaus (famous Irish knight) quest in Forest of Arroy; each goes with a maiden; agree to meet in a year

**Galeholt Leads Dal Riada:** Galeholt (son of exiled Sir Bruenor; long-time Arthur vassal; now wealthy) invades Dal Riada with Cambrian archers + Malahaut mercenaries + Irish warriors; defeats King Gilmaurius; begins building feudal kingdom

**Pictish Campaign:** Sir Griflet leads army through Pictland mountains — tremendously unsuccessful against guerilla warfare; Cador may depart; Griflet takes over

**Christmas Court:**
- Royal: "We are alarmed by Morgan's treachery. But the realm remains blessed and good."
- Salisbury: "Content."

**Special Winter Phase Modifiers:** None.

**If the player does nothing:** Morgan's plot to kill Arthur via Accolon and the poisoned cloak is foiled by the Lady of the Lake regardless; Arthur banishes Morgan and reinstates Uriens. Absent knights gain no Glory from the search for the king and cannot serve as the Lady of the Lake's instrument in the rescue.

## Year 522 — Companions of Arthur Instituted; May Babies Return; Lancelot Born Named Tristram

**Situation:** Companions of Arthur formally instituted. The "May Babies" — children Merlin took from northern lords — are returned. Merlin has a final farewell at court.

**Court:** Camelot. Special Guests: Lady of the Lake.

**Events:**

**Companions of King Arthur (first swearing):**
- Player knights may join; 100 Glory extra for being among first group
- See Companions system above

**Autumn: Return of the May Babies**
- French merchant arrives at court with time-worn letter and 24 ten-year-old boys in homespun livery
- Letter (to the Pope): Children of King Lot and Margawse; given to fisherman after being found aboard wrecked ship
- One boy wears hat and cloak; others look to him before acting
- Arthur welcomes them; sends messengers to Queen Margawse

- In Lyonesse, wicked stepmother tries to poison King's young son; plot revealed by boy's cleverness; king threatens to kill wife; boy begs mercy for her; king relieved; sends boy to his vassals in Brittany for safety

## Year 523 — Merlin Truly Gone; Silchester Rebellion Begins

**Situation:** Merlin appears briefly, says a final farewell to Arthur, and is truly gone (imprisoned by Nimue). Arthur sends all knights to search for Merlin, in vain. Silchester rebels.

**Court:** Camelot. Special Guests: Sirs Gawaine, Yvaine, and Marhaus (returned from Triple Quest); mothers of the returned children.

**New Round Table Members:** Sir Marhaus (Champion of Leicester); The White Knight.

**Events:**
- Merlin appeared briefly (in disguise as "beggar" entertainer with masked polecat; Gawaine threw bones at him); came to say farewell to Arthur — then truly gone
- Arthur sends all knights to search; Gawaine/King Brandegoris hears Merlin's voice: he has been imprisoned in an invisible tower by his lover Nimue, unable to return
- This is Merlin's real, final departure — no tricks this time

**Isle of Fees discovered:** See adventure below.

**The Grail Templars:**
- White Knight visits Camelot; seeks volunteers for Temple of the Holy Grail — brotherhood binding knightly chivalry to monastic virtues (poverty, chastity, obedience)
- Mission: ride roads and guard pilgrims
- Arthur realizes this is the third prophecy of eagle Eliwlod: "A white knight will also be a monk"

**Silchester Rebellion:**
- New Count of Silchester (Uffo, son of Ulfius) claims Arthur infringes his rights; defies king; closes roads to Pendragon friends
- Sir Griflet lays siege (Chivalric Siege — no assault)
- Sons of Ulfius refuse personal combat; winter comes before settled; count agrees to surrender if he can go free; Griflet refuses; siege lifted when winter too cold; supplies brought to starving city

**Galeholt Crowned:** Great court at Carrickfergus; crowned by Saint Patrick's heir as King of Dal Riada and Duke of the Long Isles; holds small tournament; begins building castles

**Christmas Court:**
- Royal: "The search for Merlin must continue! I will reward whoever finds him with five manors!"

**If the player does nothing:** Merlin is confirmed imprisoned by Nimue regardless and the search finds nothing; Griflet besieges Silchester under Chivalric rules without breaking it before winter. Absent knights gain no Glory and cannot compete for Arthur's promised five-manor reward for finding Merlin.

## Year 524 — Lancelot Arrives; Dolorous Garde Identity Revealed

**Situation:** Silchester surrenders. Galeholt defeats Orkney naval forces. The "Squire of the Lake" arrives and is knighted as the Knight of the Lake — revealed to be Lancelot, son of King Ban de Ganis.

**Court:** Camelot. Special Guests: Sir Melodiam de Galis (son of King Pellinore).

**New Round Table Members:** Ywaine, the Knight of the Lion.

**Events:**

**Orkney/de Gales Feud:**
- Sir Melodiam accuses Gawaine of murdering King Pellinore; offers trial by combat
- Melodiam is defeated; refuses to retract; is beheaded by Gawaine (Gawaine declared innocent)

**Event: SAINT JOHN'S DAY (Midsummer)**
- Wounded knight brought to court with two lance heads through body and a piece of sword in his head
- Knight says he killed his attacker himself; asks someone to swear to avenge him on all who love his enemy more than himself; will reveal no more until oath taken; Arthur declines to send anyone
- Next day: Lady of the Lake arrives with entourage including two men of house of King of Ganis (Heraldry Critical = Lionel and Bors)
- Lady asks Arthur to knight her companion, the "Squire of the Lake," whenever he asks — using equipment she provides; Arthur argues about being robbed of the honor; companions convince him to agree
- Squire says he will prove himself; identified only as "Squire of the Lake" — "nameless and fatherless"

**The Knighting (Saint John's Day):**
- ~100 squires knighted at once; stranger is last (least known)
- Arthur dubs him Knight of the Lake; stranger takes blank silver shield (will choose personal arms at year's end)
- During mass: knight sneaks off; player hook — do they follow?
- Knight of the Lake goes to litter of wounded knight; easily draws out two jagged spearheads; takes oath
- If reported to Arthur: Arthur sends Yvaine to investigate; Knight of the Lake says "I could not bear to see him suffer"
- Feast: Knight of the Lake begs queen's forgiveness; asks to be her champion; Guenever says he may compete to be Queen's Knight next spring

**Summer Adventures of the Knight of the Lake:**
- Defeats "lord of Northumberland"; captures mischievous pooka in Yorkshire Moors; destroys Saxon outlaw band in Deira; defeats small giant at bridge toll
- Most significantly: visits haunted Dolorous Garde; lifts great rock in courtyard (he alone of all who tried)

- Over 1,000 Glory gained in his first year as a knight

**Christmas Court:**
- Lancelot returns; warmly received; his prisoners gather to swear loyalty to him
- Royal: "All is well. As young Sir Lancelot has shown, great and talented knights continue to come to Camelot."

**Special Winter Phase Modifiers:** None.

**If the player does nothing:** Lancelot arrives, is knighted, achieves Dolorous Garde, and discovers his true name regardless; Silchester surrenders after Griflet's siege. Absent knights gain no Glory from these events and cannot follow the Squire of the Lake on Saint John's Day or witness the naming of Lancelot.

## Year 525 — Romans Demand Tribute; Arthur Refuses

**Situation:** King Arthur establishes the Noble Charter. Roman ambassadors arrive at Pentecost demanding tribute. Arthur refuses and promises to march on Rome. Planning begins for the continental campaign.

**Court:** Camelot. Special Guests: Lady of the Lake; Sir Lancelot; Bishop David of Menevia; Archbishop Dubricus; many bishops and monks.

**New Round Table Members:** Cador of Cornwall (Arthur's kinsman); Hoel of Alclud.

**Events:**

**The Noble Charter:** Guarantees all noblemen their ancient rights in the king's court: trial by jury of peers; protection from arbitrary justice. Opinion of all nobles solicited.

**Intrigue:** Lancelot's knighting was never completed by Arthur. Player hook — Intrigue Critical = Queen Guenever girded his sword for him; Courtesy roll reveals divided loyalty implications.

**Pentecost — Roman Ambassadors:**
- 12 Romans walk down the carpet: 8 armed (Roman centurion, Goths, Ethiopian with long shield and spear, African Vandal, turbaned Arab, Byzantine cataphract, steel-covered Italian); 3 senators in togas with olive branches; 1 Imperial Tribune
- Tribune: Emperor Lucius demands Arthur acknowledge him as lord and send tribute; "Your father and the kings before him paid"
- Court erupts; knights draw swords threatening envoys
- Loyalty (Arthur) roll: Success = ready to obey; Critical = outraged, draw sword, shout threat
- Loyalty (Arthur) again: Fumble = you don't stop; a Round Table knight attacks you nonlethally
- Arthur: "Anyone who harms these envoys pays with his life. Sir Bedivere, show these men to their lodging."
- Arthur retires with council; cites Belinus and Brennius conquered Rome; Constantine was a Briton

**The King's Decree (after council):**
- "I know no tribute or loyalty I owe him. I am entitled to be sovereign of the Empire by right of my predecessors. I shall go with my army to Rome. Tell him to come to me and pay homage or suffer the pains of war."
- Sir Kay gives envoys appropriate gifts; Sir Cador escorts them with safe passage
