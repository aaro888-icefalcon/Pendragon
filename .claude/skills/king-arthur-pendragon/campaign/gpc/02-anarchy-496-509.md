# Anarchy Period: 496–509

---

## Period Overview

**Situation:** No High King. The ruling class of Logres was wiped out at the poisoned feast at St. Albans. Saxons have the largest armies in the land. Petty kings war over territory. Britain is fracturing.

**Themes:** Survival, political decision-making, personal enrichment, no justice to stop ambitious knights from seizing property and castles, building reputation.

**Default home:** Salisbury, now ruled by Countess Ellen (widow of Roderick), then from 509 by young Earl Robert.

**Key NPCs:**
- Countess Ellen (496–509) — pragmatic regent; defers to player knight advice
- Earl Robert of Salisbury (from 509) — earnest new earl; needs guidance
- Duke Ulfius of Silchester — wise survivor of Uther's era; advocates neutrality
- Sir Brastias — lordless; travels between courts; later settles with Ulfius
- King Nanteleod of Escavalon — most promising British king; organizes resistance; killed 508
- King Cerdic of Wessex — Saxon lord; aggressive, demanding tribute; wins 508
- King Aesc of Kent — Saxon king; shares London occupation with Essex
- Merlin — in Egypt; completely absent this period

**The Grudge Score System:**
Track each Saxon kingdom's animosity toward Salisbury on the Grudge Record (see 00-gpc-overview.md). Refusing tribute increases the Grudge. High Grudge = raids → pillage → invasion. Each Saxon kingdom has its own threshold (GM determines).

## 496 — The Sword in the Stone Appears

**Situation:** Logres has no king. Countess Ellen holds Salisbury for her infant son Robert. Saxons demand tribute. A sword appears in a stone in London.

**Court:** Held at Sarum. Countess Ellen's court.

**Special Guests:** Aetheling Aescwine of the East Saxons; Sir Brastias (passing through)

**Gossip:**
- "Did you hear about the sword? The one in the stone in London? It has writing that says it is for the king of Britain to pull out! What do you think?"
- "Let me tell you, this looks like another one of Merlin's tricks, doesn't it? And Merlin? It's a trick. Maybe we should all go take shelter at the abbey?"

**Events:**
- Sword appears in stone in London — no one can pull it out; gossip runs wild
- Saxons demand tribute from Salisbury; countess defers to player knights on whether to pay
- Ulfius urges the central lords (Silchester, Rydychan, Salisbury, Clarence, Somerset, Dorset) to form a defensive bloc
- New walls proposed for Sarum — topics at court include whether to build stone walls now

**Countess's choice (put to players):**
- Pay tribute to which Saxon kingdom? How much?
- Align with Nanteleod? Ulfius? Remain neutral?
- Begin wall construction? (Long-term project, several years)

**Christmas Court:** Cautious; grim; everyone tense about the future

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Apply tribute modifiers to Annual Manor Wealth if tribute paid

**If the player does nothing:** The sword remains in the stone unclaimed; Saxons continue demanding tribute regardless; the Grudge Score rises if payment is refused whether or not Salisbury knights are present to advise.

## 497–499 — The Quiet Desperate Years

**Situation:** A grinding period. No great battles. Tribute demands, local skirmishes, the grind of survival. The sword in the stone remains in London; no one claims kingship.

**Court:** Held at Sarum annually.

**Pattern each year:**
- Gossip centers on which king might be winning, and when the Saxons will move
- Countess defers to player advisors for tribute/alliance decisions
- A Saxon kingdom sends tribute collectors or messengers
- Minor local adventure is available (use Anarchy Period Adventures below)
- Christmas court is somber

**Notable events 497–500 (background):**
- King Pellinore of Gomeret is active in the north
- Various petty lords of Cambria war with each other
- Merlin is not in Britain
- Ygraine and daughters (Margawse, Elaine, Morgan) remain in the north

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Tribute paid = reduce Annual Manor Wealth accordingly; tribute refused = add +1 to +3 to Grudge score of relevant Saxon kingdom

**If the player does nothing:** Tribute collectors arrive and demands are paid or ignored without the knights' counsel; the Grudge Score shifts accordingly and manor wealth suffers the standard tribute modifier. No adventures are concluded and no Glory is gained.

## 501 — Saxons Move East; Gales Wars

**Situation:** Multiple simultaneous threats. East is falling to the Angles. Nanteleod is maneuvering to become a British High King.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Two Saxon princes seeking tribute

**Gossip:**
- "It was good for a bit there! Saxons killing Saxons and Welsh and Irish killing each other."
- "But all the survivors are going to be in one army afterwards. We've been lucky to hold them off this long."

**News:**
- King Nanteleod challenges the Three Cadlews (rulers of southern Gales mountain kingdoms)
- Hertford and Huntington counts seek volunteers desperately: Anglish horde has overrun the east, is heading their way

**Events:**

**Kidnapping Attempt on Ygraine:**
- A small Saxon band breaks into Amesbury nunnery and tries to seize Queen Ygraine
- Knights sent in pursuit find the Saxons all dead — torn apart — in a clearing
- Ygraine nearby, shocked; Morgan le Fay sitting calmly, drawing in dirt, says only "they were careless"
- The queen refuses to say what happened; Morgan shows no concern

**Angles Conquer Huntington and Hertford:**
- King Cwichelm's Angles storm Royston, destroy the Hertford army, besiege and kill the Earl of Huntington
- The surviving Anglish then demand tribute from London, Rydychan, Silchester, Lonazep, Lindsey

**Wars in Gales:**
- Nanteleod outmaneuvers the Three Cadlews with a surprise Irish allied force
- Three Cadlews defeated; King Nanteleod names Maelgwen as vassal king of Norgales

**Adventure: MORGAN'S MARRIAGE**
- Setup: Queen Ygraine asks Salisbury knights to escort her and Morgan to Gaiholm Castle in Gorre for Morgan's wedding to King Uriens
- Journey north through Cambria and Rheged (dangerous hill tribes; possible bandits)
- At Gaiholm: Morgan's sisters Margawse (wife of King Lot) and Elaine (wife of King Nentres) present; Pagan wedding rites; northern chieftains in attendance
- On return: queen stays north; smaller group makes dangerous ride home through hostile territory
- **Glory:** 50 for escort assignment

**Christmas Court:** Somber; Saxon victories in east; some relief at Nanteleod's Gales success

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Apply tribute paid modifiers; Lyonesse — Tristram born (queen dies in childbirth; background)

**If the player does nothing:** Angles overrun Huntington and Hertford regardless; Nanteleod defeats the Three Cadlews without Salisbury's help. Absent knights gain no Glory from the Morgan escort and miss the 50-Glory assignment; eastern lords fall to the Angles with no Salisbury intervention.

## 502 — Saxon Civil War

**Situation:** Saxons fight each other. Nanteleod consolidates. Brief respite for Logres — if anyone can take advantage of it.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Saxon princes (seeking mercenaries or allies); Angles emissary

**Gossip:**
- "Let me tell you, found dead they were, all of them torn up like from wolves. Yes! Their blood even drunk up, and bones snapped." (about Morgan's attack on kidnappers)
- "And little Morgan was just sitting there, drawing in the dirt."

**News:**
- Nanteleod has secured Gloucester, Orofoise, Galvoie, Cheshire, Cameliard, Wuerensis as vassals
- Duke Corneus of Lindsey has Bedegraine and Lambor
- Bedegraine/Lambor have a border dispute with Cameliard — war between Nanteleod and Corneus looming
- Anglish emissary declares their king bretwalda of all Britain; demands Salisbury's loyalty

**Events:**

**War Between Saxons (Battle of Hertford):**
- King Ælle and the southern Saxons challenge the Angles' claim to bretwalda
- Battle of Hertford: Ælle wins a Decisive Victory; Anglish driven from field; Ælle does not pursue

**Aggression in Midlands:**
- Nanteleod maneuvers through Lambor and Bedegraine, accepting surrenders; Duke Corneus watches but does not engage

**Countess's dilemma:** Ulfius urges forming a defensive bloc with Silchester, Rydychan, Salisbury; possibly adding Clarence, Somerset, Dorset

**Christmas Court:**
- Discussion of closer Silchester alliance

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Apply tribute paid modifiers

**If the player does nothing:** Saxons fight each other at Hertford and Ælle wins regardless; Nanteleod maneuvers through Lambor and Bedegraine without Salisbury support. Absent knights gain no Glory and the Silchester alliance proceeds or stalls without their influence.

## 503 — London Falls to Saxons

**Situation:** Kent and Essex besiege London. Ulfius cannot hold. London surrenders and becomes a Saxon tributary.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Cynric son of Cerdic (Wessex); Uffo son of Ulfius; Lane of Lindsey; herald from Nanteleod

**Gossip:**
- "Oh my God — if the Saxons take London what will we do?"
- "London to the Saxons now! Disaster is creeping our way year by year."

**News:**
- Multiple threats: Kent, Essex besieging London; Nanteleod/Lindsey war possible; Saxons demanding tribute
- Cynric (Cerdic's son): "We are not foolish enough to wish for a battle, but we'll extract our tribute in plunder if need be."
- Lane of Lindsey: "We need your help. The duke is threatened on three sides. We expect attack from Gales, Malahaut, and the Angles."

**Events:**

**Saxon Siege of London:**
- King Aesc of Kent and King Aethelswith of Essex both march armies; besiege London from both sides
- Ulfius tries to relieve it; is outmaneuvered; Kentsmen cross river at night; Ulfius escapes at night
- Sir Helifer the Mercenary (hired by Saxons) uses siege equipment; city surrenders
- London is NOT sacked; mayor administers city and pays alternating tribute to Kent/Essex
- Saxon warriors enter city; garrisons lock up in White Tower

**Nanteleod Defeats Malahaut:**
- Nanteleod alliances: Clarence, Lambor, Bedegraine
- King of Malahaut marches south, besieges Lincoln; Nanteleod proposes the battle be winner-take-all for Lindsey
- Nanteleod wins decisive victory; Malahaut's Saxons crushed; Nanteleod and Corneus reconciled

**Christmas Court:** London to the Saxons — "disaster creeping our way year by year"

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Apply tribute; Nanteleod's victory means no Saxon tribute this round if not paid

**If the player does nothing:** London falls to Kent and Essex regardless; Nanteleod defeats Malahaut without Salisbury's forces. Absent knights gain no Glory and lose the chance to influence the countess's tribute and alliance decisions.

## 504 — Idres Invades Somerset

**Situation:** King Idres of Cornwall continues expansion north and east. The western flank of Logres is now threatened simultaneously with the eastern Saxon pressure.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Prince Cynric; Sir Lak of Estregales (Nanteleod's envoy)

**Gossip:**
- "I thought it couldn't get worse and it did! Oh my God, I hope and pray this is going to end soon."
- "We can always hope for the Wheel of Dame Fortune to turn our way."

**Events:**
- King of Somerset asks for help against Idres; Idres besieges Wells — it surrenders
- Nanteleod sending envoys (Sir Lak) to court Salisbury's alliance

**Secret offer:** Nanteleod sends Sir Lak with a private offer: he will march through Salisbury against Saxons if Salisbury allies with him and commits knights

**Countess's question:** "The Saxons are applying pressure, but I'd like to withhold payment this year. Is it possible?"

**Christmas Court:** "King Idres marches from the west, Saxons from the east. Things look ill for us."

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Apply tribute modifiers

**If the player does nothing:** Idres besieges Wells and it surrenders regardless; Nanteleod's envoy arrives and departs without an answer. Absent knights gain no Glory and the countess makes the tribute and alliance decisions without their counsel.

## 505 — War Against Saxons / Nanteleod Fights

**Situation:** Decisive year. Nanteleod marches. Saxons raid Salisbury. Cornwall takes all of Somerset.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Prince Cynric (with threats); Sir Lak and Sir Alain for Nanteleod

**News:**
- Prince Cynric threatens: Cerdic, Port, and Ælle will destroy Salisbury if countess does not submit AND send troops
- Nanteleod is mustering and will attack Essex and the Angles
- King Idres is going to war in Somerset again

**Events:**
- Somerset falls completely to Idres (Bath and Bristol taken)
- Cornish foragers raid western Salisbury (most Salisbury knights off with Nanteleod)

**Battle of Levcomagus**
- Nanteleod vs. Cerdic; Cerdic outmaneuvered and retreats
- **Length:** 2 rounds | **Size:** Medium
- **Commanders:** Nanteleod (Battle 19) vs. Cerdic (Battle 18) | **Mods:** None
- **Victor:** Nanteleod. Saxons slipped away.
- **Plunder:** None | **Glory:** 30 per round

**Battle of Royston (in Hertford)**
- Nanteleod pushes east; joins Ulfius; meets large Saxon force at Hertford
- **Length:** 2 rounds | **Size:** Medium
- **Commanders:** Nanteleod (Battle 19) vs. Cerdic (Battle 18) | **Mods:** None
- **Victor:** Indecisive. Both armies retreat; Nanteleod retreats to Beale Valet

**Nanteleod's Speech:**
- Past the 40-day service obligation; Nanteleod orates [critical success] to inspire knights to stay at own expense
- Player knights may choose to stay or go home

**Battle of Hertford:**
- Nanteleod and Corneus march into Essex, scatter forces
- King Aethelswith of Anglia marches down; full battle south of Hertford
- **Length:** 2 rounds | **Size:** Medium
- **Commanders:** Nanteleod (Battle 19) vs. Aethelswith (Battle 21) | **Mods:** None
- **Victor:** Decisive Victory. Aethelswith killed. Much plunder.
- **Plunder:** 2 rouncys, 2 palfreys, £5 of goods | **Glory:** 30 per round, x2

**Christmas Court:** People happier; Saxons beaten; Logres lords seem united; Cornish raiders hit west Salisbury

**Winter Phase Modifier:** No tribute paid this year, no tribute modifier; western Salisbury manors may have suffered from Cornish raids

**If the player does nothing:** Nanteleod defeats the Saxons in three battles regardless; Somerset falls to Idres and Cornish raiders strike western Salisbury uncontested. Absent knights gain no Glory from the battles, miss the plunder, and their home manors suffer Cornish raid damage.

## 506 — Nanteleod Bids for High Kingship

**Situation:** Nanteleod is making his play to be elected High King. Cornwall and Logres maneuver. Saxons quietly rebuild.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Prince Mark (Cornwall); Prince Alain de Carlion (Nanteleod's son, now revealed)

**Gossip:**
- "It is good to have a king, I say. I hope the countess swears fealty to Nanteleod."
- "Well, there could be a king from Logres still. My old nanny said that Prince Madoc's kid was an elf kid and could be an adult right now."

**News:**
- Aescwine is new King of the Angles; celebrating, planning, not collecting tribute this year
- Mark and Alain are both here and not friendly; chess match (Mark won); oration contest (Alain won)
- Nanteleod is preparing to fight Cornwall; preparations obvious

**Events:**
- Nanteleod contacts the Supreme Collegium to be elected High King
- Cornwall/Logres war: armies maneuver in Somerset all summer; no engagement; year entirely indecisive militarily

**Countess's dilemma:** "We could decide the matter of this war if we allied with either Nanteleod or Cornwall. We control the roads." She goes with Nanteleod after Idres offends her.

**Christmas Court:** "Are we not the stupidest people in the world? The Saxons are finally beaten down, so we Cymru attack each other instead."

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Tribute adjustments as applicable

**If the player does nothing:** The Cornwall/Logres military stalemate continues regardless; Nanteleod contacts the Supreme Collegium without Salisbury's decisive support. Absent knights gain no Glory and miss the chance to tip the war's balance by committing Salisbury's forces.

## 508 — Nanteleod Killed; Hampshire Falls

**Situation:** The most dramatic battle of the Anarchy. Britain's best hope, King Nanteleod, is ambushed and killed. Hampshire falls to the Saxons. Salisbury is pillaged.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** King Nanteleod and retinue

**Gossip:**
- "It's been a long time since we had royalty at this castle! Look at all these people!"
- "Well, I'll say that the countess should get her claws into that king if she can."

**News:** All Salisbury knights must muster this summer. Nanteleod is going to destroy Cerdic once and for all.

**Countess:** "All knights must muster this year. Sharpen your swords, and say your last goodbyes to the girls."

**Battle of Netley Marsh**
*The decisive disaster of the Anarchy Period*

- **Length:** 9 rounds | **Battle Size:** Huge
- **Commanders:** Nanteleod (Battle 20) vs. Cerdic (Battle 18)
- **Modifier:** Surprise maneuver bonus for Saxons: +5
- **Army sizes:**
  - 8,250 British: 750 Cambrian knights; 500 Logres knights; 2,000 Cymri infantry; 5,000 Irish and Galis warriors
  - 11,000 Saxons: 2,000 Wessex warriors; 5,000 Sussex warriors; 2,500 continental mercenaries; 500 Cornish knights; 1,000 Cornish foot
- Heraldry check (+10 modifier): Success = you see King Ælle's banner; Critical = also Cornish horsemen and continental mercenaries
- **The Fight:**
  - Rounds 1–6: Normal
  - Round 7: Mounted Saxons led by Prince Cynric burst from cover and attack King Nanteleod and his guards — anyone in Rear of Battle may attempt to assist the king — regardless, he is killed
  - Round 8: British army breaks and flees
  - Round 9: Pursuit

- **Victor:** King Cerdic. Decisive Victory.
- **Plunder:** None (Saxons only)
- **Glory:** 60 per round, x1/2 for Decisive Victory (note: modifier for a loss — confirm from rulebook table D–1)

**Aftermath:**
- All of Hampshire overrun; residents enslaved; Saxon families move into villages
- Salisbury and Silchester pillaged

**In Ganis (background):** A male son is born to the King of Ganis's wife — named Galahad (later Lancelot)

**Christmas Court:**
- Nanteleod dead; many Salisbury knights, soldiers, and commoners killed
- Duke Corneus of Lindsey also died of illness; nephew Derfel inherits
- Young Robert (now near manhood) demands to be knighted so he can take control

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Entire county suffers +5 modifier to Annual Manor Wealth from pillaging

**If the player does nothing:** Nanteleod is killed at Netley Marsh regardless and Cerdic wins a decisive victory; Hampshire is overrun and Salisbury is pillaged whether or not the knights fought. Absent knights gain only half Glory (defeat modifier) and miss the chance to attempt to save Nanteleod in Round 7.

## 509 — Earl Robert Knighted; Tournament Announced

**Situation:** Countess's last year. Robert takes the title. Sir Brastias brings news of a tournament in London — the Supreme Collegium will try to settle the succession via trial by combat.

**Court:** Held at Sarum.

**Special Guests:** Prince Cynric (demanding vassalage or triple tribute); Prince Mark; Sir Brastias

**Gossip:**
- "But what about that meeting in London? The lords are going to choose a king, aren't they? At last?"
- "Will it be before the Saxons get here? I'll keep getting ready."

**News:**
- Sir Brastias brings word: Supreme Collegium has decreed a non-lethal tournament in London; whoever wins gets the collective blessing; they will also discuss the sword in the stone
- Cerdic: punishes those who fight against him

**Countess:**
- "We must collect the aid due to me for the knighting of Robert."
- "We need to replace our knights and foot soldiers. I will pay coin, not homage to a barbarian. I will leave the Saxon decision for my son next year."

**Intrigue:** The countess believes the London tournament will produce a High King. She is stalling — let Earl Robert handle what comes next.

**Events:**

**Earl Robert Knighted:**
- Young Robert asks to be knighted by "the best knight in Salisbury" — could be a player knight (huge honor; near-permanent favor from Robert if so)
- Modest ceremony (Robert diverts some funds to triple tribute instead)
- Full investiture tour of county with household knights; player knights accompany
- Vassals meet him along the way and host him at their manors

**Glory:** 10 for attending the investiture; 100 for knighting Robert

**Christmas Court:**
- Earl Robert's first year in command; young and eager; consults his knights
- "I have lived all my life in Salisbury and never spent a day outside of it. What do we know about Britain today?"
- Use the session to let players brief Robert on the state of the realm; show the map of Britain; discuss next year's tournament

**Winter Phase Modifier:** Tribute and investiture aid combined: shift Annual Manor Wealth roll down three columns

**If the player does nothing:** Robert is knighted by another knight, missing the 100-Glory opportunity; the London tournament is announced regardless and the Supreme Collegium moves to settle the succession. Absent knights gain only 10 Glory for Robert's investiture and have no standing in his eyes as trusted advisors.

## Anarchy Period Adventures (Use Any Year 496–509)

### Adventure: CASTLE OF TEARS (Pleure Castle)
**Setting:** Near Cardigan coast; a grim sea-facing castle
**Hook:** Local monks at the abbey warn all travelers: do not enter these lands. The lord and lady of Pleure Castle (the "Castle of Tears") practice some horrible custom on all who visit.
**Secrets (GM):** The lord's custom is to demand a single combat from every visiting knight. The defeated knight is imprisoned. The lady collects defeated knights as trophies. The castle is essentially a dungeon full of live captives.
**Adventure:** Entering the castle reveals the prisoners; freeing them requires defeating the lord, then the lady (who is actually a capable fighter), and escaping.
**Glory:** 100 for freeing the prisoners; combat Glory as usual

### Adventure: BORDER SKIRMISH WITH A SAXON FOE
**Setting:** Borders of Salisbury
**Hook:** A specific Saxon chieftain has become a recurring enemy of the player knights — he raids, they respond, each interaction escalates
**Structure:** 4–6 episodes across multiple years: (1) First raid; (2) Prisoner exchange gone wrong; (3) Chance encounter at a neutral parley; (4) Knight taken prisoner must escape; (5) Final confrontation at enemy's home territory
**Purpose:** Develops Hate (Saxons) passion organically; gives the Saxon wars a human face; can end in the Saxon's death, conversion, or a grudging respect
**Glory:** 25 per episode; 100 for final resolution

### Adventure: THE FOREST SAUVAGE
**Setting:** The great forest between Lambor and Bedegraine (extending into Cambria)
**Hook:** Knights enter the Forest Sauvage for any reason — pursuit, shortcut, looking for adventure
**Encounters (d6):**
1. A knight with a specific quest seeks companions
2. A hermit with information about Merlin or the future king
3. A wounded knight who witnessed something impossible
4. A chapel containing a miracle (Religion check for benefit)
5. A Saxon war band, deep into territory they should not be (ambush possible)
6. Something supernatural: the Wild Hunt, a faerie knight, a talking animal

**Note:** The Forest Sauvage is specifically designed for Boy King Period adventures too — see Chapter 5 of the GPC for detailed Forest Sauvage scenarios.

### Adventure: POLITICAL MARRIAGE QUEST
**Setting:** Multiple courts
**Hook:** The countess (or later Robert) wants to create an alliance by proposing a marriage between a Salisbury noble and the daughter/son of a neighboring lord
**Challenge:** Getting there safely (road travel, border challenges), convincing the target lord (social rolls, Courtesy, Intrigue), navigating the political implications (which Saxon kingdom does this alliance threaten?)
**Complications:** Target lord is already negotiating with someone else; a rival suitor; a prior betrothal obligation; a family feud the player knights must navigate
**Glory:** 50 for completing the mission successfully

### Adventure: RELIEF OF A BESIEGED CASTLE
**Setting:** Any friendly castle under Saxon siege
**Hook:** A messenger gets through: Castle X is besieged. The garrison can hold 30 more days. Can Salisbury send relief?
**Structure:** March to the castle (encounters en route), assess the siege (Awareness, Intrigue), choose approach: direct assault on siege camp, infiltrate with supplies, harass the besiegers to break morale, parley
**Battle (if direct assault):**
- Length: 3 rounds | Size: Small
- Commanders: Player knight (Battle skill) vs. Saxon chieftain (Battle 15)
- Mods: Surprise if sneaked up (+5 for players)
**Glory:** 50 for reaching the castle; 100 for breaking the siege

## Period Summary: What Changes

| Year | Status |
|------|--------|
| 496 | Sword in stone appears; no claimant; tribute begins |
| 497–500 | Grinding survival; Saxons and petty kings war |
| 501 | Angles conquer east; Nanteleod rises in Gales |
| 502 | Saxons fight each other; London still free |
| 503 | London falls to Saxon tribute system; Nanteleod wins north |
| 504 | Somerset threatened; Idres marching |
| 505 | Nanteleod defeats Saxons in east; Somerset falls to Idres |
| 506 | Nanteleod bids for kingship; Cornwall war; stalemate |
| 507 | London liberated; Somerset liberated; Saxon raiders in Salisbury |
| 508 | **Nanteleod killed at Netley Marsh; Hampshire falls; Salisbury pillaged** |
| 509 | Robert knighted; London tournament announced |

**Into the Boy King Period:** Arthur will appear at the London tournament in 510, pull the sword from the stone, and be acclaimed king. Player knights who have survived the Anarchy are veterans — older, harder, poorer, but experienced. They will be the backbone of Arthur's early wars.
