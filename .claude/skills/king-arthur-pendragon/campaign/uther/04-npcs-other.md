### Sir Argan ap Maelgad
**Position:** Constable of Logres (appointed 490); household knight  
**Born:** 451. **Glory:** 5,648.  
**Died:** At the Battle of Netley Marsh (495 era or just after, during the Anarchy).  
**Key Event:** The most dramatic figure of 493 — he is cuckolded by Uther (allegedly), kills his own wife Dyagenne, challenges Uther to trial by combat, WINS (wounds and disarms the king), ransoms Uther's life for a strong castle, and builds "Uther's Shame" — named to be an enduring monument to the king's lust and betrayal.

| Skill | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Sword | 21 |
| Diplomacy | 17 |
| Horsemanship | 18 |

**Traits:** Energetic 16, Vengeful 18  
**Passion:** Homage (Uther) 17 (declining by 493)

**GM Notes on Argan:**
- He defeats King Uther in single combat — this is a historic scandal that rocks the court
- Invoking God's justice, he is declared to have won justly
- He is NOT a villain from the text's perspective; Uther actually wronged him
- After the duel, he effectively ceases to function as Constable while building his castle
- The castle "Uther's Shame" exists somewhere in Logres; if players want to find it, it's a legal holding

## King Pellinore of Norgales

**Position:** King of Norgales (Welsh border kingdom)  
**Key Facts:**
- Gives up his throne to hunt the Questing Beast doggedly and unceasingly
- Appears briefly at Linden Pool area (492 events) — informs player-knights he gave up Norgales for the hunt
- His disappearance throws Norgales into chaos (491-492); his brothers try to hold the kingdom; several brothers killed in internal conflict and against the Three Cadwys (mountain chieftains)
- His son Sir Lamorak comes to Uther's court desperately seeking news of him (492)

---

## Sir Lamorak

**Position:** Son of King Pellinore of Norgales  
**Appears:** 492 at court, seeking his father  
**Key Facts:**
- Reports Norgales in chaos; Pellinore's brothers fighting each other and the Three Cadwys
- A future major character of the Arthurian canon; here he is young and desperate

## Other Notable NPCs

### King Nanteleod of Escavalon
- Agrees to support Uther's attack on Malahaut (492)
- An allied king outside Logres who votes in the Supreme Collegium

### King Leodegrance of Cameliard
- Father of Guinevere (Arthur's future queen; not yet born or not yet relevant)
- Agrees to support Uther's attack on Malahaut (492)
- Supreme Collegium elector

### The Centurion King of Malahaut
- King of Malahaut (territory around Eburacum/York)
- Initially an ally; becomes hostile when Uther refuses to help against Saxon siege of Eburacum (489)
- Secretly allies with Saxons (493) — discovered by Count Roderick during embassy
- This alliance allows Saxons to bypass Malahaut and attack Logres in 494
- His betrayal is a direct cause of the Linden Pool disaster (494)

### King Lot of Lothian
- Rules Lothian (Caledonian kingdom)
- Marries Margawse (Uther's stepdaughter) (491-492)
- Son Gawaine born (493)
- Possible Infamous Feast suspect (wants to destabilize Logres, position himself for High Kingship during Anarchy)

### King Nentres of Garloth
- Rules Garloth (Caledonian kingdom)
- Marries Elaine (Uther's stepdaughter) (491-492)
- Supreme Collegium elector

### King Uriens of Gorre
- Betrothed to Morgan (Uther's youngest stepdaughter, still a child in 491)
- Marriage to occur when Morgan comes of age
- Northern Cumbrian/Caledonian king

### King Syagrius
- Last Roman-style ruler in Gaul ("The Praetor" in the GPC)
- Arrives at Uther's court (486) seeking military help to reclaim his lands from Clovis
- Uther pledges support (488) but sends Madoc instead
- Madoc betrays Syagrius at Bayeux siege, seizing booty and abandoning him
- Syagrius meets his fate at Clovis' hands
- Possible Infamous Feast suspect (revenge for Madoc's betrayal)

### Sir Hector the Brown
- Household knight, Famous Hero level
- Listed in index; no full stat block provided

### Prince Mark of Cornwall
- Listed as a Famous Hero-level figure
- Future King Mark of Cornwall (Tristram's uncle)
- Present at court during this period

### Lady Eleri
- Sir Diocenar the Butler loves her (Love: Eleri 23) — potential romantic subplot or complication
