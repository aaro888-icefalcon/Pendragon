# NPCs: Royal Court — Major Figures

Full stat blocks and motivations for Uther, Merlin, Madoc, the Great Barons, and key court officers. All stat blocks are Pendragon 5th Edition format.

**Use when:** Running combat or opposed rolls involving any of these NPCs, adjudicating their decisions, determining their reactions to player-knight actions, or answering "what would Gorlois do?"

---

## King Uther Pendragon

**Position:** King of Logres  
**Born:** ~436 (b.436). d. 495 (Infamous Feast, poison).  
**Glory:** 27,750  
**Dynasty:** House of Pendragon (son of Constantin, brother of Constans and Aurelius Ambrosius)

### Stat Block
| Attribute | Value | Derived | Value |
|-----------|-------|---------|-------|
| SIZ | 12 | Damage | 4d6 |
| DEX | 14 | Healing Rate | 3 |
| STR | 16 | Move Rate | 3 |
| CON | 12 | Hit Points | 24 |
| APP | 9 | Unconscious | 6 |

**Armor:** Reinforced Chain, 11 pts, -10 DEX mod, Heavy.

**Combat Skills:** Sword 23, Spear Expertise 24, Battle 20 (army command), Horsemanship 17

**Other Skills:** Intrigue 23, Awareness 16, First Aid 18

**Traits (notable):** Energetic 18, Lustful 17, Proud 16, Reckless 16, Worldly 18, Trusting 6 (very low — he is deeply suspicious)

**Passions:** Hate (Saxons) 18, Loyalty (Vassals) 17, Suspicious (Gorlois) 19, Suspicious (Corneus) 17; Love (Ygraine) triggers madness 489-491

**Excalibur** (acquired 486): +5 to Sword skill, opponent gets -5 to Sword, deals +1d6 damage on hit.

### Motivations and Behavior
- **Primary drive:** Military glory against the Saxons; the High Kingship through the Supreme Collegium
- **Fatal flaw:** Lust for Ygraine overwhelms all reason from 490 onward; he abandons the Supreme Collegium vote to pursue her; he alienates his most capable general (Gorlois); he begets Arthur through Merlin's deception
- **Kingship style:** Rules through fear and favorites. Does not consult widely. Suspicious of competent subordinates who might outshine him. Generous to proven loyalists to the point of corruption.
- **Key relationships:** Relies on Merlin (but resents the reliance); trusts Ulfius most; fears losing Madoc's loyalty; hates the Saxons viscerally
- **Illness (493+):** Falls ill after the Argan scandal; nature of illness unclear (wound, guilt, Merlin's curse, or natural decline); visibly weakening through 494-495

### King's Ire and Favor
- Ire triggered by: disrespect to the crown, departure without leave, questioning his judgment, showing loyalty to enemies, failure in assigned duties
- Favor granted for: battlefield excellence, gifts of information or treasure, loyalty under pressure, flattering his pride
- Once ire begins, difficult to reverse without Merlin's intervention or a dramatic service (rescuing Uther himself, major battlefield victory)

## Merlin the Magician

**Position:** Royal advisor, Magician (appears at court erratically)  
**Status:** Declared traitor and outlaw in absentia 492 (for taking Arthur from Uther); never formally pardoned  
**Glory:** N/A (beyond measure)

### Stat Block
| Attribute | Value | Derived | Value |
|-----------|-------|---------|-------|
| SIZ | * | Damage | * |
| DEX | 17 | Healing Rate | * |
| STR | 28* | Move Rate | * |
| CON | 27* | Hit Points | 39* |
| APP | * | Unconscious | * |

*Merlin's physical stats are far beyond human norms — GM should treat him as effectively immune to normal combat unless plot demands otherwise.

**Magical Combat (if forced into it):**
- Magical Armor 20 (resists 20 points of damage before any reaches him)
- Reverse Attack 20 (on success, reflects attacker's damage back)
- Magical Disarm 26 (on success, weapon flies from attacker's hand)

**Skills:** Awareness 22, Chirurgery 20, Courtesy 21, Faerie Lore 21, First Aid 24, Folklore 21, Hunting 21, Play (Harp) 19, Religion (Any) 22

**Traits:** Chaste 24, Energetic 19, Generous 18, Honest 19, Spiritual 19

**Passion:** Love (Britain) 24 — this overrides all other considerations

### Motivations and Behavior
- **True agenda:** Ensure Arthur's birth, survival, and eventual emergence as High King of Britain. Everything else is secondary. Will sacrifice Uther, the nobility, even the kingdom if it serves this purpose.
- **Surface role:** Appears as Uther's advisor, wielding supernatural information and magical capacity
- **Key actions in period:** Returns to court 487 to reveal Excalibur; brokers Uther-Gorlois peace 489; transforms Uther to impregnate Ygraine 491; takes Arthur from Uther; declared outlaw 492; in hiding thereafter
- **As GM:** Merlin appears when the plot requires it. He always knows more than he reveals. His honesty is genuine but selective — he will not lie directly, but he withholds information constantly. He can be found if a player-knight has a critical need, but he will not come when simply summoned.

## Prince Madoc ap Uther

**Position:** Prince of Logres, Uther's only legitimate son and heir  
**Born:** 459. **Died:** 491 (Battle of Terrabil, sortie against Uther's forces — killed while Merlin was disguising Uther to enter Tintagel).  
**Glory:** 3,228

### Stat Block
| Attribute | Value | Derived | Value |
|-----------|-------|---------|-------|
| SIZ | 15 | Damage | 5d6 |
| DEX | 15 | Healing Rate | 4 |
| STR | 18 | Move Rate | 3 |
| CON | 20 | Hit Points | 35 |
| APP | 12 | Unconscious | 8 |

**Combat Skills:** Sword 15, Spear Expertise 17, Horsemanship 15

**Traits:** Energetic 18, Proud 17, Suspicious (Foreigners) 18, Temperate 17, Valorous 18, Worldly 16

**Passions:** Hate (Saxons) 16, Homage (Uther) 18, Loyalty (Vassals) 17

### Motivations and Behavior
- Competent military commander, carries the Pendragon banner in battle
- Commands retaliatory raids against Saxons (490 after Battle of Linden Pool)
- In 488, betrays King Syagrius in Gaul, seizing booty but abandoning the king at Bayeux — pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness
- Fathered a bastard son, Eliwlod (b. 488), who appears later as a prophetic eagle but plays no role in this period
- **Funeral:** Buried at the Giant's Dance (Stonehenge) next to Aurelius Ambrosius. His death removes Uther's only heir, deepening the succession crisis.

## Duke Gorlois of Cornwall

**Position:** Duke of Cornwall (the only non-Logres dukedom recognized by Uther)  
**Born:** ~438. **Died:** 491 (Battle of Terrabil, nighttime sortie).  
**Glory:** 11,914

### Stat Block
| Attribute | Value | Derived | Value |
|-----------|-------|---------|-------|
| SIZ | — | Damage | — |
| DEX | — | Healing Rate | — |
| STR | — | Move Rate | — |
| CON | — | Hit Points | — |
| APP | — | Unconscious | — |

**Combat Skills:** Battle 21 (the best general in the realm after Uther), Sword — (not listed; treat as 18+)

**Passions:** Love (Ygraine) 18, Homage (Uther) 9 (dangerously low — he is loyal but barely)

### Motivations and Behavior
- Genuinely loyal to Logres but deeply protective of Cornwall's independence and of Ygraine
- The Irish raids on Cornwall are real — not an excuse. He has been hard-pressed and resents being called traitor for defending his own lands.
- Leaves court without Uther's leave (490) when the king attempts to take Ygraine — technically violating hospitality, giving Uther legal pretext
- Declared rebel 491; defends Tintagel and Castle Terrabil. Killed during the nighttime sortie that Merlin engineered as a distraction while Uther entered Tintagel disguised as Gorlois.
- **Aftermath:** His death is widely blamed on Uther's lust; his stepdaughters Morgan and Margawse are bitter. Speculation about the Infamous Feast centers partly on them.

## Lady Ygraine, Duchess of Cornwall → Queen of Logres

**Position:** Duchess of Cornwall (wife of Gorlois), then Queen of Logres (wife of Uther, from 491)  
**Glory:** Listed in the quick-reference but stat block not detailed  
**Appearance:** Beauty described as so striking that Uther could "barely conceal his desire" on first seeing her (490)

### Motivations and Behavior
- Loves her husband Gorlois genuinely; devastated by his death
- Grieves far more than celebrates at her hasty marriage to Uther (491)
- Gives birth to Arthur at Tintagel (492) — knows the child was conceived through deception (Uther disguised by Merlin as Gorlois)
- Merlin takes Arthur; Ygraine is told nothing definitively; her grief and the mystery of Arthur's fate colors her remaining years
- Falls into melancholia alongside Uther's illness (493-495)
- **After Infamous Feast (495):** Retires to Ambrius Abbey, takes her daughter Morgan with her. Morgan enters the nunnery where she will receive her education before her eventual emergence as a power in her own right.

## Sir Ulfius, Duke of the Vale (Silchester)

**Position:** Marshal of Logres; Duke of the Vale (Silchester)  
**Born:** ~439. **Glory:** 9,224.  
**Status:** One of Uther's most trusted favorites; gravely wounded at Battle of St Albans (495) — thus absent from the Infamous Feast and survives.

### Stat Block (Key Skills)
**Combat:** Battle 19, Sword 20, Spear Expertise 19, Siege 17  
**Other:** Intrigue 18, Courtesy 16  
**Traits:** Energetic 19, Proud 18, Valorous 20  
**Passion:** Homage (Uther) 24 — absolute loyalty

### Role and Behavior
- De facto second-in-command of Logres' military
- Commands the British knights at the Battle of St Albans (495), crushing Malahaut's force and turning the Saxon flank
- Gravely wounded at St Albans → not at the Infamous Feast → survives
- His survival means he is one of the few competent military leaders available during the Anarchy

## Count Roderick of Salisbury

**Position:** Count of Salisbury (player-knights' liege lord)  
**Born:** ~450. **Died:** 495 (Infamous Feast, poisoned).  
**Glory:** 4,044

### Stat Block (Key Skills)
**Combat:** Battle 17  
**Other:** Intrigue — (not detailed; treat as 14-16)  
**Traits:** Just (notable), prudent  

### Motivations and Behavior
- The player-knights' direct liege lord — the most important relationship in the campaign
- Loyal to Uther but independently minded; will push back through legal channels rather than defiance
- In 492 sends Sir Elad and Father Dewi to court to defend the player-knights at their treason trial, endangering his own status as a royal favorite
- In 493 sent to Malahaut with Uther's ultimatum to the Centurion King; ambushed by Saxons on return; survives
- **Son Robert** born 492 (same year as Arthur) — Robert will become Count Salisbury during the Anarchy
- **Death:** Poisoned at the Infamous Feast. His countess Ellen is left widowed with young Robert and no experienced officers.

## Duke Eldol of Glevum

**Position:** Duke of Glevum  
**Born:** Before 380 (over 100 years old in 480). **Glory:** 19,067.

### Stat Block (Key Skills)
**Combat:** Battle 22 (the most experienced general in Logres)

### Key Facts
- Sole survivor of the Night of Long Knives (when Vortigern and his Saxons murdered the unarmed nobles of Britain — Eldol escaped with a broken table leg as a weapon)
- His extreme age is a constant story element — he is a living legend, and no one disputes his authority
- His rival heirs-apparent for Glevum have begun maneuvering (the Clarence-Gloucester feud is imminent but hasn't started in this period)
- Birchwood Castle in Clarence owes 0 days of upkeep — a favor granted to Eldol

## Duke Lucius of the Saxon Shore

**Position:** Duke of the Saxon Shore (eastern coast defense)  
**Status:** Minor in 480 (under Uther's wardship). Coming of age mid-period.  
**Key holdings:** Caercolun and Caerwent coastline fortifications; Fort Bran (Caerwent), Broadstream Fort, Old Fort

### Key Facts
- Wardship by Uther 480 = Uther collects duchy revenues until Lucius reaches majority
- Saxon Shore ducal army: 26+ knights plus coastal garrison
- Linden Pool (Duke Corneus' territory, separate from Saxon Shore) falls to Saxons in 494 — this is NOT Lucius' duchy but nearby
- Caercolun area is progressively compromised by Saxon expansion from 485 onward

## Duke Corneus of Lindsey/Bedegraine

**Position:** Duke of Lindsey (Linden Pool), later referenced in connection with Bedegraine  
**Key location:** Linden Pool Castle (Lincoln)

### Key Facts
- Uther visits Corneus at Linden Pool Castle in 487 (Lindsey Embassy) — displays Excalibur; Corneus renews his oath
- Uther is Suspicious of Corneus (17) — not entirely trusted
- Linden Pool is sacked by Saxons (494); Corneus disappears (presumably killed); his army scatters

## King Cadwy of Summerland

**Position:** Count (later King) of Summerland  
**Notes:** Technically Count of Summerland from 482. The GPC treats Summerland as a separate kingdom; here it is nominally under Uther via a special agreement.

### Agreement Terms
- Cadwy sends a small annual gift to Uther
- Cadwy will never attack Uther first
- In return, Uther leaves Summerland alone
- Uther (agnostic in spiritual matters) recognizes Summerland's weird ancient powers

## Lady Nineve

**Position:** Uther's new magical advisor (appears 494)  
**Source:** New character in 494; keeps news of the Saxons from the king, tends his illness

### GM Notes
- Her appearance 494 is suspicious — she keeps information from Uther during a critical military buildup
- Could be a faerie agent, a traitor's plant, or an innocent healer depending on GM's choice
- She is a Lady of the Lake (Faerie connection implied)
