# Timeline: The Uther Period, 480-495 AD

Year-by-year chronicle of the Uther Period. Each entry includes situation, court location, key events, battles, and default consequences if player-knights are inactive.

**Use when:** Running a specific year's events, looking up what has already happened, establishing context for a scene, or adjudicating NPC actions.

**Note:** Events marked * differ from the Great Pendragon Campaign 1st Edition. Events marked † are new additions to this book.

---

## 480 — The Year of Three Wars

**Situation:** Uther Pendragon is crowned King of Logres following his brother Aurelius Ambrosius' death. He inherits three ongoing crises simultaneously: Irish raiders threatening Wales, Saxon pressure in the north and east, and internal baronial instability. Merlin is present at court.

**Court:** Easter Court at Castle of the Rock, Salisbury.

**Events:**
- Uther is crowned King of Logres
- Paschent (son of Vortigern) brings an Irish army across the sea to Wales
- Duke Gorlois of Cornwall is pressed by Irish raiders on Cornwall's western coasts

**Battles/Adventures:**
- **Battle of Menevia** (Wales): Uther defeats Irish King Gillomanius. 9 rounds, Large battle. Uther [Battle 20] vs. Gillomanius [Battle 15]. Decisive Victory for Uther. £2 plunder available.
- **Battle of Salisbury** (near Sarum): Aurelius fights the Saxons under Aesc of Kent. 7 rounds, Large battle. Aurelius [Battle 16] vs. Aesc [Battle 18]. Aurelius is poisoned before the battle (a Saxon spy); he fights from a litter and dies in round 6. Gorlois [Battle 21] rallies the British knights; Decisive Victory for the British despite Aurelius' death.

**If the player does nothing:** Aurelius is dead by summer. Uther is crowned. Gillomanius is defeated. The Saxons are beaten at Salisbury but not destroyed.

## 481 — Bedegraine Conquered

**Situation:** Uther consolidates his kingship by attacking Bedegraine, a semi-independent territory that has not sworn homage. The conquest adds significant resources (lead, silver mines) to the royal treasury.

**Court:** Easter Court location not specified for 481; likely Castle of the Rock or Londinium.

**Events:**
- Uther declares Bedegraine subject to Logres
- Prince Madoc is knighted (his formal entry into military service)

**Battles/Adventures:**
- **Battle of Bedegraine**: 3 rounds, Small battle. Uther [Battle 20] vs. King of Bedegraine [Battle 17]. Decisive Victory for Uther. Uther personally slays the King of Bedegraine in the fighting. £0-1 plunder (the goal is subjugation, not looting).

**Resources gained:** 10 lead mines at Oakmeet and 500 £/year in lead and silver (100% royal share — Uther takes all of it from Bedegraine).

**If the player does nothing:** Bedegraine is conquered and absorbed into Logres. Its king is dead. A new sheriff is appointed.

## 482 — Summerland and Consolidation

**Situation:** Uther turns to southwestern politics. An agreement is reached with King Cadwy of Summerland that keeps that kingdom out of Logres' wars in exchange for autonomy.

**Court:** Not specified for 482.

**Events:**
- Cadwy is formally recognized as Count (later King) of Summerland under the special agreement: annual gift to Uther; never attacks first; Uther leaves Summerland alone.*
- Uther extends his administrative reforms through the county system — sheriffs appointed across Logres
- Invasion of Summerland is recorded in the extended chronology (107, 113) but with asterisk; may be alternate version of events — GM choice

**If the player does nothing:** Summerland agreement stabilizes the southwest. Logres' administrative machinery continues to function.

---

## 483 — Irish War Continues; Court Events

**Situation:** Irish raids continue to threaten Cornwall and western Logres. Gorlois is fully occupied defending his duchy. The court grows larger as Uther seeks to consolidate his nobles' loyalty.

**Events (483):**
- Irish raids continue on Cornwall's coasts
- Gorlois defends Cornwall; Irish are the real enemy here, not Gorlois being disloyal
- Court intrigue events (see Table A-J for scenario generation)
- The Prince's Passion adventure (see 06-adventures.md): a court-based intrigue involving Madoc

**If the player does nothing:** The Irish are eventually repulsed by Gorlois; no decisive change in political situation.

## 484 — Saxon Disaster at Eburacum; Night Victory at Mt. Damen

**Situation:** The northern Saxons under Octa and Eosa strike south. Uther marches to meet them but is ambushed at Eburacum (York), suffering a severe defeat. A desperate night counterattack saves the campaign.

**Court:** Not specified.

**Events:**
- Octa and Eosa lead large Saxon forces from Nohaut and Deira against Logres
- Uther marches north to relieve pressure on Malahaut

**Battles/Adventures:**
- **Battle of Eburacum (Ambush):** 6 rounds, Large battle. Uther [Battle 20] vs. Octa [Battle 19]. DECISIVE DEFEAT for the British. The Saxons set an ambush; British suffer -10 modifier in round 1. Many British knights killed or captured.
- **Battle of Mt. Damen (Night Attack):** 3 rounds, Medium battle. Surprise night attack by Uther on the Saxon camp. Decisive Victory for Uther. £10 plunder available. This recovers British morale and prevents complete disaster.

**If the player does nothing:** Uther nearly loses the war in the north but recovers with the night attack. The situation stabilizes but the Saxons remain strong.

## 485 — Saxon Shore; Administrative Reforms

**Situation:** The eastern coast is increasingly threatened by Saxon expansion. The Saxon Shore duchy (Duke Lucius, still under Uther's wardship as a minor) struggles to hold the coastline. Uther begins administrative consolidation.

**Events:**
- Saxons seize territory along the eastern coast (Caercolun area starts to be compromised)
- Duke Ventrius of the Saxon Shore dies (the minor Lucius' predecessor); Uther's wardship secures the duchy's revenues
- In 485, Logres has 541 hundreds — the administrative geography is fully mapped
- Caercolun and Caerwent increasingly vulnerable; the King's Progress table marks these locations with † (only available before 485 conquest)

**Saxons who raid this year:** East Seaxe, Suth Seaxe, Kent

**If the player does nothing:** The eastern coast erodes. Saxon pressure mounts. Lucius grows toward majority age.

## 486 — Excalibur Revealed; Syagrius Arrives

**Situation:** The pivotal year. Merlin returns to court and presents Uther with Excalibur, the Sword of Victory. This weapon becomes Uther's primary political tool for the remainder of his reign.

**Court:** Easter Court — location not specified for 486.

**Events:**
- **Merlin Returns:** Merlin comes back to Uther's court with Excalibur
- **Excalibur Presented:** Uther receives the Sword of Victory. Stats: +5 Sword skill, -5 opponent's Sword skill, +1d6 damage. A legendary artifact.
- **King Syagrius Arrives:** The last Roman-style king of Gaul ("the Praetor") arrives at Uther's court in exile, seeking military help against Clovis and the Franks. Uther makes him an honored guest but stalls on promises.

**If the player does nothing:** Excalibur is in Uther's hands. Syagrius is a guest. The political situation hasn't changed yet, but the tool that will change it is now present.

## 487 — The Great Sword Feast; Lindsey Embassy

**Situation:** Uther uses Excalibur as a diplomatic tool for the first time, revealing it publicly and visiting northern lords to secure their oaths.

**Court:** Easter Court at Castle of the Rock, Salisbury.

**Events:**
- **The Great Sword Feast:** At Easter Court, Uther reveals Excalibur to the assembled nobles, united by the sight of the legendary blade. None present doubt his intentions or future success.*
- **King Æscwine crowned** King of the East Saxons by "a bloody-handed priest"; distributes lands to his men.†
- **Lindsey Embassy:** Uther and his entourage visit Sir Corneus, Duke of Lindsey, at Linden Pool Castle. Displaying Excalibur has the desired effect — the Duke renews his oaths to Uther. (Note: Uther is Suspicious (Corneus) 17; this is a tense visit.)
- **Malahaut Embassy:** While at Linden Pool, Uther sends envoys to nearby kings. The Centurion King of Malahaut claims to need help against the northern Saxons but Uther does not yet commit.

**Raided:** Caerwent by Saxons; retaliatory strikes by Madoc's army.

**If the player does nothing:** The diplomatic groundwork for the Supreme Collegium campaign is laid. Excalibur is publicly known. Corneus is loyal again.

---

## 488 — The Frankland Expedition; Ygraine First Seen

**Situation:** Uther keeps his promise to Syagrius — but in the worst way. Prince Madoc leads an army to Gaul, achieves military success, then betrays his ally for the sake of plunder. Back in Britain, Uther sees Ygraine for the first time.

**Court:** Easter Court at the Castle of the White City, Silchester. King Uther pledges support to King Syagrius.

**Events:**
- **The Invasion of Frankland:** Uther sends Prince Madoc to Gaul with an army. Initially successful; Madoc betrays Syagrius at the siege of Bayeux — takes much booty but abandons Syagrius to his fate.
- **Eliwlod born:** Bastard son of Prince Madoc.† His story plays no role in this campaign; after his death, he appears as a prophetic eagle.
- **The Great Victory Feast:** At Linden Pool (after the battle with northern Saxons), King Uther first lays eyes on Lady Ygraine, the Duchess of Cornwall. He is utterly smitten. Can barely conceal his desire.
- **Retaliation:** Prince Madoc organizes his army alongside Malahaut forces to plunder the defeated Saxon kingdoms of Deira and Nohaut. They discover King Lot of Lothian doing the same.
- **The King's Progress:** Uther tours Malahaut, Rheged, Rouse, Lestroite, Cambenet, Garloth, Roestoc, Amans, Cameliard, Deesmouth, Legioncamp, Powys, Orofoise — collecting Supreme Collegium votes. He now has 14 votes from Logres vassals + 8 from foreign lords = 22 of 32. The Supreme Collegium is called for the autumn of 489.

**Christmas Court:** Castle of the White Tower, Londinium. Centurion King of Malahaut sends envoys requesting Uther come north in spring.

**Adventure:** The Adventure of Water Leapers — knights track down and destroy a nest of water leapers somewhere in Logres.†

**Raided:** Rheged, Malahaut, Garloth, Lothian, and Gorre by Saxons.

**If the player does nothing:** Syagrius is destroyed. Uther has 22 Supreme Collegium votes. The stage is set for the war with Gorlois.

## 489 — Excalibur's Peace; Saxon Siege of Eburacum II

**Situation:** Uther attempts to muster against Cornwall (a power play or genuine grievance) but lacks support. Merlin brokers peace. In the north, the Saxons besiege Eburacum again, which Uther ignores in favor of Logres politics.

**Court:** Easter Court at Corinium, Clarence.

**Events:**
- **Uther Refuses Malahaut:** Uther tells the Centurion King he has "more pressing business in Logres." Malahaut is left to fend for itself against the Saxons. The Centurion King is deeply displeased — this will have consequences.
- **Saxon Siege of Eburacum II:** King Octa besieges Eburacum for the second time. Only the ancient Roman walls hold. King Eosa leads forces south into Logres.†
- **Excalibur's Peace:** Uther musters against Cornwall, but Duke Edaris of the Marche is fighting Cambrian raiders, Lindsey needs to guard against Malahaut Saxons, and eastern barons send minimal forces. Gorlois maintains the loyalty of all Cornwall's knights. **Merlin brokers peace:** Uther displays Excalibur, acknowledges Gorlois' claims about Irish raids, and all is forgiven. They march their armies northward together to meet the Saxons.
- **Lindsey Skirmishing:** Uther's combined army skirmishes with Saxon raiding parties in Roestoc, keeping them from entering Linden Pool. As winter falls, the northern Saxons abandon the Eburacum siege.

**Raided:** Roestoc by Saxons; Pillaged: Malahaut by Saxons.

**If the player does nothing:** The Cornwall crisis is averted by Merlin. The Saxons withdraw from Eburacum for now. The Centurion King becomes an enemy.

## 490 — Battle of Linden Pool; Ygraine Crisis Begins

**Situation:** The biggest British victory of the period is followed immediately by the biggest political crisis: Uther tries to take Ygraine, Gorlois leaves in fury, and everything begins to unravel.

**Court:** Easter Court at Warcastle, Wuerensis.

**Events:**
- **Battle of Linden Pool:** Octa and Eosa bypass Malahaut, march south boasting they will claim Excalibur for Eosa (to match Octa's magical axe). Near Linden Pool, a battle is fought. Decisive Victory for the British. Both Octa and Eosa are captured and sent to Londinium in chains, along with many Saxon hostages. Sir Bellias (Captain of the Guard) is killed in this battle.
- **The Great Victory Feast:** Uther first met Ygraine here (in 488 or at this feast — sources suggest 490 is the key year). He is utterly smitten and can barely conceal his desire.
- **Prisoner of Courtesy:** Uther orders all nobles to bring their wives and remain through winter. His lust overcomes him and he attempts to take Duchess Ygraine. Duke Gorlois quits the court without the king's leave, taking his wife and men away through winter snow.
- **Legal Pretext:** Departing without leave violates the king's hospitality — this gives Uther cause to pursue Gorlois as a traitor. Uther pursues the fleeing duke but cannot overtake him.
- **Saxons raid:** Lonazep, Lambor, Bedegraine (raid), Roestoc, Linden (raid). Nohaut and Deira are pillaged by Prince Madoc and the northern kings.

**If the player does nothing:** Gorlois flees to Cornwall. Uther is furious. The Supreme Collegium vote never happens. War against Cornwall is coming.

## 491 — The Battle of Terrabil; Arthur's Conception; Madoc Dies

**Situation:** The most pivotal year. Uther declares Gorlois a rebel, besieges Cornwall, makes a devil's bargain with Merlin, begets Arthur through deception, and loses his son and heir Madoc in the same night.

**Court:** Easter Court at Londinium Civitas, Thamesmouth. Uther declares Gorlois a rebel for violating the king's hospitality.

**Events:**
- **War against Cornwall declared:** Only Uther's inner circle knows his near-madness over Ygraine. He ignores the Supreme Collegium vote and rushes off with his army.
- **The Battle of Terrabil:** Uther besieges Tintagel Castle (where Ygraine and the treasury are) and Castle Terrabil (guarding the road to Tintagel). The countryside is ravaged but neither castle falls. Uther turns to Merlin.
- **Merlin's Price:** Merlin agrees to help obtain Ygraine — but Uther must promise any child born of the union to Merlin. Uther rashly agrees.
- **The Deception:** Merlin instructs Uther to withdraw from Tintagel as if defeated, then magically convinces Gorlois to sortie against the army at Castle Terrabil. Merlin conjures a cloaking mist and changes Uther's appearance to that of Gorlois.
- **Arthur Conceived:** Uther enters Tintagel disguised as Gorlois and lies with Ygraine. Arthur is conceived. Duke Gorlois is killed during the nighttime sortie at Castle Terrabil.
- **Prince Madoc also killed** in the fighting at Terrabil. The later surrender of Tintagel by Ygraine (when told of her husband's death) is described as Ygraine surrendering "when her slain lord is returned on his shield."
- **Royal Funeral:** Madoc is buried at the Giant's Dance (Stonehenge) next to Aurelius Ambrosius.
- **Supreme Collegium Collapse:** The Centurion King of Malahaut, learning of Uther's behavior, convinces the Collegium representatives to depart hastily to their own lands. The election is dead.
- **King Ælle of Suth Seaxe** and his son Cissa besiege Anderida (a port town). After starving residents for weeks, the town surrenders. Ælle sacrifices all inhabitants to Wotan. Uther, still in Cornwall, cannot intervene.
- **Uther's Wedding:** Uther hastily marries Ygraine — only months after her husband's death. She grieves more than celebrates. Speculation focuses on the suspicious timing.
- **Sir Brastias** welcomed into Uther's service; elevated to Captain of the Guard.

**Player Adventure:** "Helping Merlin" — Merlin takes Uther's newborn son and tricks the player-knights into thwarting pursuers who turn out to be Uther's own men trying to recover the "kidnapped" child.

**Christmas Court:** Tintagel Castle, Tintagel. Uther plots to punish Malahaut. He offers Margawse to King Lot of Lothian, Elaine to King Nentres of Garloth, and betroths Morgan to King Uriens of Gorre (once Morgan grows up). In return, these three kings promise their Supreme Collegium votes and military support against Malahaut.

**Raided:** Hantonne by King Ælle's army.

**If the player does nothing:** Gorlois is dead. Arthur is born. Madoc is dead. Uther married Ygraine. The Supreme Collegium vote is ruined. Anderida's population is massacred. The northern kings are now allies of Uther but at the cost of three future enemies' connections (Morgawse, Elaine, Morgan).

## 492 — Arthur Born; Treason Trial; Pellinore Vanishes

**Situation:** Arthur is born and immediately given to Merlin. The player-knights face a treason trial. Merlin is declared an outlaw. Foreign alliances are built for the coming Malahaut war.

**Court:** Easter Court at Tintagel Castle, Tintagel. Double wedding: Margawse to Lot; Elaine to Nentres.

**Events:**
- **Foreign Alliances:** Uther obtains support from King Nanteleod of Escavalon and King Leodegrance of Cameliard for the attack on Malahaut.†
- **Arthur Born:** Arthur, heir to Logres and future High King, is born at Tintagel. Queen Ygraine seems to overcome her sorrow. Arthur is immediately taken by Merlin (per the agreement). Ygraine is told nothing definitive.
- **Robert Born:** Son and heir of Count Roderick of Salisbury is born at Castle of the Rock, Sarum.
- **Adventure — Questing Beast:** Player-knights encounter the Glatisant (Questing Beast) and its pursuer, King Pellinore, who informs them he gave up the throne of Norgales to hunt it unceasingly.
- **Adventure — Helping Merlin:** Merlin takes Arthur (seemingly kidnapping him from Uther's perspective). Player-knights may be tricked into helping Merlin escape Uther's men.
- **Treason Trial:** Player-knights who helped Merlin are put on trial at the King's Bench for treason. Merlin is declared a traitor and outlaw and sentenced to death in absentia. Count Roderick endangers his own standing by sending Sir Elad and Father Dewi from Salisbury to mount a defense. If player-knights are released, they must tread carefully to avoid reigniting the king's ire.
- **Sir Lamorak** arrives at court seeking his father Pellinore; Norgales is in chaos; Pellinore's brothers are killing each other and fighting the Three Cadwys.

**Raided:** Norgales (due to civil war there).

**If the player does nothing:** Arthur is in Merlin's hands. Merlin is an outlaw. The player-knights may or may not be under royal ire. The Malahaut war is planned for the following year.

## 493 — The Argan Scandal; King Uther Falls Ill; Octa and Eosa Escape

**Situation:** The most politically damaging year for Uther. He is humiliated in single combat, falls mysteriously ill, and the Saxons escape from prison. Everything begins falling apart.

**Court:** Easter Court at Silchester Castle, Silchester.

**Events:**
- **Gawaine Born:** In Lothian, King Lot and Margawse celebrate the birth of their son Gawaine.
- **The Argan Scandal:** Sir Argan ap Maelgad, Royal Constable, is brought to court for killing his wife Dyagenne in a fit of passion. Argan declares openly that Uther cuckolded him. He challenges Uther to trial by combat — Uther accepts, refusing his Champion's offer to fight in his stead. God delivers Justice: Argan wounds Uther and disarms the king, then threatens to slay him unless justice is granted. Uther ransoms his life for the price of a strong castle. Argan builds it and names it **Uther's Shame** — an enduring monument to the king's lust and betrayal.†
- **King Uther Falls Ill:** After the Argan scandal, Uther withdraws from court. Theories: distress at losing both sons; shame at his defeat; festering wound; Merlin's curse.
- **Octa and Eosa Escape:** The two Saxon kings escape from their prison in Londinium. Treachery is suspected. They return to Nohaut and Deira and recruit new troops throughout summer and winter.
- **Envoys to Malahaut:** Uther sends Count Roderick north to the Centurion King with an ultimatum: pay homage or suffer Cornwall's fate. The negotiations fail badly. Roderick discovers the Centurion King is allying with the Saxons. Roderick heads home empty-handed.
- **Saxon Ambush:** Count Roderick is ambushed by Saxons on his return from Malahaut. He survives.

**Raided:** Rheged by Saxons.

**If the player does nothing:** Uther is humiliated and ill. Octa and Eosa are free and raising armies. Malahaut is allied with the Saxons. Logres is weakening.

## 494 — Linden Pool Falls; Lady Nineve; Saxons Winter Inside Logres

**Situation:** The worst year since the realm's founding. The Saxons, with Malahaut as allies, smash through the north and sack Linden Pool. For the first time, they winter inside Logres.

**Court:** Easter Court at the White Tower, Londinium, Thamesmouth.

**Events:**
- **Lady Nineve appears:** The king's new magical advisor tends to Uther and keeps news of the Saxon advance from reaching him.*
- **Embassy to Estregales:** Uther sends envoys seeking alliance with King Canan of Estregales. Canan is assassinated during the mission. Estregales descends into tribal warfare. The alliance is dead.
- **Octa and Eosa Attack:** Saxon kings move south through Malahaut unopposed (Centurion King actively allies with them). They enter Linden so quickly that Duke Corneus is caught off-guard; his forces withdraw into their strongholds. The Saxons scale the walls and sack Linden Pool. Corneus disappears (presumed dead). The duchy's castles surrender. **The Saxon army winters in Linden Pool** — the first time Saxons have successfully wintered inside Logres proper.*

**Raided:** Bedegraine, Lambor, Lonazep, Roestoc by Saxons. **Plundered:** Linden by Saxons.

**If the player does nothing:** Linden Pool is in Saxon hands. Duke Corneus is probably dead. A major chunk of central Logres is under enemy occupation. Spring will bring Uther's last battle.

## 495 — Battle of St Albans; The Infamous Feast

**Situation:** Uther's last year. He assembles a sick man's army and wins one final victory — then he and most of Logres' nobility die at the victory feast. The Anarchy begins.

**Court:** Easter Court at Corinium Castle, Clarence.

**Events:**
- **Uther's illness shows no improvement.** The Queen is seldom seen. Merlin remains in hiding.
- **The Saxons march early**, pillaging everything southward toward Londinium. Malahaut troops march with them.
- **Uther assembles his army** at Castle of the Rock. Though visibly ill, he personally leads the march.*
- **The Battle of St Albans:** The Saxons take St Albans before Uther arrives. They set a trap. A mad assault on the walls fails. Uther's army withdraws but holds. Next morning, the Saxons choose to fight in the field, counting on their Malahaut allies to cancel Logres' knights. The gamble fails — Marshal Ulfius and Count Roderick smash Malahaut's force and turn the Saxon flank. The Saxons are routed with great losses. Both Duke Ulfius and Sir Brastias are gravely wounded — they are NOT at the celebratory feast that follows.*
- **The Infamous Feast:** The victory feast is shattered when King Uther and most of the nobility of Logres are poisoned. The treacherous deed is blamed on a Saxon physician who is immediately executed — but others are not so sure of his guilt.
- **Funerals:** Uther is buried at Stonehenge between Madoc and Aurelius Ambrosius. Count Roderick is buried at Sarum Abbey. Many grand funerals across Logres.
- **Aftermath:** "There are no lords in Logres" becomes a common adage. Queen Ygraine retires to Ambrius Abbey, taking Morgan. In Salisbury, Countess Ellen is despondent; no officers have survived. Surviving household officers attempt to maintain order but fail without a king or heir. An attempt to assemble the Supreme Collegium also fails. Royal knights are unattached until they find new liege lords.
- **Sir Brastias** is especially sought after; he spends the rest of the year traveling among lords, entertaining offers from all, accepting none.
- **The Sword in the Stone** appears in the churchyard of St Paul's Cathedral in Londinium during Christmas Mass.
- **The Savage Forest** suddenly appears around Castle Dykes and begins its rapid expansion.

**Who Died at the Feast (confirmed):** King Uther, Count Roderick of Salisbury, Sir Goreau (Seneschal), Sir Sadinal (Dapifer), Abner (Cofferer), Sir Segurant the Brown (Famous Hero). Sir Geraint (Seneschal) also d. 495 Infamous Feast.

**Who Survived:** Sir Ulfius (gravely wounded, at surgeon's care), Sir Brastias (gravely wounded, at surgeon's care), Countess Ellen (not at feast), Queen Ygraine (likely not at feast or survived).

**Raided:** Linden Pool and Lonazep by Saxons. **Pillaged:** Huntland. **Plundered:** Hartland.

**If the player does nothing:** Uther is dead. The Anarchy Period of the Great Pendragon Campaign begins. Player-knights must find new liege lords or serve the leaderless Salisbury.
